**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter references to **PM evidence codes**. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, that PM represents a family of standardized **moderate pathogenic evidence codes**, each describing a different type of scientific evidence used during professional variant interpretation.
