# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Explain what lung metastasis means.
- Understand the lungs in simple, patient-friendly language.
- Understand that gastric cancer can spread to the lungs.
- Recognize that lung metastasis is still gastric cancer, not primary lung cancer.
- Understand that not every patient with gastric cancer develops lung metastasis.
- Understand that doctors use appropriate medical evaluation to determine whether lung metastasis is present.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Are the Lungs?

## Patient Explanation

The **lungs** are the main organs of the respiratory system.

They help your body take in oxygen from the air and remove carbon dioxide when you breathe out.

---

## Clinical Importance

The lungs are essential organs and may become involved if gastric cancer spreads.

---

## Key Concepts

- The lungs are responsible for breathing.
- They exchange oxygen and carbon dioxide.
- They are separate organs from the stomach.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Is Lung Metastasis?

## Patient Explanation

**Lung metastasis** means that stomach cancer has spread from the stomach to the lungs.

This is one form of metastatic gastric cancer.

---

## Clinical Importance

Lung metastasis represents distant spread of gastric cancer to the lungs.

---

## Key Concepts

- Gastric cancer can spread to the lungs.
- Lung metastasis is one type of metastatic disease.
- It develops from the original stomach cancer.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Is Lung Metastasis the Same as Lung Cancer?

## Patient Explanation

No.

If stomach cancer spreads to the lungs, it is still called **gastric cancer with lung metastasis**.

It is **not** the same as a cancer that starts in the lungs.

---

## Clinical Importance

Metastatic tumors retain the biological identity of the original cancer.

---

## Key Concepts

- Lung metastasis differs from primary lung cancer.
- The original cancer remains gastric cancer.
- Treatment decisions are based on the primary cancer.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does Every Patient Develop Lung Metastasis?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Many patients with gastric cancer never develop lung metastasis.

Whether cancer spreads to the lungs depends on the characteristics of each person's disease.

---

## Clinical Importance

Patterns of metastatic spread differ between patients.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not every patient develops lung metastasis.
- Disease spread varies among individuals.
- Medical evaluation is required.

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — How Do Doctors Determine Whether Lung Metastasis Is Present?

## Patient Explanation

Doctors do not determine lung metastasis based only on symptoms.

They combine information from:

- imaging studies;
- pathology findings;
- physical examination;
- surgery (when appropriate);
- other clinical information.

Only after reviewing all available information can doctors determine whether lung metastasis is present.

---

## Clinical Importance

Diagnosis requires comprehensive clinical assessment using appropriate investigations.

---

## Key Concepts

- Diagnosis requires medical evaluation.
- Multiple sources of information are considered.
- Symptoms alone are not enough.

---

# Knowledge Block 6 — Should I Compare My Situation with Someone Else's?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Every person's gastric cancer is different.

Even if two patients both have lung metastasis, they may have different disease characteristics, treatment options and overall clinical situations.

---

## Clinical Importance

Management of metastatic gastric cancer is individualized.

---

## Common Misconception

**Myth**

Everyone with lung metastasis has the same disease and the same treatment.

**Fact**

Lung metastasis varies greatly between patients. Doctors evaluate the complete clinical picture before discussing treatment or prognosis.

---

# Key Messages

- The lungs are essential organs for breathing.
- Lung metastasis means gastric cancer has spread to the lungs.
- Lung metastasis is still gastric cancer, not primary lung cancer.
- Not every patient with gastric cancer develops lung metastasis.
- Doctors diagnose lung metastasis using appropriate medical evaluation.
