The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

- definition of lung metastasis;

- importance of comprehensive medical evaluation.

Excluded:

- diagnostic imaging;
- bronchoscopy;
- lung biopsy;
- pulmonary metastasectomy;
- radiation therapy;
- systemic treatment;
- prognosis.
