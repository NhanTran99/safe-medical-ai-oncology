# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Understand what ACMG Evidence Codes are.
- Understand why genetics professionals use evidence codes.
- Recognize that evidence codes help organize scientific evidence rather than provide a diagnosis.
- Understand that multiple types of evidence are considered together during variant interpretation.
- Recognize that evidence codes are tools for professionals rather than information patients need to memorize.
- Feel more confident understanding how genetic variant classifications are developed.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Are ACMG Evidence Codes?

## Patient Explanation

**ACMG Evidence Codes** are standardized labels used by genetics professionals to organize different kinds of scientific evidence when evaluating a genetic variant.

These codes help experts assess the available evidence in a consistent and structured way.

---

## Clinical Importance

Evidence codes support standardized genomic interpretation across laboratories.

---

## Key Concepts

- Evidence codes
- Scientific evidence
- Standardization

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — Why Are Evidence Codes Needed?

## Patient Explanation

Different types of scientific evidence may provide different pieces of information about a genetic variant.

Evidence codes provide a common language that allows experts to describe and combine these different sources of evidence consistently.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence documentation improves consistency and transparency in variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Standardized language
- Scientific consistency
- Evidence evaluation

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Do Evidence Codes Determine the Final Classification?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Evidence codes help organize scientific information, but professionals evaluate all relevant evidence together before assigning a final classification.

No single evidence code usually determines the final result by itself.

---

## Clinical Importance

Variant classification is based on the overall body of available evidence rather than a single observation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Expert interpretation
- Variant classification

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Do Patients Need to Understand Evidence Codes?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Most patients will never need to learn individual evidence codes.

What is most important is understanding that your genetic test result is based on a careful review of many different types of scientific evidence.

---

## Clinical Importance

Evidence codes are professional tools used to support high-quality genomic interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Professional tools
- Patient education
- Clinical genomics

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Do Evidence Codes Matter?

## Patient Explanation

Using standardized evidence codes helps laboratories produce more consistent genetic reports.

This makes it easier for healthcare professionals to communicate results and explain them to patients.

---

## Clinical Importance

Evidence codes improve consistency, transparency, and reproducibility of genomic reporting.

---

## Key Concepts

- Transparency
- Reproducibility
- Laboratory standards

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Evidence codes are the same as my genetic test result.

**Fact**

Evidence codes are professional tools used to evaluate scientific evidence. The final classification of a genetic variant is based on the combined assessment of multiple evidence sources.

---

# Key Messages

- ACMG Evidence Codes organize scientific evidence.
- They support standardized variant interpretation.
- Multiple evidence types are evaluated together.
- Evidence codes are intended for healthcare professionals and laboratories.
- Patients do not need to memorize individual evidence codes.
