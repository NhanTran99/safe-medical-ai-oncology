**Reason:**

Ovarian metastasis is an important metastatic pattern in female patients with gastric cancer. Patients and caregivers may encounter the term **Krukenberg tumor** in pathology reports or clinical discussions. This Population Package establishes the conceptual foundation before introducing pathology, immunohistochemistry, surgery or treatment.

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

Although ovarian metastasis occurs only in patients with ovaries, the term **Krukenberg tumor** is well established in oncology and pathology. This Population Package provides prerequisite knowledge for future Population Packages covering ovarian imaging, pathology, immunohistochemistry and surgical management.

# Clinical Scope

## Included

- Definition of the ovaries
- Definition of ovarian metastasis
- General explanation of Krukenberg tumor
- Relationship between gastric cancer and the ovaries
- Difference between ovarian metastasis and primary ovarian cancer
- Importance of determining the primary site of an ovarian tumor
- Importance of appropriate medical evaluation

---

## Explicitly Excluded

- Primary ovarian cancer
- FIGO staging
- Signet-ring cell pathology
- Immunohistochemistry
- Ovarian ultrasound
- Pelvic MRI
- PET-CT
- Oophorectomy
- Cytoreductive surgery
- HIPEC
- Systemic therapy
- Prognostic statistics

These topics are intentionally delegated to independent Population Packages.
