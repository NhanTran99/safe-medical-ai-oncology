# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is an ovarian metastasis (Krukenberg tumor)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **ovarian metastasis (Krukenberg tumor)**, including what the ovaries are, how gastric cancer can spread to the ovaries, why ovarian metastasis remains gastric cancer rather than primary ovarian cancer, what the medical term **Krukenberg tumor** generally refers to, and why appropriate medical evaluation is required to determine where an ovarian tumor originated.

This Population Package introduces the concept of ovarian metastasis without discussing pathology, immunohistochemistry, imaging studies, surgery, systemic therapy or prognosis.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- The ovaries are normal organs of the female reproductive system.
- Gastric cancer can sometimes spread to the ovaries.
- Ovarian metastasis means gastric cancer has spread to one or both ovaries.
- Doctors may use the term **Krukenberg tumor** for certain metastatic ovarian tumors, especially those originating from gastric or other gastrointestinal cancers.
- Ovarian metastasis remains gastric cancer because it originated in the stomach.
- Doctors determine whether an ovarian tumor is primary or metastatic through comprehensive medical evaluation.
