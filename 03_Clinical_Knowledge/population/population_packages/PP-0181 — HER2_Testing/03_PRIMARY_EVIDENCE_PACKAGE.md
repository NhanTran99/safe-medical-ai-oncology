# 2. Clinical Question

> **How should HER2 be tested and interpreted in gastric adenocarcinoma, when is testing relevant, what do IHC and ISH/FISH results mean, and how does HER2 status relate to treatment selection?**

---

# 3. Educational Intent

The package should allow a patient to move from:

**“Why am I being tested for HER2?”**

to:

**“What does my HER2 result mean?”**

without crossing into individualized treatment prescribing.

The educational sequence is:

1. why HER2 matters;
2. when testing is relevant;
3. what specimen is used;
4. how IHC works conceptually;
5. what 0/1+/2+/3+ mean;
6. why 2+ needs clarification;
7. what ISH/FISH means;
8. what HER2-positive/negative means;
9. limitations and heterogeneity;
10. treatment-selection relevance.

# 8. Evidence Notes

## Evidence Note 1 — HER2 Testing Timing

NCCN v2.2026 recommends HER2 testing at diagnosis when advanced/metastatic disease is documented or suspected.

This should be preserved without overgeneralizing to an unconditional statement that every gastric-cancer patient must always undergo the same testing at every point.

---

## Evidence Note 2 — IHC-First Strategy

NCCN identifies IHC as the initial HER2 assessment and recommends ISH methods when IHC is 2+.

This creates the central testing algorithm.

---

## Evidence Note 3 — Gastric-Specific Scoring

The gastric scoring system is not simply a generic HER2 scoring scale.

The NCCN Table 3 separately describes:

- surgical specimen;
- biopsy specimen.

This distinction must be retained.

---

## Evidence Note 4 — IHC 2+

IHC 2+ is explicitly equivocal.

It should not be converted into a positive result without the relevant ISH/FISH assessment.

---

## Evidence Note 5 — ISH/FISH

The guideline identifies positive criteria based on ERBB2:CEP17 ratio and average ERBB2 copy number.

The numerical criteria are retained because they are clinically meaningful interpretation criteria.

Detailed laboratory counting methodology is excluded.

---

## Evidence Note 6 — Prognosis

NCCN describes conflicting evidence regarding the prognostic significance of HER2 in gastric cancer.

Therefore the package should not teach HER2-positive status as a deterministic prognostic label.

---

## Evidence Note 7 — NGS

NCCN describes IHC/ISH/targeted PCR as preferred for initial biomarker assessment and validated NGS as a possible later approach.

The package should therefore avoid saying NGS universally replaces HER2-specific testing.

---

## Evidence Note 8 — Repeat Testing

NCCN states repeat biomarker testing may be considered at clinical or radiologic progression.

This is a context-dependent recommendation, not a universal retesting rule.

# 9. Clinical Claims Summary

### Claim A

HER2 testing is clinically relevant because HER2-positive gastric cancer may be eligible for HER2-directed treatment.

### Claim B

IHC is the primary initial HER2 assessment.

### Claim C

Gastric HER2 scoring uses a specific 0–3+ framework.

### Claim D

0 and 1+ are negative.

### Claim E

2+ is equivocal.

### Claim F

3+ is positive.

### Claim G

2+ should undergo ISH/FISH clarification.

### Claim H

ISH/FISH evaluates ERBB2 amplification.

### Claim I

A HER2 result must be interpreted in the clinical and specimen context.

### Claim J

HER2 status does not independently determine the entire treatment plan.

# 11. Evidence Gaps

## Gap 1 — Prognostic meaning

Evidence is not sufficiently consistent to define HER2 positivity as an independent prognostic marker.

### Handling

Retain uncertainty.

---

## Gap 2 — Longitudinal HER2 change

Repeat testing is permitted/considered in selected progression settings, but the package should not imply universal biological conversion.

### Handling

Use cautious language.

---

## Gap 3 — Specimen concordance

Differences between biopsy and resection can occur, but the package does not establish a universal superiority rule for one specimen type.

### Handling

Explain heterogeneity without overclaiming.

---

## Gap 4 — NGS versus IHC/ISH

NGS can assess multiple molecular events, but the project sources do not support universal replacement of established HER2-specific testing.

### Handling

Keep NGS contextual.
