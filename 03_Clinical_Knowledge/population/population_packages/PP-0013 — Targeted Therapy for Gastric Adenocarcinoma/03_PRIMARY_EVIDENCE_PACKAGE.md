# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is targeted therapy and what is its role in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based introduction to targeted therapy as a precision medicine approach for gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package establishes the foundational knowledge required before introducing biomarker testing, biomarker-specific therapies, companion diagnostics and individual targeted medicines.

---

# Scope

## Included

- Definition of targeted therapy
- Difference between targeted therapy and chemotherapy
- Importance of biomarker testing
- General role of targeted therapy
- Common biomarker examples (overview only)
- Clinical situations where targeted therapy may be used
- Expected benefits
- General limitations
- Common side effects (overview)
- General patient expectations

---

## Excluded

- Drug-specific therapies
- Trastuzumab
- Ramucirumab
- Zolbetuximab
- Individual biomarkers in detail
- Biomarker testing methodology
- Companion diagnostics
- Drug dosing
- Treatment-line recommendations
- Combination regimens
- Resistance mechanisms
- Toxicity management

These topics require dedicated Population Packages.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Targeted therapy treats selected cancers with specific biological characteristics.
- Biomarker testing is essential before many targeted therapies can be used.
- Targeted therapy is one component of precision oncology.
- Treatment eligibility depends on the biology of the tumor.
- Targeted therapy differs from chemotherapy in both mechanism and patient selection.
- Different targeted medicines have different side-effect profiles.
- Treatment decisions should always be individualized.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary authoritative references.

The major references consistently describe targeted therapy as a biomarker-directed treatment strategy within multidisciplinary gastric cancer management.

Differences among references primarily relate to:

- specific drugs discussed;
- evolving treatment indications;
- biomarker availability;
- educational wording.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- treatment principles from NCCN;
- evidence interpretation from JNCCN;
- patient-oriented explanations from NCI and ACS;
- internationally harmonized terminology from ESMO.
