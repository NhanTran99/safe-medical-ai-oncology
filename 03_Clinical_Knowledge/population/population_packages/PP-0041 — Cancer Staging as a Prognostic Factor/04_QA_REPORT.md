# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of cancer stage;
- why stage is one of the most important prognostic factors;
- relationship between stage and treatment planning;
- relationship between stage and prognosis;
- explanation that prognosis varies among patients with the same stage;
- explanation that stage should always be interpreted together with other clinical information.

Excluded:

- TNM staging methodology;
- T, N and M categories;
- AJCC Stage Groups;
- stage-specific survival statistics;
- stage-specific treatment recommendations;
- clinical versus pathological staging;
- restaging.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
