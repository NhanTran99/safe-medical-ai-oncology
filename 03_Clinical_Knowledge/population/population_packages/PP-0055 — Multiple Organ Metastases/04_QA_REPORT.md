# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of multiple organ metastases;
- explanation that metastatic patterns vary between patients;
- relationship between multiple organ metastases and metastatic gastric cancer;
- explanation that the number of involved organs is only one aspect of clinical assessment;
- importance of individualized medical evaluation.

Excluded:

- oligometastatic disease;
- tumor burden;
- RECIST;
- treatment;
- palliative care;
- prognosis.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational concept of **multiple organ metastases** while reinforcing that metastatic gastric cancer is highly individualized and should never be interpreted solely by counting the number of involved organs.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- Oligometastatic Disease
- Tumor Burden
- Number of Metastatic Lesions
- RECIST
- Monitoring Metastatic Disease
- Treatment of Metastatic Gastric Cancer
- Prognosis of Metastatic Gastric Cancer
- Palliative Care

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
