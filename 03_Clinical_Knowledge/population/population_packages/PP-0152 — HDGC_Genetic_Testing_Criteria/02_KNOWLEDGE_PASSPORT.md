# Primary Runtime Roles

- Determine whether personal/family history meets HDGC testing criteria
- Explain exact criteria in patient-facing language
- Clarify relevant histology
- Clarify age thresholds
- Explain family relationship requirements
- Encourage verification of cancer diagnoses
- Explain affected-relative-first testing
- Explain CDH1 → CTNNA1 testing pathway
- Prevent confusion between testing eligibility and diagnosis
- Explain the possibility of an HDGC-like state after negative testing
