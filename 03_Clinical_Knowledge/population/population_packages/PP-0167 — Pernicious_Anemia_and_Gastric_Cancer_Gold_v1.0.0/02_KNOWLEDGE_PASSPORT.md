## Evidence Classification
### Established
Pernicious anemia is associated with increased gastric-cancer risk; it is a recognized higher-risk condition; older people with pernicious anemia may be considered for gastric-cancer screening/upper endoscopy.

### Context-dependent
The precise individual magnitude of risk and optimal screening strategy.

### Not established in this package
Universal screening interval, individual absolute-risk calculation, detailed autoimmune-gastritis diagnostic algorithm.
