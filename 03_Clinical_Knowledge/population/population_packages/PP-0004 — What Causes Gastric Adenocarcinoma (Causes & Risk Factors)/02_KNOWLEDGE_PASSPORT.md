# Educational Intent

Primary Goal

Help patients understand why gastric adenocarcinoma develops.

Secondary Goals

- Distinguish causes from risk factors.
- Reduce misconceptions (for example, "I ate one unhealthy meal so I got cancer").
- Improve understanding of modifiable versus non-modifiable risks.
- Prepare patients for future Population Packages on prevention and screening.

# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. Gastric adenocarcinoma usually develops through a multifactorial process.
2. Most patients do not have a single identifiable cause.
3. A risk factor increases probability but does not guarantee disease.
4. Some risk factors are modifiable.
5. Some risk factors cannot be changed.
6. Helicobacter pylori is one of the strongest established risk factors for non-cardia gastric cancer.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Having one or more risk factors does not mean a person will definitely develop gastric cancer.
- Many patients with gastric cancer have no clearly identifiable cause.
- Patients should not blame themselves for developing cancer.
- Risk information should support informed prevention, not create guilt or fear.
