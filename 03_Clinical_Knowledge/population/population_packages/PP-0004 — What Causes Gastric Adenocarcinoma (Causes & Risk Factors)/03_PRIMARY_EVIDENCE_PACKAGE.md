# Evidence Extraction Summary

## Definition

No single cause.

Multifactorial disease.

---

## Strongly Established Risk Factors

- Helicobacter pylori infection
- Increasing age
- Male sex
- Smoking
- High-salt diet
- Smoked / preserved food
- Chronic atrophic gastritis
- Intestinal metaplasia
- Pernicious anemia
- Family history
- Hereditary cancer syndromes

---

## Additional Factors

- Epstein–Barr virus
- Obesity (certain gastric locations)
- GERD (cardia cancers)
- Occupational exposure
- Radiation exposure

---

## Important Patient Messages

Risk factor

≠

Disease.

No risk factor

≠

No disease.

Most cancers result from multiple interacting factors.

Patients should not blame themselves.
