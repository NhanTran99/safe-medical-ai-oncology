**Reason:**

Patients may wonder why genetics professionals consider previous reports from scientific organizations or databases when interpreting a genetic variant. This Population Package explains how **reputable published sources** may contribute **supporting pathogenic evidence** while emphasizing that such reports alone never determine the final classification.
