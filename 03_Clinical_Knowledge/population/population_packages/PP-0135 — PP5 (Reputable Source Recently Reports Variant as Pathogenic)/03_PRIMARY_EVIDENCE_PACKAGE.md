# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PP5 (Reputable Source Recently Reports Variant as Pathogenic)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PP5 (Reputable Source Recently Reports Variant as Pathogenic)** as one of the standardized **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **PP5 provides supporting evidence when a reputable scientific source has previously reported that a genetic variant is pathogenic.** Information from trusted expert organizations, databases, or laboratories may contribute valuable scientific knowledge during variant interpretation. However, **PP5 represents supporting evidence only** and must always be interpreted together with clinical findings, laboratory evidence, family information, and other ACMG evidence categories. Modern professional recommendations have refined the practical application of PP5, but those implementation details intentionally remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PP5 is one individual ACMG evidence criterion.
- PP5 incorporates information from reputable scientific sources.
- Published expert reports may provide supporting evidence.
- PP5 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PP5** as one of the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance explains that previously published pathogenic classifications from reputable sources may contribute supporting evidence during variant interpretation. ClinGen SVI has subsequently provided updated recommendations regarding the practical use of PP5, but these implementation details intentionally remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
