# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **PP5**, the final individual **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criterion within the ACMG/AMP framework. It explains, in patient-friendly language, that **reports from reputable scientific sources identifying a variant as pathogenic may provide supporting evidence during variant interpretation**, while emphasizing that **PP5 alone never establishes pathogenicity**. ClinVar workflows, expert panel review processes, ClinGen implementation recommendations, database governance, Bayesian implementation, and ACMG evidence-combination rules are intentionally deferred to dedicated Population Packages, maintaining strict adherence to the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
