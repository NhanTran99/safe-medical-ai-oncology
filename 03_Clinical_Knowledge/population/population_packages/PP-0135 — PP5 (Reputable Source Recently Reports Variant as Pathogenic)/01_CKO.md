# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PP5?

## Patient Explanation

**PP5** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

PP5 represents one type of **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence**.

It considers whether a **reputable source** has previously reported that a variant is pathogenic.

---

## Clinical Importance

PP5 provides supporting evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PP5
- Supporting pathogenic evidence
- ACMG Evidence Codes

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Is a Reputable Source?

## Patient Explanation

A **reputable source** is an organization, database, or laboratory that follows recognized scientific and professional standards when evaluating genetic variants.

These organizations carefully review available evidence before publishing their conclusions.

---

## Clinical Importance

Reliable scientific sources contribute valuable information to variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Reputable source
- Scientific review
- Expert evaluation

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Is PP5 Important?

## Patient Explanation

If a trusted scientific source has already reported that a genetic variant is disease-causing, this information may provide **supporting evidence** during variant interpretation.

However, genetics professionals still review the available evidence themselves rather than relying on one report alone.

---

## Clinical Importance

Published expert knowledge contributes to evidence-based genomic medicine.

---

## Key Concepts

- Published evidence
- Supporting evidence
- Variant interpretation

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PP5 Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PP5 **does not prove** that a variant causes disease.

Reports from reputable sources provide only one part of the overall evidence.

The final classification always considers many different types of scientific and clinical information.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on integrating multiple evidence categories.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Professional interpretation
- Supporting evidence

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use PP5?

## Patient Explanation

Medical genetics builds on knowledge collected by scientists and healthcare professionals around the world.

PP5 allows genetics professionals to consider trustworthy published information while still performing their own complete evidence-based evaluation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized use of published scientific evidence improves consistency in clinical genomics.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

If a trusted database says a variant is pathogenic, no further evaluation is needed.

**Fact**

Reports from reputable sources provide **supporting evidence**, but genetics professionals still evaluate all available clinical, laboratory, and genetic evidence before reaching a final classification.

---

# Key Messages

- PP5 is one Supporting Pathogenic Evidence criterion.
- PP5 considers reports from reputable scientific sources.
- Trusted reports may contribute supporting evidence.
- PP5 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires integration of multiple evidence categories.
