# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package extends the staging pathway beyond purely anatomical classification by introducing the concept of **prognostic stage**. It explains that some modern staging systems integrate validated biological and pathological factors in addition to TNM, while emphasizing that this approach is **cancer-specific** and does not replace anatomical staging for all malignancies. It provides the conceptual bridge toward future Population Packages on **Prognostic Factors**, **Risk Stratification**, and **Personalized Oncology**.
