# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is prognostic stage?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **prognostic stage** as a staging approach that may integrate anatomical stage with additional prognostic information for selected cancers.

This Population Package explains why prognostic staging was developed, how it differs conceptually from anatomical staging, and why it is applied only in certain cancer types.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Prognostic stage expands upon anatomical staging in selected cancers.
- TNM staging remains fundamental.
- Prognostic staging incorporates additional clinically important information where validated.
- Not every cancer uses prognostic staging.
- Prognostic stage contributes to individualized patient management.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

AJCC consistently describes prognostic staging as an evolution of anatomical staging for selected malignancies by incorporating validated prognostic factors beyond tumor extent. Major oncology organizations similarly emphasize that staging systems should be interpreted according to the specific cancer type and current evidence.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- staging principles from AJCC;
- educational principles from NCI and ACS;
- multidisciplinary oncology guidance consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.
