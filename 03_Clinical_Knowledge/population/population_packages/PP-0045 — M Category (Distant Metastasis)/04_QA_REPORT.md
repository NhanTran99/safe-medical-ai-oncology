# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of the M category;
- explanation of distant metastasis;
- relationship between M category and distant metastatic spread;
- general relationship between metastatic disease and disease extent;
- contribution of the M category to TNM staging, treatment planning and prognostic assessment;
- explanation that M category must always be interpreted together with T, N and other clinical information.

Excluded:

- M0–M1 classifications;
- specific metastatic sites;
- mechanisms of metastasis;
- imaging investigations;
- AJCC Stage Groups;
- stage-specific prognosis;
- treatment recommendations according to M category.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
