# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package begins the repository's **individual ACMG evidence-code series** by introducing **PVS1**, the highest-strength pathogenic evidence code within the ACMG/AMP framework. It establishes the important educational distinction that **evidence codes are components of variant interpretation rather than final classifications**, preventing a common misconception among patients reading detailed genomic reports. The package also introduces the concept of **loss-of-function evidence** at a non-technical level while appropriately deferring ClinGen algorithms and implementation details to future Population Packages.
