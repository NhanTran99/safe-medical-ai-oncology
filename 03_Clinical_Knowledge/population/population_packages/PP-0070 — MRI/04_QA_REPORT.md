# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational patient understanding of **MRI** within cancer care. It explains why MRI is used, clarifies that it **does not use ionizing radiation**, and emphasizes that MRI is selected according to the specific clinical question rather than replacing all other imaging modalities. It serves as the foundation for future Population Packages on **MRI contrast agents**, **MRI safety**, **MRI preparation**, **MRI report interpretation**, and **comparison of CT, MRI and PET/CT**.
