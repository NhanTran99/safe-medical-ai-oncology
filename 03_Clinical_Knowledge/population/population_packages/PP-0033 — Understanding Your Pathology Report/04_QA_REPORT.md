# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of a pathology report;
- role of the pathologist;
- relationship between biopsy and pathology;
- importance of microscopic tissue examination;
- integration of pathology with other clinical information;
- importance of physician explanation.

Excluded:

- pathology report interpretation;
- histologic classification;
- tumor grade;
- biomarker interpretation;
- molecular pathology;
- treatment planning.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
