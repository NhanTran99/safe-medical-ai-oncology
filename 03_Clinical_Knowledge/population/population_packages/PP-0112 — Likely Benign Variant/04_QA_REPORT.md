# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the repository's **fourth standardized ACMG variant classification node**. It complements **Likely Pathogenic Variant** by explaining the opposite side of the evidence continuum while reinforcing that the distinction between **Likely Benign** and **Benign** reflects the **strength of supporting scientific evidence**, not biological behavior or patient safety. Together with the preceding packages, it prepares users for the final classification category (**Benign Variant**) and the future package on **Variant Reclassification**, while maintaining strict compliance with the Atomic Knowledge Principle.
