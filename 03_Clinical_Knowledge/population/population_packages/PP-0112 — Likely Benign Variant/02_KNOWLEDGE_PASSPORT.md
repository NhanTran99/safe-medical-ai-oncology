**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic reports may encounter the term **Likely Benign Variant**. This Population Package explains that the classification reflects strong evidence supporting a non-disease-causing role while avoiding the misconception that "likely" refers to uncertainty about patient safety.

---

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

Likely Benign Variant is one of the five standardized ACMG variant classification categories and provides the conceptual bridge between **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)** and **Benign Variant**, completing the educational progression toward non-disease-causing classifications.
