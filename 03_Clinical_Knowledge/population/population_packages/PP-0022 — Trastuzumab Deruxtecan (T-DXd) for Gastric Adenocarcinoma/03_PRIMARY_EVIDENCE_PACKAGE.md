# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) and why is it used in the treatment of HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an evidence-based understanding of trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) as an advanced HER2-targeted therapy for appropriate patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package explains what T-DXd is, how it differs from trastuzumab at a general level, why HER2 testing is required, its role in modern gastric cancer care, and the overall benefits and limitations of treatment.

It serves as the prerequisite before introducing antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs), DESTINY-Gastric clinical trials, infusion procedures, ILD/pneumonitis monitoring and toxicity management.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- T-DXd is an advanced HER2-targeted treatment for appropriate patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.
- HER2 testing is essential before T-DXd treatment.
- T-DXd is different from trastuzumab, although both target HER2.
- Treatment recommendations require consideration of biomarker status together with the overall clinical picture.
- Patients should understand both expected benefits and potential limitations before treatment.
- Shared decision-making is an essential part of T-DXd treatment planning.
