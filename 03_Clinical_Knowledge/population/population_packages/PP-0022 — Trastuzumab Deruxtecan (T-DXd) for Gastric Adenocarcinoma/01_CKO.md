# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is Trastuzumab Deruxtecan?

## Patient Explanation

Trastuzumab deruxtecan, often abbreviated as **T-DXd**, is a HER2-targeted cancer medicine used for certain patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.

It belongs to a newer generation of targeted therapies designed to deliver treatment directly to HER2-positive cancer cells.

---

## Clinical Importance

T-DXd represents an important advance in HER2-directed treatment for gastric cancer.

---

## Key Concepts

- HER2-targeted medicine
- Precision oncology
- Used for selected HER2-positive patients

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — Why Is HER2 Testing Required?

## Patient Explanation

Before T-DXd can be considered, the tumor must first be tested for HER2.

Only patients whose tumors are confirmed to be HER2-positive may be candidates for this treatment.

HER2 testing helps doctors determine whether T-DXd is an appropriate option.

---

## Clinical Importance

HER2 testing is essential before HER2-directed therapies can be selected.

---

## Key Concepts

- HER2-positive required
- Biomarker-guided treatment
- Personalized therapy

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — How Is T-DXd Different from Trastuzumab?

## Patient Explanation

Both trastuzumab and T-DXd are HER2-targeted medicines.

However, they are different medicines and are used in different clinical situations according to current treatment guidelines.

Your healthcare team will determine which medicine, if any, is appropriate for you.

---

## Clinical Importance

Multiple HER2-targeted therapies are available, each with specific clinical roles.

---

## Key Concepts

- Both target HER2
- Different medicines
- Different clinical uses

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — When May T-DXd Be Used?

## Patient Explanation

T-DXd may be considered for selected patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.

Whether it is recommended depends on factors such as:

- HER2 status;
- previous treatments;
- cancer stage;
- overall health;
- current treatment guidelines.

---

## Clinical Importance

Treatment recommendations are individualized according to the complete clinical situation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not suitable for everyone
- Individualized treatment
- Guideline-based care

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — What Are the Potential Benefits?

## Patient Explanation

For appropriate patients, T-DXd may:

- provide another treatment option;
- support personalized cancer treatment;
- improve treatment outcomes in appropriate clinical settings.

The benefit varies between individuals.

---

## Clinical Importance

T-DXd expands treatment options within HER2-directed therapy.

---

## Key Concepts

- Additional treatment option
- Precision medicine
- Individual benefit varies

---

# Knowledge Block 6 — What Are the Limitations?

## Patient Explanation

T-DXd is not appropriate for every patient.

Treatment depends on:

- HER2-positive status;
- overall health;
- previous treatments;
- clinical judgement.

The medicine also has potential side effects that require careful monitoring.

---

## Clinical Importance

Treatment decisions require balancing potential benefits and risks.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not appropriate for everyone
- Requires careful assessment
- Ongoing monitoring

---

# Knowledge Block 7 — Common Side Effects and Questions

## Patient Explanation

Like all cancer medicines, T-DXd may cause side effects.

Some side effects can be serious, so patients are monitored carefully throughout treatment.

Your healthcare team will explain which symptoms should be reported promptly.

Detailed side-effect management is discussed in separate Population Packages.

---

## Common Misconception

**Myth**

T-DXd is simply another name for trastuzumab.

**Fact**

Although both medicines target HER2, they are different drugs with different roles in treatment.

---

# Knowledge Block 8 — What Should Patients Expect?

Before starting T-DXd, patients should discuss:

- HER2 test results;
- expected benefits;
- possible risks;
- treatment goals;
- alternative treatment options.

Shared decision-making helps patients understand why this treatment may or may not be appropriate.

---

## Clinical Importance

Patient education supports informed treatment decisions.

---

# Key Messages

- Trastuzumab deruxtecan (T-DXd) is a HER2-targeted medicine.
- HER2 testing is required before treatment.
- T-DXd and trastuzumab are different medicines.
- T-DXd is appropriate only for selected HER2-positive patients.
- Treatment decisions depend on the complete clinical situation.
- Patients should discuss benefits and risks with their healthcare team.
