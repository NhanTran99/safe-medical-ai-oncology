# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is radiotherapy and what is its role in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based understanding of radiotherapy as one component of multidisciplinary treatment for gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package establishes the foundational knowledge required before introducing chemoradiotherapy, radiotherapy planning, radiation techniques, radiation toxicities and other radiotherapy-related Population Packages.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Radiotherapy is an important local treatment modality.
- Radiotherapy is recommended only in selected clinical situations.
- Radiotherapy may be combined with chemotherapy as part of multidisciplinary treatment.
- Radiotherapy may improve local disease control or relieve symptoms.
- Treatment is carefully planned before delivery.
- Most patients receive treatment over multiple sessions.
- Side effects differ among patients and are often manageable.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

All major references consistently describe radiotherapy as an important component of multidisciplinary management for selected patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.

Differences among references primarily involve:

- treatment algorithms;
- indications;
- educational wording;
- level of technical detail.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- clinical principles from NCCN;
- evidence interpretation from JNCCN;
- patient-oriented explanations from NCI and ACS;
- internationally harmonized terminology from ESMO.
