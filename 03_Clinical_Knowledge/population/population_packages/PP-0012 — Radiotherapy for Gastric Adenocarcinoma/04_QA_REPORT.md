# Scope Verification

This Population Package intentionally focuses only on the foundational concept of radiotherapy.

The following topics are appropriately excluded and delegated to future Population Packages:

- Radiation planning
- CT simulation
- IMRT
- VMAT
- Proton therapy
- Radiation dose and fractionation
- Detailed toxicity management
- Chemoradiotherapy protocols
- Radiation physics
- Re-irradiation

This satisfies the Gold Population Package principle of **Atomic Knowledge (Single Educational Question).**
