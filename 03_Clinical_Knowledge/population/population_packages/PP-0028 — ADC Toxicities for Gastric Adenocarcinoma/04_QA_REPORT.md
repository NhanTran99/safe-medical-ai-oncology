# Scope Verification

This Population Package intentionally focuses only on the **general safety principles and toxicities of antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).**

The following topics are intentionally excluded and delegated to future Population Packages:

- Drug-specific toxicity profiles
- Interstitial lung disease (ILD)
- Cardiotoxicity
- Ocular toxicity
- Peripheral neuropathy
- Hepatotoxicity
- CTCAE grading
- Dose modification algorithms
- Supportive medications
- Clinical trial safety analyses

This satisfies the Gold Population Package principle of **Atomic Knowledge (Single Educational Question).**
