# Clinical Scope

## Included

- Definition of the peritoneum
- Definition of peritoneal metastasis
- Relationship between gastric cancer and the peritoneum
- Difference between primary gastric cancer and peritoneal metastasis
- Relationship between peritoneal metastasis and cancer spread
- Importance of appropriate medical evaluation

---

## Explicitly Excluded

- Peritoneal Carcinomatosis Index (PCI)
- Staging laparoscopy
- Peritoneal cytology
- Ascites
- HIPEC
- Cytoreductive surgery
- Molecular mechanisms
- M1 classification
- Treatment recommendations
- Prognostic statistics

These topics are intentionally delegated to independent Population Packages.
