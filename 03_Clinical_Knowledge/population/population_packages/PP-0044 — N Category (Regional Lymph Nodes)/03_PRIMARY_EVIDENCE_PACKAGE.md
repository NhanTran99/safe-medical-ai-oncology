# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What does the N category mean in the TNM staging system?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of the **N Category (Regional Lymph Nodes)** within the TNM staging system, including what regional lymph nodes are, what the N category represents, and how lymph node involvement contributes to cancer staging, treatment planning and prognostic assessment.

This Population Package introduces the concept of the N category without discussing the detailed AJCC N classifications, lymph node stations, lymphadenectomy techniques or stage-specific treatment recommendations.

---

# Scope

## Included

- Definition of the N category
- Meaning of regional lymph nodes
- Simple explanation of lymph nodes
- Purpose of the N category
- Relationship between N category and regional lymph node involvement
- General relationship between increasing N category and disease extent
- Contribution to TNM staging
- Contribution to treatment planning
- Contribution to prognostic assessment
- Integration with T and M categories

---

## Excluded

- N0
- N1
- N2
- N3
- Number of positive lymph nodes
- Regional lymph node stations
- Sentinel lymph node
- D1 lymphadenectomy
- D2 lymphadenectomy
- AJCC Stage Groups
- Stage-specific survival statistics
- Treatment recommendations according to N category

These topics require dedicated Population Packages.

---

# Evidence Matrix

| The N category represents regional lymph node involvement within the TNM staging system. | NCI PDQ |

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- The N category describes **regional lymph node involvement**.
- Regional lymph nodes are lymph nodes located close to the stomach.
- Lymph nodes are an important part of the body's immune and lymphatic systems.
- Increasing N categories generally reflect greater involvement of regional lymph nodes.
- The N category is one component of the TNM staging system.
- Doctors combine the N category with the T and M categories to determine overall stage.
- Clinical decisions are based on the complete clinical picture rather than the N category alone.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence is highly consistent regarding:

- the definition of the N category;
- the role of regional lymph node involvement in gastric cancer staging;
- the contribution of the N category to prognosis and treatment planning;
- the importance of integrating the N category with T, M and other clinical findings.

Differences between references are limited to terminology and educational emphasis.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- scientific concepts from NCI PDQ;
- patient-centered educational language from ACS;
- terminology consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.

---

# Evidence Gaps

This Population Package intentionally does **not** discuss:

- detailed AJCC N classifications;
- lymph node station mapping;
- number of positive lymph nodes;
- D1 and D2 lymphadenectomy;
- sentinel lymph node concepts;
- AJCC stage grouping;
- stage-specific prognosis;
- treatment recommendations according to N category.

These subjects require independent Population Packages.

---
