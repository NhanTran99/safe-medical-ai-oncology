**Reason:**

Patients may encounter discussions about whether two variants are located **in cis** or **in trans** during genetic testing. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how the relative position of variants may contribute **Supporting Benign Evidence** while emphasizing that BP2 alone cannot establish benign classification.
