# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is BP2 (Observed in Trans with a Pathogenic Variant for a Fully Penetrant Dominant Gene / Observed in Cis with a Pathogenic Variant)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **BP2 (Observed in Trans with a Pathogenic Variant for a Fully Penetrant Dominant Gene / Observed in Cis with a Pathogenic Variant)** as one of the standardized **Supporting Benign Evidence** criteria within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **BP2 evaluates the relative position of two genetic variants**. Under specific situations defined by the ACMG/AMP framework, observing a variant **in trans** with a pathogenic variant in a fully penetrant dominant gene or **in cis** with a pathogenic variant may provide **supporting benign evidence**. However, **BP2 represents supporting evidence only** and must always be interpreted together with clinical findings, laboratory evidence, and other ACMG evidence categories before a final variant classification is made.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- BP2 is one individual ACMG evidence criterion.
- BP2 considers the relative position of genetic variants.
- Variant location may contribute supporting benign evidence.
- BP2 alone cannot establish benign classification.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **BP2** as one of the **Supporting Benign Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that the relationship between two variants (**in cis** or **in trans**) may contribute supporting benign evidence in specific clinical situations. ClinGen SVI provides additional implementation recommendations for BP2, but these technical refinements intentionally remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
