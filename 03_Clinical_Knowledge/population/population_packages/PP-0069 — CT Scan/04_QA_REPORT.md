# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of CT;
- purpose of CT during cancer care;
- repeated CT examinations;
- role of CT in treatment monitoring;
- basic concept of contrast material;
- integration into comprehensive clinical assessment.

Excluded:

- CT physics;
- radiation exposure;
- contrast safety;
- CT preparation;
- CT interpretation;
- RECIST measurements;
- treatment planning.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational patient understanding of **CT imaging** within cancer care. It explains why CT is frequently performed throughout diagnosis and treatment while deliberately avoiding technical radiology content. The package provides the conceptual foundation for subsequent Population Packages on **MRI**, **PET/CT**, **contrast agents**, **CT preparation**, **radiation safety**, and **understanding CT reports**.
