# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational concept of **malignant tumors as cancerous growths** while deliberately avoiding disease-specific complexity. Together with **PP-0082 (Benign Tumors)**, it forms the conceptual basis for **PP-0084 (Benign vs Malignant Tumors)** and later Population Packages covering **Metastasis**, **TNM Staging**, **Tumor Grading**, and **Cancer Treatment**.
