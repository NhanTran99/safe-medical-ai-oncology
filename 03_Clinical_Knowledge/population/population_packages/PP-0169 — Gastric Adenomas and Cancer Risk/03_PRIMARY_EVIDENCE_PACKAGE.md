# Evidence Notes

## Source-Grounded Interpretation

The package uses the supplied Source Materials as the primary evidence base. It does not silently replace missing evidence with general medical knowledge.

## Population Context

Where the evidence is derived from Japan, Korea, China, high-incidence populations, hereditary groups, or selected clinical cohorts, that context is preserved.

## Study-Specific Estimates

Any numerical estimate appearing in the package is retained as a study/program-specific result unless the source explicitly establishes general applicability.

## Uncertainty

Where the NCI evidence states that mortality benefit, standard cutoffs, generalizability, or other parameters remain uncertain, the uncertainty is preserved.

# Clinical Claims Summary

- Gastric Adenomas and Cancer Risk is clinically meaningful within gastric-cancer risk/screening.
- The evidence base contains both established findings and context-dependent observations.
- Screening/risk information does not itself establish a diagnosis.
- Population and clinical context materially affect interpretation.
- Universal algorithms should not be inferred when the supplied evidence does not establish them.

# Evidence Consistency Review

The source set is interpreted without silently reconciling conflicting or incomplete evidence. Observational findings are identified as observational. Population-specific results are not generalized beyond their source context.

# Evidence Gaps

- mortality-benefit claims beyond the cited evidence;

These gaps remain explicit.
