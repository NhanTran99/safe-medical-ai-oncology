# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is chemotherapy and what is its role in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based understanding of chemotherapy as one of the principal treatment modalities for gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package establishes the foundational knowledge required before introducing regimen-specific chemotherapy, perioperative therapy, systemic therapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy, toxicity management and supportive care Population Packages.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Chemotherapy is a cornerstone treatment for gastric adenocarcinoma.
- The role of chemotherapy depends on the stage of disease and the overall treatment strategy.
- Chemotherapy may be administered before surgery, after surgery or for advanced disease.
- Treatment is usually delivered in cycles to balance effectiveness with recovery.
- Combination chemotherapy is commonly used in appropriate patients.
- Side effects are expected because chemotherapy also affects some normal cells, but many toxicities can be prevented or managed.
- Treatment decisions should always be individualized.
