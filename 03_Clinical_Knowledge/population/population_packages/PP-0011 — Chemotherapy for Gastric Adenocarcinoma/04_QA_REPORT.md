This Population Package intentionally focuses only on the foundational concept of chemotherapy.

- Individual chemotherapy regimens
- Chemotherapy drug selection
- Drug dosing
- Toxicity grading
- Side-effect management
- Targeted therapy
- Immunotherapy
- Nutrition during chemotherapy
- Treatment response evaluation

This satisfies the Gold Population Package principle of **Atomic Knowledge (Single Educational Question).**
