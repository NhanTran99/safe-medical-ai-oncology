# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is CLDN18.2 testing and why is it important in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an evidence-based understanding of CLDN18.2 testing as an emerging biomarker assessment in gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package explains why CLDN18.2 testing is performed, when it is recommended, how the results contribute to treatment planning and why CLDN18.2 testing is becoming an important component of precision oncology.

It serves as the prerequisite before introducing CLDN18.2 biology, laboratory testing methodologies, companion diagnostics and CLDN18.2-targeted therapies.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- CLDN18.2 testing is an important biomarker assessment in modern gastric cancer care.
- CLDN18.2 testing helps personalize targeted therapy decisions.
- CLDN18.2-positive and CLDN18.2-negative results should always be interpreted within the broader clinical context.
- CLDN18.2 is one component of comprehensive biomarker evaluation.
- Testing is commonly performed using tumor tissue.
- Patients benefit from understanding how CLDN18.2 results contribute to shared treatment decision-making.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary authoritative references.

All major references consistently recommend CLDN18.2 testing as an important biomarker assessment for appropriate patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.

Differences among references primarily involve:

- laboratory methodology;

- companion diagnostic requirements;
- targeted therapy algorithms.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- clinical recommendations from NCCN;
- evidence interpretation from JNCCN;
- patient-oriented explanations from NCI and ACS;
