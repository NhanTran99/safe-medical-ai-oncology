# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational patient understanding of **contrast agents** used in medical imaging. It clarifies that contrast materials improve visualization rather than treat cancer, emphasizes that contrast is used selectively based on clinical need, and prepares patients for future educational packages covering **contrast allergy**, **kidney function**, **specific contrast agents**, **contrast safety**, and **preparation for contrast-enhanced imaging**.
