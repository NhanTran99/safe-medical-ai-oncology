## Intended Runtime Usage

Use when a patient asks:
- whether diet affects stomach-cancer risk;
- whether salt or preserved foods matter;
- whether fruits and vegetables lower risk;
- whether specific foods/supplements prevent gastric cancer;
- what dietary changes are reasonable for risk reduction.

Do not use as:
- a generic nutrition curriculum;
- an individualized diet prescription;
- a post-gastrectomy nutrition guide;
- a supplement-treatment guide;
- a gastric-cancer recurrence-prevention package.

## Evidence Character

### Established / guideline-consistent
- Diet is part of the environmental gastric-cancer risk framework.
- High salt and certain preserved-food exposures are risk-associated.
- Low fruit/vegetable intake is risk-associated.

### Supported association
- Fresh fruits/vegetables and lower risk.
- Whole grains and selected dietary factors.
- Processed/grilled/barbecued foods and risk.

### Uncertain / not proven as intervention
- Changing diet as a direct gastric-cancer prevention intervention.
- Specific supplements.
- Single-food preventive effects.
