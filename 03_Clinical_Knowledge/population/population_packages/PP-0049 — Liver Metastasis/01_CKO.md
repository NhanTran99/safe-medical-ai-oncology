# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Explain what liver metastasis means.
- Understand the liver in simple, patient-friendly language.
- Understand that gastric cancer can spread to the liver.
- Recognize that liver metastasis is still gastric cancer, not primary liver cancer.
- Understand that not every patient with gastric cancer develops liver metastasis.
- Understand that doctors use appropriate medical evaluation to determine whether liver metastasis is present.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is the Liver?

## Patient Explanation

The **liver** is one of the largest organs in the body.

It performs many important jobs, including helping process nutrients, remove waste products and produce substances that support normal body function.

---

## Clinical Importance

The liver is an essential organ and is one of the possible sites where gastric cancer may spread.

---

## Key Concepts

- The liver is an important organ.
- It performs many vital functions.
- It is separate from the stomach.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Is Liver Metastasis?

## Patient Explanation

**Liver metastasis** means that stomach cancer has spread from the stomach to the liver.

This is one type of metastatic gastric cancer.

---

## Clinical Importance

Liver metastasis represents distant spread of gastric cancer to the liver.

---

## Key Concepts

- Gastric cancer can spread to the liver.
- Liver metastasis is one form of metastatic disease.
- It develops from the original stomach cancer.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Is Liver Metastasis the Same as Liver Cancer?

## Patient Explanation

No.

If stomach cancer spreads to the liver, it is still called **gastric cancer with liver metastasis**.

It is **not** the same as a cancer that starts in the liver.

---

## Clinical Importance

Metastatic tumors retain the biological identity of the original cancer.

---

## Key Concepts

- Liver metastasis is different from primary liver cancer.
- The original cancer remains gastric cancer.
- Treatment decisions are based on the primary cancer.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does Every Patient Develop Liver Metastasis?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Many patients with gastric cancer never develop liver metastasis.

Whether cancer spreads to the liver depends on the individual disease.

---

## Clinical Importance

Patterns of metastatic spread vary between patients.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not every patient develops liver metastasis.
- Disease patterns differ between individuals.
- Medical evaluation is required.

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — How Do Doctors Determine Whether Liver Metastasis Is Present?

## Patient Explanation

Doctors do not determine liver metastasis based only on symptoms.

They combine information from:

- imaging studies;
- pathology findings;
- surgery (when appropriate);
- physical examination;
- other clinical information.

Only after reviewing all available information can doctors determine whether liver metastasis is present.

---

## Clinical Importance

Diagnosis requires comprehensive clinical assessment using appropriate investigations.

---

## Key Concepts

- Diagnosis requires medical evaluation.
- Multiple sources of information are considered.
- Symptoms alone are not enough.

---

# Knowledge Block 6 — Should I Compare My Situation with Someone Else's?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Every person's gastric cancer is different.

Even if two patients both have liver metastasis, they may have different disease characteristics, treatment options and overall clinical situations.

---

## Clinical Importance

Management of metastatic gastric cancer is individualized.

---

## Common Misconception

**Myth**

Everyone with liver metastasis has the same disease and the same treatment.

**Fact**

Liver metastasis varies greatly between patients. Doctors evaluate the complete clinical picture before discussing treatment or prognosis.

---

# Key Messages

- The liver is an important organ in the body.
- Liver metastasis means gastric cancer has spread to the liver.
- Liver metastasis is still gastric cancer, not primary liver cancer.
- Not every patient with gastric cancer develops liver metastasis.
- Doctors diagnose liver metastasis using appropriate medical evaluation.
