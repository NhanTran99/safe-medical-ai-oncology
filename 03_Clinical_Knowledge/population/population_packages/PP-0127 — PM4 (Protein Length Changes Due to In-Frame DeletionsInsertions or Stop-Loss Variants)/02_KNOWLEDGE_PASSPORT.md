**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PM4** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how certain genetic variants that change the normal length of a protein may contribute one category of moderate evidence during professional interpretation while emphasizing that these changes do not automatically indicate disease.
