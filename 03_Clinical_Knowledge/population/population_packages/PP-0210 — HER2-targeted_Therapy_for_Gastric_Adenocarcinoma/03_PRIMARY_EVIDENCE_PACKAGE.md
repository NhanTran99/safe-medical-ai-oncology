# Clinical Question

## Primary Educational Question

> **What is HER2-targeted therapy for gastric adenocarcinoma, how does
> HER2 status connect to treatment, what are the major HER2-directed
> treatment approaches, and what should patients understand about
> benefit, limitations, safety, and treatment context?**

## Clinical-Educational Intent

Provide a source-grounded, patient-facing explanation of HER2-directed
treatment without converting the package into a drug-specific treatment
manual or an individualized prescribing algorithm.

The package is deliberately positioned:

**HER2 Testing**

→ **Biomarker-Directed Treatment Selection**

→ **HER2-targeted Therapy**

→ **Trastuzumab / T-DXd / specialized downstream packages**

# Evidence Notes

## Note 1 --- HER2 as a Predictive Treatment Biomarker

The supplied NCCN framework distinguishes HER2 treatment relevance from
broader prognostic interpretation.

The key clinical role for this PP is:

**HER2 status**

→ **potential HER2-directed treatment benefit**

rather than:

**HER2 status**

→ **automatic prognosis**.

This distinction is necessary to prevent overclaiming.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Note 2 --- HER2 Testing Is Upstream

The testing package owns:

-   specimen;
-   IHC;
-   IHC category;
-   equivocal interpretation;
-   ISH/FISH;
-   testing limitations.

PP-0210 begins once a clinically relevant HER2 result is available.

This is an explicit architecture decision, not an omission.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Note 3 --- ToGA

NCI reports ToGA as an international randomized phase III trial in
HER2-positive metastatic, inoperable locally advanced, or recurrent
gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma.

The reported median OS was:

**13.8 months**

with trastuzumab + chemotherapy versus:

**11.1 months**

with chemotherapy alone.

The reported HR was:

**0.74**.

The educational conclusion is that HER2-directed treatment provided a
clinically meaningful benefit in an appropriately selected population.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Note 4 --- KEYNOTE-811

NCI describes KEYNOTE-811 as a randomized study adding pembrolizumab or
placebo to trastuzumab plus chemotherapy in HER2-positive metastatic
gastric adenocarcinoma.

The initial interim analysis demonstrated a higher response rate in the
pembrolizumab-containing group.

NCCN incorporates this combination into the appropriate first-line
HER2-positive treatment architecture.

PP-0210 uses this evidence to establish that HER2-directed treatment can
participate in a multimodality systemic-treatment combination.

It does not own pembrolizumab selection.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Note 5 --- DESTINY-Gastric01

DESTINY-Gastric01 enrolled patients with advanced/metastatic gastric or
EGJ adenocarcinoma whose disease had progressed after at least two
previous treatment lines including trastuzumab.

NCCN reports:

  Endpoint             T-DXd   Physician's Choice
  ------------ ------------- --------------------
  ORR                  40.5%                  11%
  Median OS      12.5 months           8.4 months
  Median PFS      5.6 months           3.5 months
  Median DOR     11.3 months           3.9 months

The evidence supports T-DXd as a major subsequent HER2-directed
treatment approach.

The package explicitly states that these are trial-level results and not
individual predictions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Note 6 --- DESTINY-Gastric01 Safety

NCCN reports important adverse events with T-DXd, including:

-   neutropenia;
-   anemia;
-   leukopenia;
-   ILD/pneumonitis.

The source reports a drug-related death associated with
ILD/pneumonitis-related complications.

This evidence is why PP-0210 must not present targeted therapy as "low
risk" simply because it is biologically targeted.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Note 7 --- DESTINY-Gastric02

DESTINY-Gastric02 complements the DESTINY-Gastric01 evidence in Western
patients with unresectable/metastatic HER2-positive gastric/EGJ
adenocarcinoma after progression on or after first-line therapy.

The package uses it to reinforce:

-   treatment-line dependence;
-   prior-treatment dependence;
-   population specificity;
-   subsequent-line HER2-directed treatment.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence supports the following major educational claims:

1.  HER2 is a clinically important treatment target in selected
    gastric/GEJ adenocarcinoma.
2.  HER2 testing is necessary to identify the relevant treatment
    population.
3.  HER2 positivity does not independently determine the entire
    treatment plan.
4.  Trastuzumab is a foundational HER2-directed treatment.
5.  Trastuzumab + chemotherapy has randomized phase III evidence.
6.  KEYNOTE-811 supports selected trastuzumab + chemotherapy +
    pembrolizumab treatment.
7.  T-DXd is an important subsequent HER2-directed therapy.
8.  DESTINY-Gastric01 provides randomized evidence of benefit for T-DXd
    after prior HER2-directed treatment.
9.  DESTINY-Gastric02 provides complementary evidence in a Western
    population.
10. HER2-directed treatment can cause important adverse effects.
11. Trastuzumab has cardiac safety considerations.
12. T-DXd has ILD/pneumonitis risk.
13. HER2-targeted treatment does not guarantee response.
14. Treatment resistance may occur.
15. Treatment decisions depend on disease setting, treatment line,
    previous therapy, and patient factors.

# Evidence Interpretation by Treatment Context

## Context A --- First-Line Advanced HER2-positive Disease

The major evidence-supported paradigm is:

**HER2-positive advanced disease**

→ **trastuzumab + chemotherapy**

with selected patients also receiving pembrolizumab-containing treatment
according to the current treatment framework.

PP-0210 should not convert this into a universal prescription.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Context B --- After Prior Trastuzumab

The treatment context changes.

For selected patients with progression after prior
trastuzumab-containing treatment:

**T-DXd**

may become an important subsequent HER2-directed option.

The exact choice remains clinical.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Context C --- HER2-negative Disease

The HER2-directed pathway is not the relevant pathway.

The package must not imply absence of treatment options.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Context D --- Earlier-Stage Disease

The supplied evidence does not justify treating metastatic HER2-directed
evidence as a universal perioperative or adjuvant treatment algorithm.

Therefore early-stage HER2-directed treatment is not a core PP-0210
pathway.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Evidence Limitations

## Limitation 1 --- Trial population

Clinical-trial results describe selected populations.

They should not be interpreted as guarantees for every patient.

## Limitation 2 --- HER2 heterogeneity

Tumors can contain heterogeneous biological populations.

## Limitation 3 --- Treatment evolution

The HER2 landscape continues to change.

## Limitation 4 --- Resistance

Response can be temporary.

## Limitation 5 --- Toxicity

Targeted therapy remains pharmacologically active treatment and can
produce serious adverse effects.

## Limitation 6 --- Context dependence

A HER2-positive result must be integrated with stage, prior therapy,
treatment line, other biomarkers, performance status and patient goals.

# Evidence Gaps

The following gaps are intentionally not filled with unsupported general
knowledge:

1.  Exact individualized treatment selection for a specific patient.
2.  Universal treatment sequencing across every disease setting.
3.  A universal algorithm for repeat HER2 testing after progression.
4.  Detailed resistance mechanisms.
5.  Detailed cardiac monitoring thresholds.
6.  Detailed ILD/pneumonitis management.
7.  Drug-specific dosing and administration.
8.  Detailed combination-treatment algorithms.
9.  Individual prognosis based solely on HER2 status.

These are downstream or context-dependent.
