# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the repository's **foundational fusion biomarker node**. It bridges **molecular sequencing technologies** and **clinically actionable biomarkers** by explaining that **Fusion Gene Testing is a clinical testing objective rather than a single laboratory technology**. It prepares the knowledge graph for subsequent Population Packages covering **Companion Diagnostics**, **Targeted Therapy**, and individual fusion biomarkers such as **ALK**, **ROS1**, **RET**, **NTRK**, and **BCR::ABL1**, while preserving the repository's Atomic Knowledge Principle.
