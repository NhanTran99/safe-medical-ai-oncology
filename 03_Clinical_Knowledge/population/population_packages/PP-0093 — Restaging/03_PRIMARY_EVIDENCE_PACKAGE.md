# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is restaging?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **restaging** as the process of reassessing the current status of cancer after diagnosis or treatment.

This Population Package explains why restaging may be performed, the information used during reassessment, and how restaging supports ongoing cancer management without implying that the original stage was incorrect.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Restaging reassesses the current status of cancer.
- It is performed only when clinically indicated.
- Multiple sources of clinical information may contribute.
- Restaging supports ongoing treatment decisions.
- Restaging differs from the original staging assessment.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently describe restaging as an updated assessment performed when new clinical information is required during the patient's cancer journey. Restaging is intended to guide future management rather than replace the original stage assigned at diagnosis.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- clinical management principles from NCCN and ESMO;
- educational principles from NCI and ACS;
- staging concepts consistent with AJCC and JNCCN.
