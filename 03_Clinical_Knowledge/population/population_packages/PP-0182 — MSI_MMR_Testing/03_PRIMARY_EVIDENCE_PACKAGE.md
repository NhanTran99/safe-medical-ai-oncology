# 2. Clinical Question

> **What are MSI and MMR testing in gastric adenocarcinoma, how are these biomarkers assessed and interpreted, and what does an MSI-H/dMMR result mean for clinical care?**

---

# 3. Educational Intent

The evidence package must support a patient-facing explanation of the complete pathway:

**new diagnosis**

→ **universal MSI/MMR testing**

→ **tumor tissue**

→ **MSI by PCR/NGS or MMR by IHC**

→ **MSI-H/MSI-L/MSS or dMMR/MMR-proficient interpretation**

→ **clinical relevance**

→ **immunotherapy-selection context**

→ **genetic counseling/further hereditary assessment when appropriate**

The package must stop before detailed laboratory methodology, detailed genetics, and treatment prescribing.

# 8. Evidence Notes

## Evidence Note 1 — Universal Testing

NCCN v2.2026 explicitly recommends universal testing for MSI by PCR or NGS, or MMR by IHC, for all newly diagnosed gastric cancers. fileciteturn31file0L1-L1

This is a central PP-0182 claim.

It should not be narrowed to metastatic disease.

---

## Evidence Note 2 — MSI and MMR Are Distinct Testing Approaches

NCCN explicitly notes that:

> PCR/NGS for MSI and IHC for MMR proteins measure different biological effects caused by dMMR function. fileciteturn31file6L1-L1

This distinction must be preserved.

---

## Evidence Note 3 — MMR Proteins

The guideline identifies:

- MLH1;
- MSH2;
- MSH6;
- PMS2

as the MMR proteins assessed by IHC. fileciteturn31file16L1-L1

---

## Evidence Note 4 — MMR Interpretation

NCCN defines:

- no loss of nuclear MMR protein expression → no evidence of dMMR / low probability of MSI-H;
- loss of nuclear expression of one or more MMR proteins → dMMR. fileciteturn31file6L1-L1

---

## Evidence Note 5 — MSI Interpretation

NCCN defines:

### MSS

Microsatellite stable.

### MSI-L

1%–29% of markers exhibit instability, with the corresponding specified marker interpretation.

### MSI-H

≥30% of markers exhibit instability, with the corresponding specified marker interpretation. fileciteturn31file6L1-L1

---

## Evidence Note 6 — Specimens

NCCN's pathology table includes:

- biopsy;
- EMR;
- gastrectomy

and recommends universal MSI/MMR testing in the relevant specimens. fileciteturn31file2L1-L1

---

## Evidence Note 7 — FFPE

NCCN specifies FFPE tissue for MSI/MMR testing. fileciteturn31file0L1-L1

---

## Evidence Note 8 — Genetic Counseling

NCCN states that patients with MSI-H or dMMR tumors should be referred to a genetics counselor for further assessment in the appropriate clinical context. fileciteturn31file0L1-L1

This supports a genetics bridge but not an automatic Lynch syndrome diagnosis.

---

## Evidence Note 9 — Prognostic Context

NCCN reports better prognosis in MSI-H/dMMR gastric tumors compared with microsatellite-stable/MMR-proficient tumors. fileciteturn31file7L1-L1

This is retained as population-level context rather than individualized prognosis.

---

## Evidence Note 10 — Clinicopathologic Associations

NCCN reports associations with:

- earlier stage;
- older age;
- intestinal subtype;
- distal stomach location. fileciteturn31file7L1-L1

These are associations, not diagnostic criteria.

---

## Evidence Note 11 — Prevalence

NCCN cites approximately:

- 22% MSI-H in TCGA gastric adenocarcinoma;
- 19% MSI-H/dMMR in another pan-cancer analysis.

These estimates are population-level and methodology-dependent. fileciteturn31file7L1-L1

# 9. Clinical Claims Summary

### Claim A

MSI/MMR testing is part of the initial gastric-cancer biomarker pathway.

### Claim B

NCCN recommends universal MSI/MMR testing for all newly diagnosed gastric cancers.

### Claim C

MSI can be assessed by PCR or NGS.

### Claim D

MMR can be assessed by IHC.

### Claim E

MLH1, MSH2, MSH6 and PMS2 are key MMR proteins evaluated by IHC.

### Claim F

MSI interpretation includes MSS, MSI-L and MSI-H.

### Claim G

MMR interpretation includes dMMR versus no evidence of dMMR.

### Claim H

MSI and MMR are related but different testing approaches.

### Claim I

MSI-H/dMMR can influence immunotherapy-related treatment discussions.

### Claim J

MSI-H/dMMR may prompt genetic counseling in an appropriate clinical context.

### Claim K

MSI-H/dMMR is not itself a diagnosis of Lynch syndrome.

### Claim L

Population-level MSI-H/dMMR prognosis should not be converted into individualized prognosis.

# 11. Evidence Gaps

## Gap 1 — MSI/MMR assay concordance

MSI and MMR assays evaluate related but different biological effects.

### Handling

Do not claim universal one-to-one equivalence.

---

## Gap 2 — Individual prognosis

Population-level prognostic associations do not determine an individual patient's prognosis.

### Handling

Retain population-level wording.

---

## Gap 3 — Hereditary origin

An MSI-H/dMMR tumor does not itself establish hereditary cancer.

### Handling

Delegate hereditary assessment to genetics packages.

---

## Gap 4 — Universal superiority of one testing method

The sources support PCR/NGS for MSI and IHC for MMR but do not justify a blanket claim that one method is always superior.

### Handling

Present both as recognized approaches.

---

## Gap 5 — Individual treatment selection

MSI-H/dMMR can influence treatment decisions, but the package does not establish a universal treatment algorithm.

### Handling

Delegate detailed treatment to immunotherapy packages.
