# 10. Clinical Interpretation Rules

### Rule 1

**Universal testing**

All newly diagnosed gastric cancers should undergo MSI by PCR/NGS or MMR by IHC under the NCCN pathway.

### Rule 2

**MSI interpretation**

- MSS
- MSI-L
- MSI-H

### Rule 3

**MMR interpretation**

- no loss of nuclear MMR protein expression → no evidence of dMMR / low probability of MSI-H;
- loss of nuclear expression of one or more MMR proteins → dMMR.

### Rule 4

**MSI ≠ MMR**

They are related but different testing approaches.

### Rule 5

**Tumor testing ≠ germline testing**

An MSI-H/dMMR tumor result does not itself establish inherited predisposition.

### Rule 6

**MSI-H/dMMR ≠ automatic treatment**

The result informs clinical decisions but does not independently determine treatment.

### Rule 7

**Population prognosis ≠ individual prognosis**

Better outcomes reported in MSI-H/dMMR populations should not be converted into an individualized prognosis.
