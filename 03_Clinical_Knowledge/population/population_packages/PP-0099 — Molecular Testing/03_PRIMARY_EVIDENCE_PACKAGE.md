# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is molecular testing?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **molecular testing** as laboratory testing that evaluates DNA, RNA, or other molecular characteristics of cancer or other biological samples.

This Population Package explains the purpose of molecular testing, its relationship with biomarker testing and precision medicine, and its role in modern oncology while emphasizing that molecular testing is individualized and should be performed only when clinically appropriate.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Molecular testing evaluates clinically relevant molecular alterations.
- Molecular testing is one component of biomarker testing.
- Molecular testing contributes to precision medicine.
- Different cancers require different molecular testing strategies.
- Molecular testing should always be interpreted together with other clinical information.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently describe molecular testing as an important component of modern cancer diagnostics and precision oncology. Molecular testing is performed to answer specific clinical questions and is selected according to the cancer type, available evidence, and current professional guidelines. Results are interpreted alongside pathology, imaging, staging, and the patient's overall clinical condition.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- terminology from NCI;
- oncology guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP;
- patient education principles from ACS;
- international guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
