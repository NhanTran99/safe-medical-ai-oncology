# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of oligometastatic disease;
- explanation that it represents limited metastatic spread;
- recognition that it is a clinical concept rather than a TNM stage;
- explanation that no universal definition exists;
- importance of comprehensive clinical evaluation.

Excluded:

- numerical definitions;
- ESTRO/EORTC consensus;
- SABR/SBRT;
- metastasectomy;
- treatment;
- prognosis.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational concept of **oligometastatic disease** while intentionally avoiding operational definitions that vary between cancer types, studies and professional societies.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- ESTRO/EORTC Definition of Oligometastatic Disease
- How Many Metastases Are Considered Oligometastatic?
- SABR/SBRT
- Metastasectomy
- Local Treatment for Oligometastatic Disease
- Tumor Burden
- RECIST
- Prognosis of Oligometastatic Disease

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
