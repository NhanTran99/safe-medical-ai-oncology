## Intended Runtime Usage

Use this package when the user asks:
- whether overweight or obesity increases stomach-cancer risk;
- whether body weight matters for gastric-cancer prevention;
- whether losing weight reduces gastric-cancer risk;
- whether BMI can predict gastric-cancer risk;
- how obesity fits with other gastric-cancer risk factors.

Do not use this package as a generic obesity-management resource.

## Evidence Classification

### Established / directly supported
- Excess body weight is associated with increased risk of some types of stomach cancer.

### Emerging / context-dependent
- Association between excess body weight, gastric-cancer risk, and tissue damage associated with GERD.
- Exact subtype-specific implications.
- Magnitude of risk by BMI category.

### Not established by the supplied source set
- Individual absolute risk from BMI.
- Gastric-cancer-specific BMI threshold.
- Exact risk reduction from a specific amount of weight loss.
- Bariatric surgery as proven gastric-cancer prevention.
- Anti-obesity medication as proven gastric-cancer prevention.
