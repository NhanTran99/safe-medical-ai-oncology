# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is biomarker testing and why is it important in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an evidence-based understanding of biomarker testing as the foundation of modern precision oncology.

This Population Package introduces why biomarker testing is performed, when it is recommended, how it influences treatment decisions and why different patients may receive different therapies.

It serves as the prerequisite before introducing individual biomarker tests such as HER2, PD-L1, MSI-H/dMMR and CLDN18.2.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Biomarker testing is an essential component of modern gastric cancer care.
- Different tumors have different biological characteristics.
- Biomarker results help personalize treatment selection.
- Several biomarkers may be evaluated depending on the clinical situation.
- Biomarker testing connects pathology with precision oncology.
- Results should always be interpreted within the overall clinical context.
- Patients should understand how biomarker results influence treatment decisions.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary authoritative references.

All major references consistently recommend biomarker testing as an important part of treatment planning for appropriate patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.

Differences among references primarily involve:

- specific biomarkers discussed;
- timing of implementation;
- treatment algorithms;
- educational wording.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- clinical recommendations from NCCN;
- evidence interpretation from JNCCN;
- patient-oriented explanations from NCI and ACS;
- internationally harmonized terminology from ESMO.
