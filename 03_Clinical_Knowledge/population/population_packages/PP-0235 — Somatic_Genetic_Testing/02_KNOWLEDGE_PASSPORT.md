**Reason:**

Patients may encounter somatic genetic testing during diagnostic work-up, molecular characterization, treatment planning, response assessment, or evaluation of resistance. The package provides foundational understanding of why tumor genomic testing may be requested and how its results fit into the wider clinical pathway.

# Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

Somatic genetic testing is a major bridge between tumor molecular characterization and downstream clinical interpretation. It connects sequencing/test selection with variant interpretation, biomarker assessment, precision oncology, and subsequent liquid-biopsy/ctDNA packages.

# Clinical Scope

## Included

- Definition of somatic genetic testing
- Somatic vs germline testing
- Gastric-cancer relevance
- Clinical indications
- Tumor specimen and adequacy
- Pre-analytic considerations at conceptual level
- Major genomic alteration categories
- Targeted testing
- Multigene panels
- Broader genomic profiling
- Tumor-only testing
- Possible germline implications
- Conceptual clinical actionability
- Resistance and molecular evolution
- Negative results and limitations
- Patient-facing explanation

---

## Explicitly Excluded

- Detailed NGS/WGS/WES/RNA sequencing methodology
- Detailed variant interpretation
- Somatic oncogenicity classification
- ACMG/ClinGen evidence criteria
- Detailed germline testing workflow
- Detailed HER2 testing
- Detailed MSI/MMR testing
- Detailed PD-L1 testing
- Detailed CLDN18.2 testing
- Liquid biopsy methodology
- ctDNA methodology
- Treatment algorithms
- Drug selection for individual mutations

These topics require independent Population Packages.
