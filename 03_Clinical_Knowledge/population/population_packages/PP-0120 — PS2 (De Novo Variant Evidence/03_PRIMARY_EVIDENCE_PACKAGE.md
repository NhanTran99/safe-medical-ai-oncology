# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PS2 (De Novo Variant Evidence)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PS2 (De Novo Variant Evidence)** as one of the standardized **ACMG/AMP Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes**.

This Population Package explains that **PS2 represents strong evidence supporting pathogenicity when a genetic variant is demonstrated to have arisen de novo (newly occurring in the affected individual rather than inherited from either biological parent under appropriate evaluation).** It emphasizes that PS2 is **one component of the ACMG evidence framework**, not a diagnosis or final variant classification.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PS2 is one standardized ACMG/AMP evidence code.
- PS2 represents strong evidence supporting pathogenicity.
- De novo variants arise newly rather than being inherited from either biological parent.
- PS2 is interpreted together with multiple evidence categories.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PS2** as one of the standardized **Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes** within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that PS2 may be applied when a variant is demonstrated to have arisen **de novo** under appropriate evaluation, because de novo occurrence is an important disease mechanism for many genetic disorders. ClinGen SVI recommendations further refine the technical application of PS2 while emphasizing that it represents **one component of structured evidence-based variant interpretation** rather than an independent diagnostic conclusion.
