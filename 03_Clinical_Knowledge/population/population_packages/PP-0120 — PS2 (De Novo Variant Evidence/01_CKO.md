# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Understand what PS2 is.
- Understand the basic meaning of a **de novo** genetic variant.
- Recognize that PS2 is one individual ACMG Evidence Code within the PS evidence-code family.
- Understand why a de novo variant may provide strong evidence supporting pathogenicity.
- Recognize that PS2 is one component of evidence rather than a final diagnosis.
- Feel more confident understanding genomic interpretation reports.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PS2?

## Patient Explanation

**PS2** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

It belongs to the **PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence)** family and represents one specific type of **strong scientific evidence**.

---

## Clinical Importance

PS2 contributes one category of strong evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PS2
- Strong evidence
- ACMG Evidence Code

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Does "De Novo" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

A **de novo** genetic variant is a genetic change that appears to have occurred **for the first time in an individual**, rather than being inherited from either biological parent.

Some genetic conditions may develop because of this type of newly occurring genetic change.

---

## Clinical Importance

De novo variants are an established mechanism for certain genetic diseases.

---

## Key Concepts

- De novo variant
- Newly occurring variant
- Genetic change

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Can a De Novo Variant Be Important?

## Patient Explanation

When a disease-associated genetic variant is found to have arisen **de novo**, this observation may provide **strong supporting evidence** that the variant is related to disease.

However, this is **only one part of the overall evaluation**.

---

## Clinical Importance

PS2 provides one category of evidence supporting pathogenicity under appropriate circumstances.

---

## Key Concepts

- Scientific evidence
- Pathogenicity
- Variant interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PS2 Mean the Variant Is Definitely Pathogenic?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PS2 is **not the final classification**.

Healthcare professionals always combine PS2 with many other types of scientific evidence before deciding whether a variant should be classified as pathogenic.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final classification depends on the complete body of available evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Final classification
- Professional interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Is PS2 Important?

## Patient Explanation

PS2 helps experts interpret genetic variants using internationally standardized scientific methods.

This improves consistency between laboratories and helps ensure that variant interpretation is based on structured evidence.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence codes improve reproducibility and transparency in clinical genomics.

---

## Key Concepts

- Standardization
- Consistency
- Clinical genomics

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

If a variant is de novo, it automatically causes disease.

**Fact**

A de novo variant may provide **strong supporting evidence**, but experts always evaluate it together with many other types of evidence before reaching a final conclusion.

---

# Key Messages

- PS2 is one ACMG Evidence Code.
- PS2 belongs to the PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence) family.
- A de novo variant is a newly occurring genetic variant.
- PS2 contributes one category of strong evidence but is not the final classification.
- Professional interpretation always combines multiple evidence sources.
