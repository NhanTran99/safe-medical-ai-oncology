**Reason:**

Patients discussing detailed genomic findings with healthcare professionals may encounter the term **PS2**. This Population Package explains, in plain language, how a **newly occurring (de novo)** genetic variant may contribute one category of strong evidence during professional variant interpretation while emphasizing that it is not a final diagnosis.
