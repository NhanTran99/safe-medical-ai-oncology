# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **PS2**, one of the most clinically important ACMG evidence codes, by explaining the concept of a **de novo genetic variant** in language appropriate for patients and caregivers. It distinguishes **newly occurring variants** from **inherited variants** while reinforcing that de novo occurrence represents **one category of strong evidence**, not a definitive diagnosis. The package deliberately defers advanced topics such as parentage confirmation, mosaicism, trio sequencing, and ClinGen implementation guidance, maintaining strict compliance with the **Atomic Knowledge Principle** and providing a clean bridge toward the remaining **PS evidence-code series (PS3 and PS4)**.
