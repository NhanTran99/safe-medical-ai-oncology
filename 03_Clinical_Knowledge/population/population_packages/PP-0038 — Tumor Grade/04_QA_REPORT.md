# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of tumor grade;
- purpose of tumor grading;
- explanation of well, moderately and poorly differentiated tumors;
- relationship between tumor grade and pathology reports;
- distinction between tumor grade and TNM staging;
- role of tumor grade within the overall pathological assessment.

Excluded:

- microscopic grading criteria;
- histologic differentiation mechanisms;
- WHO grading methodology;
- molecular classification;
- biomarker interpretation;
- prognostic implications;
- treatment recommendations.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
