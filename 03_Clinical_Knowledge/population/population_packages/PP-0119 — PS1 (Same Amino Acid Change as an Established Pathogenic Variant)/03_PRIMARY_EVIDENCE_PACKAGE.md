# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PS1 (Same Amino Acid Change as an Established Pathogenic Variant)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PS1 (Same Amino Acid Change as an Established Pathogenic Variant)** as one of the standardized **ACMG/AMP Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes**.

This Population Package explains that **PS1 provides strong supporting evidence when two different DNA variants produce the same amino acid (protein) change and that protein change has already been established as pathogenic.** It emphasizes that PS1 contributes one category of evidence within the ACMG/AMP framework and is never interpreted in isolation.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PS1 is one standardized ACMG/AMP evidence code.
- PS1 represents strong evidence supporting pathogenicity.
- Different DNA variants may sometimes produce the same protein alteration.
- PS1 is evaluated together with many other evidence categories.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional interpretation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PS1** as one of the standardized **Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes** within the ACMG/AMP framework. Guidance consistently explains that PS1 may be applied when a variant results in the **same amino acid substitution** as a previously established pathogenic variant, even when the underlying DNA sequence differs. ClinGen SVI recommendations further refine the technical implementation of PS1 while emphasizing that it represents **one component of structured evidence-based variant interpretation**, not an independent diagnostic conclusion.
