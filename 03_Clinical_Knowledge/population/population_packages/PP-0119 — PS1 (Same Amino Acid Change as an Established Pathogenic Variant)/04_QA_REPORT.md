# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces the **first individual PS evidence code** and establishes one of the most important educational concepts in clinical genomics: **clinical interpretation is driven by biological consequence rather than DNA sequence identity alone**. By explaining that different DNA variants can sometimes produce the same protein alteration, PP-0119 helps patients understand why previously established scientific evidence may inform interpretation of newly identified variants. Technical molecular genetics, codon biology, and ClinGen implementation remain appropriately deferred to future Population Packages, preserving strict compliance with the Atomic Knowledge Principle.
