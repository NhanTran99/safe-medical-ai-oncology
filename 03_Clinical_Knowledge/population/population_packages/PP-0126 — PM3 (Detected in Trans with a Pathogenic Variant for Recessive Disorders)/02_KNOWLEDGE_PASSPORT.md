**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PM3** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how the arrangement of variants on the two copies of a gene may contribute one category of moderate evidence during professional interpretation in appropriate recessive disorders.
