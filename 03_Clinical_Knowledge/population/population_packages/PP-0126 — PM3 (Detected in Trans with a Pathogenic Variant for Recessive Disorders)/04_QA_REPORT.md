# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **PM3**, one of the central **Moderate Pathogenic Evidence** codes within the ACMG/AMP framework, by explaining in patient-friendly language how the **relative position of two variants across the two copies of a gene** can contribute to variant interpretation in appropriate recessive disorders. The package intentionally simplifies the concept of **in trans** while avoiding technical discussions of inheritance mechanisms, phasing technologies, compound heterozygosity, segregation analysis, and ClinGen implementation guidance. This preserves strict compliance with the **Atomic Knowledge Principle** while providing a solid conceptual foundation for future Population Packages covering Mendelian inheritance and phasing.
