# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package explains another highly misunderstood radiology term encountered in CT reports. By emphasizing that **mass** is a descriptive imaging finding rather than a diagnosis, it reduces unnecessary anxiety and prepares patients for subsequent Population Packages covering **Nodule**, **Benign Tumors**, **Malignant Tumors**, **Biopsy**, and **imaging characterization of masses**.
