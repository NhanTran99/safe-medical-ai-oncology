**Reason:**

The word **mass** is one of the most anxiety-provoking terms patients encounter in CT reports. This Population Package explains its general meaning while clarifying that it is an imaging description rather than a diagnosis and that many masses are not cancerous.

## Typical Trigger Questions

- What does mass mean?
- Does a mass mean cancer?
- My CT report says mass. Should I worry?
- Can a mass be benign?
- What happens after a mass is found?
- Does every mass need a biopsy?

---

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

"Mass" is among the most emotionally significant radiology terms for patients. Providing an early explanation reduces unnecessary anxiety and establishes a foundation before discussing nodules, biopsies and tumor classification.
