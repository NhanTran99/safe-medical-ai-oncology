# Evidence Extraction Summary

## Diagnostic Workflow

- Clinical assessment.
- Medical history.
- Physical examination.
- Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy.
- Biopsy.
- Histopathological confirmation.
- Imaging evaluation.
- Multidisciplinary treatment planning.

---

## Core Diagnostic Tests

Primary diagnostic test

- Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy.

Definitive diagnostic test

- Histopathological examination of biopsy specimens.

Complementary investigations

- CT scan.
- Endoscopic ultrasound (selected patients).
- PET/CT (selected indications).
- Laboratory evaluation.

---

## Important Clinical Messages

Endoscopy detects suspicious lesions.

Biopsy confirms the diagnosis.

Imaging determines disease extent after pathological confirmation.

Treatment planning depends on both pathology and staging.
