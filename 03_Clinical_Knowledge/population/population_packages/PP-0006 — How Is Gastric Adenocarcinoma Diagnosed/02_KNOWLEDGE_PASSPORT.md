# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. Diagnosis begins with clinical evaluation.
2. Endoscopy allows direct visualization.
3. Biopsy confirms the diagnosis.
4. Imaging evaluates disease extent after diagnosis.
5. Treatment planning depends on accurate diagnosis.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Symptoms alone cannot diagnose gastric cancer.
- Endoscopy strongly suggests disease but biopsy establishes the diagnosis.
- Imaging complements, but does not replace, pathological confirmation.
- Final treatment decisions depend on complete diagnostic evaluation.
