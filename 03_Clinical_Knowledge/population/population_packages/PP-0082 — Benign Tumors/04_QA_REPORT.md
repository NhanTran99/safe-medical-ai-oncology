# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational concept that **"tumor" does not automatically mean "cancer."** It serves as the prerequisite for **PP-0083 (Malignant Tumors)** and **PP-0084 (Benign vs Malignant Tumors)**, creating a clear conceptual bridge from radiologic findings (lesion, mass, nodule) to fundamental tumor biology.
