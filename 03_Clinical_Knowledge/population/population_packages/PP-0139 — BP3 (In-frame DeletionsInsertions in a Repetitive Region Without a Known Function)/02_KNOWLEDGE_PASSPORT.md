**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **BP3** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how certain **in-frame insertions or deletions** occurring in **repetitive regions without a known function** may contribute **Supporting Benign Evidence**, while emphasizing that BP3 alone cannot establish benign classification.
