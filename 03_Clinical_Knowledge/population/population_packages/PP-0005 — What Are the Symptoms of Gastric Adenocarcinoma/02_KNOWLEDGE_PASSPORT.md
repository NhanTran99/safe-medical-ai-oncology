# Educational Intent

Primary Goal

Help patients recognize common symptoms of gastric adenocarcinoma.

Secondary Goals

- Explain that early disease may be asymptomatic.
- Differentiate common symptoms from diagnosis.
- Encourage timely medical evaluation without causing unnecessary fear.
- Prepare patients for the Diagnosis Population Package.

# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. Early gastric cancer may cause no symptoms.
2. Most symptoms are nonspecific.
3. Symptoms alone cannot diagnose gastric cancer.
4. Persistent or concerning symptoms require medical evaluation.
5. Earlier assessment may improve opportunities for diagnosis and treatment.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Symptoms do not equal cancer.
- Many benign gastrointestinal diseases produce similar symptoms.
- Persistent or worsening symptoms deserve medical assessment.
- Patients should not self-diagnose based on internet information.
