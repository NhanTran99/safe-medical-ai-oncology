# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Understand what PS4 is.
- Understand the basic concept of comparing how often a genetic variant occurs in people with and without a disease.
- Recognize that PS4 is one individual ACMG Evidence Code within the PS evidence-code family.
- Understand why population-level evidence may provide strong support for pathogenicity.
- Recognize that PS4 is one component of evidence rather than a final diagnosis.
- Feel more confident understanding how research involving many individuals contributes to genetic variant interpretation.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PS4?

## Patient Explanation

**PS4** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

It belongs to the **PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence)** family and represents one specific type of **strong scientific evidence**.

---

## Clinical Importance

PS4 contributes one category of strong evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PS4
- Strong evidence
- ACMG Evidence Code

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Does "Increased Prevalence in Affected Individuals" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

Researchers sometimes compare how often a genetic variant is found in **people with a disease** and **people without the disease**.

If the variant is found much more often in people with the disease, this finding may provide important scientific evidence.

---

## Clinical Importance

Population-level comparisons help researchers understand whether a variant may be associated with disease.

---

## Key Concepts

- Population studies
- Variant frequency
- Disease association

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Can This Provide Strong Evidence?

## Patient Explanation

When high-quality research consistently shows that a variant is more common in people with a particular disease, this information may strengthen the evidence that the variant contributes to that disease.

---

## Clinical Importance

Population evidence is one important category of scientific evidence used during variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Scientific evidence
- Population data
- Pathogenicity

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PS4 Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PS4 is **not the final classification**.

Healthcare professionals always combine PS4 with many other types of scientific evidence before determining whether a variant should be classified as pathogenic.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on the complete body of available evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Final classification
- Professional interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Is PS4 Important?

## Patient Explanation

PS4 allows experts to use information gathered from many people to help interpret genetic variants.

Using standardized evidence codes helps ensure that research findings are evaluated consistently across laboratories.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evaluation of population evidence improves consistency, transparency, and reproducibility in clinical genomics.

---

## Key Concepts

- Standardization
- Population evidence
- Clinical genomics

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

If a variant is more common in people with a disease, it automatically causes that disease.

**Fact**

A higher frequency in affected individuals may provide **strong supporting evidence**, but PS4 is always considered together with many other evidence categories before reaching a final classification.

---

# Key Messages

- PS4 is one ACMG Evidence Code.
- PS4 belongs to the PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence) family.
- PS4 uses evidence from studies involving many people.
- Higher prevalence in affected individuals may provide strong supporting evidence.
- PS4 contributes one category of evidence but is not the final variant classification.
