**Reason:**

Patients discussing detailed genomic findings or reviewing professional genetic reports may encounter **PS4**. This Population Package explains, in plain language, how research comparing variant frequency between affected and unaffected groups may contribute one category of strong evidence during professional variant interpretation while emphasizing that PS4 is not a final diagnosis.
