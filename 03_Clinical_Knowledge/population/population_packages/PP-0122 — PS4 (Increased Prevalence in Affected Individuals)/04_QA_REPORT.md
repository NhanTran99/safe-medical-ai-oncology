# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the introductory educational series covering the four principal **PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence)** codes within the ACMG/AMP framework. It introduces the concept of **population-based evidence**, explaining how comparing variant frequency between affected and unaffected groups can contribute strong evidence during variant interpretation. The package clearly distinguishes **statistical association** from **proof of causation**, reinforcing that PS4 represents one structured evidence category rather than a final diagnosis. Technical topics including epidemiologic study design, statistical methodology, population genetics, and ClinGen implementation remain appropriately deferred, maintaining full compliance with the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
