# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PS4 (Increased Prevalence in Affected Individuals)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PS4 (Increased Prevalence in Affected Individuals)** as one of the standardized **ACMG/AMP Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes**.

This Population Package explains that **PS4 represents strong evidence supporting pathogenicity when a genetic variant is found more frequently in appropriately studied individuals with a disease than in comparable individuals without that disease.** It emphasizes that PS4 represents **population-based scientific evidence** and is only one component of the overall ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PS4 is one standardized ACMG/AMP evidence code.
- Population-level comparisons may provide strong evidence supporting pathogenicity.
- Reliable scientific studies are necessary before PS4 can be applied.
- PS4 contributes one category of evidence within structured variant interpretation.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PS4** as one of the standardized **Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes** within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that PS4 may be applied when a genetic variant is observed significantly more often in appropriately evaluated affected individuals than in comparable unaffected individuals. ClinGen SVI recommendations further define the quality standards for population-based evidence while emphasizing that PS4 represents **one component of structured evidence-based variant interpretation** rather than an independent diagnostic conclusion.
