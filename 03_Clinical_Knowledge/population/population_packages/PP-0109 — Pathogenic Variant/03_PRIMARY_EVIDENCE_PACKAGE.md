# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is a Pathogenic Variant?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of a **Pathogenic Variant** as a genetic variant for which sufficient scientific evidence demonstrates a disease-causing or disease-contributing role.

This Population Package introduces the concept of a pathogenic variant as one standardized outcome of evidence-based variant classification while emphasizing that pathogenic classification does **not** by itself predict whether a person will develop disease, how severe the disease will be, or which treatment is appropriate.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Pathogenic is an evidence-based scientific classification.
- Strong scientific evidence supports a disease-causing role.
- Pathogenic does not mean disease is inevitable.
- Clinical interpretation requires integration with the patient's medical context.
- Scientific classifications may evolve when substantial new evidence becomes available.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently define a pathogenic variant as a genetic variant with sufficient evidence supporting a causal relationship with disease. Guidance consistently distinguishes pathogenic classification from disease prediction and emphasizes that interpretation must consider the individual's clinical presentation, family history when appropriate, and other relevant medical information. Current recommendations also recognize that variant classifications are subject to ongoing review as scientific knowledge advances.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- classification standards from ACMG and AMP;
- clinical genomics guidance from ClinGen;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP;
- oncology implementation guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- patient education principles from NCI and ACS;
- international consistency with ESMO.
