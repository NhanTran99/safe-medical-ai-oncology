# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the repository's **first standardized variant classification category node**. It deliberately separates the concept of **"pathogenic"** from the common patient misconception that disease is inevitable. By distinguishing **scientific classification**, **clinical interpretation**, and **disease prediction**, the package creates the educational foundation for the remaining ACMG classification categories—**Likely Pathogenic Variant**, **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)**, **Likely Benign Variant**, and **Benign Variant**—while maintaining strict adherence to the Atomic Knowledge Principle.
