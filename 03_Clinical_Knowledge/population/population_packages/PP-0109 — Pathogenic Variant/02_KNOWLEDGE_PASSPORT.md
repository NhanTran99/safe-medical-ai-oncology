**Reason:**

Patients may encounter the term **Pathogenic Variant** when reviewing genetic or genomic test reports. Understanding this standardized classification helps patients interpret report terminology while avoiding common misconceptions regarding disease certainty and treatment.

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

Pathogenic Variant is one of the core standardized classification categories used throughout clinical genomics and serves as the conceptual foundation for understanding adjacent categories such as Likely Pathogenic Variant and Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS).
