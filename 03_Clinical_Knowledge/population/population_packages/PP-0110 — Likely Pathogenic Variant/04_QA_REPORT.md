# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the repository's **second standardized ACMG variant classification node**. It clarifies that **Likely Pathogenic** represents a very high level of scientific confidence while remaining distinct from **Pathogenic** because the distinction reflects **strength of evidence rather than disease severity or clinical outcome**. The package prepares the educational transition to **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)**, which introduces the concept of insufficient evidence while preserving strict compliance with the Atomic Knowledge Principle.
