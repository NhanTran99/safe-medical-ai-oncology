**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic reports may encounter the term **Likely Pathogenic Variant**. This Population Package explains the scientific meaning of the classification while preventing the common misunderstanding that "likely" refers to disease severity or certainty of developing disease.

## Typical Trigger Questions

- What is a likely pathogenic variant?
- Is likely pathogenic different from pathogenic?
- Does likely pathogenic mean I will probably become ill?
- Can a likely pathogenic classification change?
- Why did my report use "likely pathogenic" instead of "pathogenic"?

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

Likely Pathogenic Variant is one of the five core standardized ACMG classification categories and serves as the conceptual bridge between **Pathogenic Variant** and **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)**.
