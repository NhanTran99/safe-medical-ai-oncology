## Clinical Scope

### Core
upper endoscopy as a gastric-cancer screening modality, purpose, conceptual detection capability, evidence from Asian screening studies, observational mortality evidence, major limitations, and screening-specific procedural risks

### Supporting
high-risk context, visible lesion concept, biopsy handoff, center experience, and relationship to false-negative screening

### Explicitly Excluded
detailed endoscopy preparation, sedation protocols, diagnostic endoscopy algorithms, biopsy mapping/technique, pathology, hereditary-specific surveillance protocols, individualized screening recommendation, universal intervals, and treatment

### Delegated-to PP
PP-0170, PP-0172, PP-0173, PP-0174, PP-0175, PP-0176, PP-0177, PP-0178, hereditary gastric-cancer surveillance packages, and downstream treatment
