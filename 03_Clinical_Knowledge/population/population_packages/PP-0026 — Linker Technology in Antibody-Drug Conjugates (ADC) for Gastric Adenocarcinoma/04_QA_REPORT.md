# Scope Verification

This Population Package intentionally focuses only on the **linker component of antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).**

The following topics are appropriately excluded and delegated to future Population Packages:

- Cleavable linker chemistry
- Non-cleavable linker chemistry
- Chemical bond structures
- Drug release mechanisms
- Intracellular cleavage
- Lysosomal degradation
- Payload pharmacology
- Bystander effect
- Resistance mechanisms
- Individual ADC medicines
- Clinical trial interpretation

This satisfies the Gold Population Package principle of **Atomic Knowledge (Single Educational Question).**
