# Intended Runtime Usage

## Primary Runtime Role

- Foundational educational response regarding the linker component of antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs).

## Secondary Runtime Role

- Prerequisite knowledge retrieval before Cleavable Linkers, Drug Release Mechanisms and Bystander Effect Population Packages.

## Typical Trigger Questions

- What is the linker in an ADC?
- Why does an ADC need a linker?
- What does the linker do?
- Does every ADC have a linker?
- Why is linker stability important?
- Is the linker the cancer medicine?
- How does the linker support targeted therapy?

## Retrieval Priority

High

**Reason**

The linker is one of the three essential structural components of every ADC and is necessary for understanding the overall design of targeted drug delivery.

## Included

- Purpose of the linker

## Explicitly Excluded

- Non-cleavable linker chemistry
- Chemical bond structures
- Drug release mechanisms
- Intracellular cleavage
- Lysosomal degradation
- Payload pharmacology
- Bystander effect
- Resistance mechanisms

- Clinical trial interpretation
