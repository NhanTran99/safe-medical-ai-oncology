# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of Progressive Disease;
- Progressive Disease as a standardized treatment response category;
- explanation that disease progression does not automatically determine prognosis;
- clarification that additional treatment options may still exist;
- importance of comprehensive reassessment.

Excluded:

- RECIST technical criteria;
- response algorithms;
- second-line treatment;
- prognosis;
- palliative care;
- end-of-life decision making.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the four foundational RECIST treatment response concepts (Complete Response, Partial Response, Stable Disease and Progressive Disease). Its primary educational objective is to prevent patients from incorrectly interpreting **Progressive Disease** as meaning that all treatment possibilities have ended, while reinforcing that subsequent management depends on individualized clinical reassessment.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- Response Assessment Algorithm
- Treatment After Progressive Disease
- Changing Cancer Treatment
- Cancer Recurrence

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
