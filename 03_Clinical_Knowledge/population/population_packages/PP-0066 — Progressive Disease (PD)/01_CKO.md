# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Explain what **Progressive Disease (PD)** means.
- Understand that Progressive Disease is one of the standardized RECIST response categories.
- Recognize that PD means the cancer has shown evidence of progression according to standardized assessment.
- Understand that Progressive Disease does not automatically mean there are no remaining treatment options.
- Understand that doctors usually reassess the overall clinical situation after Progressive Disease is identified.
- Understand that doctors interpret PD together with many other clinical findings.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is Progressive Disease (PD)?

## Patient Explanation

**Progressive Disease (PD)** is one of the standardized categories used to describe **how cancer responds to treatment**.

It means that, according to standardized assessment methods, **the cancer has shown evidence of progression**.

This may include cancer becoming larger or new cancer lesions being detected during evaluation.

---

## Clinical Importance

PD represents disease progression within standardized RECIST assessment.

---

## Key Concepts

- PD describes treatment response.
- Cancer has progressed according to standardized assessment.
- Progression may involve growth or new lesions.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — Does Progressive Disease Mean There Is No Hope?

## Patient Explanation

No.

A diagnosis of **Progressive Disease does not automatically mean that all treatment options have been exhausted**.

Many patients may still have additional treatments that can help control the cancer, relieve symptoms or improve quality of life.

---

## Clinical Importance

Disease progression often leads to reassessment rather than automatic discontinuation of cancer care.

---

## Key Concepts

- PD is not the end of treatment.
- Additional treatment options may still exist.
- Reassessment is an important next step.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — What Happens After Progressive Disease?

## Patient Explanation

If Progressive Disease is identified, your healthcare team will usually review your overall condition.

They may consider:

- your symptoms;
- imaging results;
- laboratory tests;
- previous treatments;
- your overall health;
- available treatment options.

The next step is individualized for each patient.

---

## Clinical Importance

Treatment decisions following PD depend on the complete clinical picture rather than RECIST classification alone.

---

## Key Concepts

- Comprehensive reassessment follows PD.
- Multiple clinical factors are considered.
- Treatment planning is individualized.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does Progressive Disease Mean the Cancer Is Getting Worse Very Quickly?

## Patient Explanation

Not necessarily.

Progressive Disease only indicates that the cancer has met standardized criteria for progression.

It does **not** describe how quickly the cancer is growing or predict exactly what will happen next.

---

## Clinical Importance

RECIST response categories describe treatment response at a specific assessment rather than the future course of disease.

---

## Key Concepts

- PD describes a standardized assessment.
- PD does not measure growth speed.
- PD does not determine future outcome by itself.

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Is Progressive Disease the Only Information Doctors Use?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Doctors also consider:

- your symptoms;
- physical examination;
- imaging studies;
- laboratory tests;
- pathology results;
- your overall health;
- your treatment goals.

Progressive Disease is **one part** of the overall clinical assessment.

---

## Clinical Importance

Clinical decisions are never based on RECIST response categories alone.

---

## Key Concepts

- PD is one assessment result.
- Multiple clinical factors are considered.
- Comprehensive evaluation remains essential.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Progressive Disease means there is nothing more doctors can do.

**Fact**

Progressive Disease means the cancer has progressed according to standardized assessment. Doctors usually reassess the overall situation to determine the most appropriate next treatment or supportive care plan.

---

# Key Messages

- Progressive Disease (PD) is a standardized treatment response category.
- PD indicates that cancer has progressed according to standardized assessment.
- PD does not automatically mean there are no remaining treatment options.
- Doctors usually reassess the complete clinical picture after PD.
- Continued medical care remains important after Progressive Disease.
