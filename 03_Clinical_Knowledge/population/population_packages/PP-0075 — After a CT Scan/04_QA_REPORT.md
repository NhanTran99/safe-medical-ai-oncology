# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the **patient CT journey** by explaining what typically happens after image acquisition. Together with **PP-0069 (CT Scan)**, **PP-0073 (Preparing for a CT Scan)** and **PP-0074 (What Happens During a CT Scan?)**, it provides a complete, patient-centered explanation of the CT experience while appropriately deferring result interpretation and clinical decision-making to subsequent Population Packages.
