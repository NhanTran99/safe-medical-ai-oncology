# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What should I expect after a CT scan?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **what typically happens after a CT examination**, including returning to normal activities, possible short observation after contrast-enhanced examinations, the radiologist review process, and when patients should contact their healthcare team after leaving the imaging department.

This Population Package introduces routine post-examination expectations without discussing CT report interpretation, treatment decisions, contrast reactions, or emergency management.

---

# Scope

## Included

- General expectations after CT
- Returning to normal activities
- Short observation after contrast when appropriate
- Following discharge instructions
- Radiologist review process
- When to contact the healthcare team

---

## Excluded

- CT report interpretation
- Exact timing of CT results
- Contrast allergy
- Contrast reaction management
- Radiation safety
- Treatment planning
- Abnormal CT findings
- Emergency management

These topics require dedicated Population Packages.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Most CT examinations end without restrictions on normal activities.
- Individual discharge instructions should always be followed.
- Brief observation may occur after some contrast-enhanced examinations.
- Radiologists review CT images before the treating clinician discusses the findings.
- Patients should seek medical advice if unexpected symptoms occur after leaving.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence is highly consistent regarding:

- routine discharge after CT examinations;
- individualized post-examination instructions;
- the radiologist's role in interpreting CT images;
- the importance of contacting healthcare professionals if concerns arise after discharge.

Differences between references primarily concern institution-specific observation practices and workflow logistics, which remain intentionally outside the scope of this Population Package.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- educational principles from NCI PDQ and ACS;
- imaging recommendations consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO;
- patient discharge guidance from ACR and RSNA.

---

# Evidence Gaps

This Population Package intentionally does **not** discuss:

- interpretation of CT reports;
- timing of CT results;
- management of contrast reactions;
- emergency symptoms requiring immediate intervention;
- radiation safety;
- treatment planning.

These subjects require independent Population Packages.
