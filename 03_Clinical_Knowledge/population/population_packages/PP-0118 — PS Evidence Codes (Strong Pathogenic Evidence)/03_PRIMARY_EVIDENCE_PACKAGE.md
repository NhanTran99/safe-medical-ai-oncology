# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What are PS Evidence Codes (Strong Pathogenic Evidence)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of the **PS Evidence Code family** as a group of standardized ACMG/AMP evidence codes representing **strong evidence supporting pathogenicity** during genetic variant interpretation.

This Population Package explains that PS Evidence Codes form one category within the ACMG/AMP evidence framework, that each individual PS code represents a different type of strong scientific evidence, and that all PS evidence must be interpreted together with other evidence before a final variant classification is assigned.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PS represents a family of standardized pathogenic evidence codes.
- Each PS code reflects a different category of strong scientific evidence.
- PS evidence contributes to the overall assessment rather than acting as a final diagnosis.
- Professional interpretation combines PS evidence with many other evidence categories.
- Standardization improves consistency, transparency, and communication in genomic medicine.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize the **PS evidence-code family** as one component of the ACMG/AMP evidence framework used during genetic variant interpretation. Guidance consistently explains that different PS codes represent different categories of strong pathogenic evidence and are integrated with additional evidence codes to support standardized, reproducible variant classification. Recommendations also emphasize that evidence codes are intended for professional genomic interpretation rather than independent patient use.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- evidence standards jointly developed by ACMG and AMP;
- implementation guidance from ClinGen SVI;
- molecular pathology standards from CAP;
- oncology implementation guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- patient education principles from NCI and ACS;
- international consistency with ESMO.
