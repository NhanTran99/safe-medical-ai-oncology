# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is a biopsy?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **biopsy** as a fundamental diagnostic procedure in cancer care.

This Population Package explains that a biopsy involves obtaining a **sample of tissue or cells for laboratory examination** to help establish or clarify a diagnosis. It also emphasizes that biopsy findings are interpreted together with clinical history, imaging, physical examination, and other investigations.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- A biopsy obtains tissue or cells for laboratory examination.
- Biopsy is one of the most important diagnostic tools in oncology.
- Not every abnormality requires biopsy.
- Biopsy findings are interpreted together with other clinical information.
- Biopsy helps establish diagnosis and supports treatment planning.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence consistently identifies biopsy as the diagnostic procedure that provides tissue or cellular material for pathological examination. Major oncology organizations emphasize that biopsy findings should always be interpreted within the broader clinical context rather than in isolation.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- educational principles from NCI and ACS;
- diagnostic principles consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.
