# Knowledge Classification

## Knowledge Type

Patient-facing clinical education / treatment literacy / targeted anti-angiogenic therapy.

## Atomic Clinical Question

> **What is anti-angiogenic therapy, how does it work against gastric cancer, when is it used, what benefit has been demonstrated, and what are its important limitations and safety considerations?**

## Primary Function

PP-0212 is the **anti-angiogenic treatment node** within the gastric-cancer targeted-therapy branch.

It explains:

- angiogenesis;
- VEGF/VEGFR signaling;
- VEGFR-2-directed therapy;
- ramucirumab;
- evidence-supported treatment settings;
- major clinical-trial evidence;
- subsequent-line treatment context;
- important safety considerations;
- patient-facing interpretation.

It does not own the broader targeted-therapy architecture, biomarker-testing methodology, detailed drug administration, detailed toxicity management or individualized treatment selection.

# Knowledge Units

## KU-01 — Anti-angiogenic Therapy Definition

Anti-angiogenic therapy interferes with biological signaling involved in new blood-vessel formation.

## KU-02 — Tumor Angiogenesis

Tumors can promote new blood-vessel formation to support growth and survival.

## KU-03 — VEGF/VEGFR

VEGF is an angiogenic signaling factor and VEGFRs are receptors through which angiogenic signals are mediated.

## KU-04 — VEGFR-2

VEGFR-2 is the principal therapeutic target of ramucirumab.

## KU-05 — Ramucirumab

A monoclonal antibody directed against VEGFR-2.

## KU-06 — Treatment Setting

The strongest established evidence is in previously treated advanced/metastatic gastric or EGJ adenocarcinoma.

## KU-07 — REGARD

Ramucirumab monotherapy improved median OS versus placebo in a randomized phase III study.

## KU-08 — RAINBOW

Ramucirumab plus paclitaxel improved OS, PFS and ORR compared with paclitaxel alone.

## KU-09 — RAINFALL

Routine first-line addition of ramucirumab to chemotherapy is not recommended based on the negative phase III evidence.

## KU-10 — Biomarker Distinction

Ramucirumab has a molecular target but the supplied current guideline does not establish a routine companion biomarker requirement analogous to HER2 or CLDN18.2 selection.

## KU-11 — Safety

Important risks include hypertension, bleeding, thrombosis, gastrointestinal perforation and impaired wound healing.

## KU-12 — Individualization

Treatment choice depends on prior treatment, performance status, disease setting, risks, goals and clinician judgment.

# Safety / Runtime Rules

1. Do not interpret an individual's treatment eligibility.
2. Do not provide individualized treatment recommendations.
3. Do not instruct the user to start, stop or modify ramucirumab.
4. Do not provide dosing or dose-modification instructions.
5. Do not convert trial outcomes into guaranteed individual benefit.
6. Do not imply that ramucirumab requires a positive VEGF/VEGFR biomarker unless supported by a future governing evidence update.
7. Do not replace clinician assessment with this package.
8. Preserve the distinction between targeted therapy and biomarker-selected therapy.
9. Preserve treatment-line context.
10. Preserve uncertainty and evidence limitations.
