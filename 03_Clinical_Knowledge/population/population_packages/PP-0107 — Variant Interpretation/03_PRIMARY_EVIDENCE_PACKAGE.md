# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is Variant Interpretation?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **Variant Interpretation** as the process of determining the **clinical significance of a genetic variant** after it has been identified through molecular testing.

This Population Package introduces variant interpretation as the essential bridge between detecting a genetic variant and understanding whether that variant has clinical meaning for diagnosis, prognosis, or treatment, while emphasizing that interpretation relies on evolving scientific evidence and expert review.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Variant interpretation follows variant detection.
- Scientific evidence is required before assigning clinical meaning.
- Many genetic variants have uncertain or evolving significance.
- Interpretation is an ongoing evidence-based process.
- Clinical decisions rely on interpreted results together with the patient's overall clinical context.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently describe variant interpretation as a structured, evidence-based process that integrates laboratory findings with scientific literature, clinical evidence, expert consensus, and validated genomic resources. Guidance consistently emphasizes that interpretation evolves over time as new evidence emerges and that laboratory findings alone should not be used in isolation for clinical decision-making.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- interpretation principles from ACMG and AMP;
- clinical genomics standards from ClinGen;
- molecular pathology guidance from CAP;
- oncology implementation guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- patient education principles from NCI and ACS;
- international consistency with ESMO.
