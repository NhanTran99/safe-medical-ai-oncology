# Intended Runtime Usage

## Primary Runtime Role

- Foundational educational response regarding MSI-H / dMMR testing.

## Secondary Runtime Role

- Prerequisite knowledge retrieval before molecular pathology, Lynch syndrome and immunotherapy-specific Population Packages.

## Typical Trigger Questions

- What is MSI-H?
- What is dMMR?
- Why do I need MSI testing?
- What does dMMR mean?
- When is MSI-H / dMMR testing performed?
- What do MSI-H or MSS results mean?
- Does MSI-H affect immunotherapy?
- How do MSI-H / dMMR results influence treatment?

## Retrieval Priority

High

**Reason**

MSI-H / dMMR testing is one of the major predictive biomarker assessments recommended in modern gastric cancer management and is directly connected with precision immunotherapy.
