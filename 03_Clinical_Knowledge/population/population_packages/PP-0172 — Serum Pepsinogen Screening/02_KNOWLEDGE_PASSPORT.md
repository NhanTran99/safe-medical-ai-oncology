## Clinical Scope

### Core
serum pepsinogen as a blood-based gastric-cancer risk/precursor marker, relationship with atrophic gastritis and intestinal-type cancer risk, PGI/PGII concept, study-specific cutoffs and performance, medication/eradication effects, evidence limitations, and patient-facing interpretation

### Supporting
atrophic gastritis context, endoscopic follow-up concept, high-risk population context, and false-result concepts

### Explicitly Excluded
universal cutoff algorithms, detailed laboratory methodology, H. pylori testing/eradication treatment, PPI management, diagnostic endoscopy, biopsy/pathology, individualized screening recommendation, and treatment

### Delegated-to PP
PP-0165, PP-0160, PP-0170, PP-0171, PP-0173, PP-0174, PP-0175, PP-0177, PP-0178, and downstream management
