# 2. Package Purpose

PP-0208 exists to answer one clinical educational question:

> **How does palliative systemic therapy work as a treatment strategy for advanced gastric cancer?**

The package connects:

**advanced disease**

→

**treatment intent**

→

**clinical assessment**

→

**biomarker information**

→

**first-line treatment**

→

**response/progression**

→

**subsequent treatment**

→

**supportive/palliative-care integration**.

It deliberately does not become a catalog of every individual systemic therapy.

# 5. Evidence Classification

## Established / Guideline-Supported

1. Palliative systemic therapy is used in unresectable, recurrent, and metastatic gastric cancer.
2. Systemic therapy can provide palliation, improved survival, and enhanced quality of life.
3. Biomarker information can influence systemic-treatment selection.
4. HER2 can influence targeted-treatment selection.
5. PD-L1 can influence selected immunotherapy-containing strategies.
6. CLDN18.2 can influence selected targeted-treatment strategies.
7. MSI-H/dMMR can influence immunotherapy options.
8. Two-drug cytotoxic regimens are generally preferred over more intensive triplets because of toxicity considerations.
9. Later-line treatment is influenced by prior therapy and performance status.
10. Best supportive care is a legitimate management pathway.
11. Palliative care may be integrated with active anticancer treatment.

## Context-Dependent

1. Exact first-line regimen.
2. Addition of immunotherapy.
3. Addition of targeted therapy.
4. Second-line regimen.
5. Third-line/subsequent regimen.
6. Treatment continuation after stable disease.
7. Treatment discontinuation.
8. Clinical-trial eligibility.
9. Transition toward supportive care.

## Evidence-Supported but Not Universal

1. Chemotherapy may prolong survival compared with supportive care in selected metastatic patients.
2. Ramucirumab-containing treatment has later-line evidence.
3. Trastuzumab deruxtecan has later-line evidence in HER2-positive disease.
4. CLDN18.2-directed treatment has first-line evidence.
5. Immunotherapy-containing regimens have evidence in selected biomarker-defined settings.

# 14. Safety and Interpretation Rules

1. Never convert PP-0208 into individualized treatment advice.
2. Never treat a biomarker as a treatment order.
3. Never imply that one systemic regimen is universally preferred for every patient.
4. Never imply that palliative treatment means end-of-life care.
5. Never imply that stopping anticancer therapy means stopping medical care.
6. Always consider treatment line and previous therapy.
7. Always consider performance status and toxicity.
8. Keep therapy-specific details delegated.
9. Preserve the distinction between palliative systemic therapy, palliative care, and best supportive care.
10. Use the most current governed evidence available in the project when the package is updated.

# 16. Evidence Gaps

- No universal palliative systemic-treatment sequence applies to all patients.
- Exact regimen selection requires patient-specific clinical information.
- Treatment duration varies by disease behavior, response, toxicity, and clinical context.
- Biomarker-defined options continue to evolve.
- Evidence for individual later-line strategies may come from selected patient populations and may not generalize to every patient.
- The project Source Materials do not support universal individualized treatment rules.

# 20. Conceptual Retrieval Map

# 21. Patient-Facing Learning Sequence

A patient encountering PP-0208 should be able to move through the following sequence:

1. **What does palliative mean?**
2. **Why is systemic treatment being considered?**
3. **What is the goal of treatment?**
4. **What information is used to choose treatment?**
5. **Why do biomarkers matter?**
6. **What is first-line treatment?**
7. **What happens if it works?**
8. **What happens if it stops working?**
9. **What are later-line options?**
10. **When might treatment be changed or stopped?**
11. **What is palliative care?**
12. **What is best supportive care?**
13. **What questions should I ask my care team?**

This sequence is intentionally educational rather than prescriptive.

# 24. Package Integrity Statement

PP-0208 is a **strategy-level treatment package**.

It is intentionally broader than any single drug package but narrower than the complete “advanced gastric cancer management” domain.

Its defining ownership is:

> **How palliative systemic treatment is selected and sequenced.**

Its boundary is not:

> **Everything that can ever be done for advanced gastric cancer.**
