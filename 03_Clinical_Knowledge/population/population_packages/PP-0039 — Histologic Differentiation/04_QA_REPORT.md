# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of histologic differentiation;
- purpose of histologic differentiation;
- explanation of well, moderately, poorly differentiated and undifferentiated terminology;
- relationship between differentiation and tumor grade;
- distinction from histologic type, Lauren Classification and TNM staging;
- role of differentiation within the overall pathological assessment.

Excluded:

- microscopic differentiation criteria;
- WHO grading methodology;
- tumor grade scoring systems;
- molecular classification;
- biomarker interpretation;
- prognostic implications;
- treatment recommendations.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
