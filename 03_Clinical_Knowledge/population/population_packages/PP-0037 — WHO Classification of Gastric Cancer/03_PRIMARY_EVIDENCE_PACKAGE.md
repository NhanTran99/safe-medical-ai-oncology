# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is the WHO Classification of Gastric Cancer and what should patients understand about it?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of the WHO Classification of Gastric Cancer, including its purpose, how it is determined from pathological examination, why it appears in pathology reports, and how it differs from other pathological classification systems.

This Population Package introduces the WHO Classification without discussing detailed histologic criteria, prognostic implications or treatment decisions.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- WHO Classification provides an internationally standardized method for describing gastric cancer histology.
- The classification is determined by pathological examination of biopsy or surgical tissue.
- Several WHO histologic subtypes may be identified in gastric adenocarcinoma.
- WHO Classification may appear in the pathology report.
- WHO Classification is different from Lauren Classification and from TNM staging.
- WHO Classification contributes to diagnosis but does not independently determine prognosis or treatment.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence is highly consistent regarding:

- the international role of the WHO Classification;
- pathological assignment after microscopic examination;
- relationship between WHO Classification and pathology reporting;
- distinction between WHO Classification, Lauren Classification and TNM staging;
- integration of WHO Classification into the overall diagnostic assessment.

Differences between references are limited to terminology, educational emphasis and explanatory depth.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- scientific concepts from NCI PDQ;
- patient-centered educational language from ACS;
- terminology consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.

# Evidence Gaps

This Population Package intentionally does **not** discuss:

- detailed WHO histologic diagnostic criteria;
- microscopic characteristics of each subtype;
- tumor grading;
- molecular classification;
- biomarker testing;
- prognostic implications of WHO subtypes;
- treatment recommendations.

These subjects require independent Population Packages.
