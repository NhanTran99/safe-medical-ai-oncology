# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of WHO Classification;
- purpose of the WHO Classification;
- international role of the WHO system;
- introduction to common WHO histologic subtypes;
- relationship between WHO Classification and pathology reports;
- distinction between WHO Classification, Lauren Classification and TNM staging.

Excluded:

- detailed histologic criteria;
- microscopic morphology of each subtype;
- tumor grading;
- histologic differentiation;
- molecular classification;
- biomarker interpretation;
- prognostic implications;
- treatment recommendations.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
