# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the **first interpretation-oriented educational layer** within the pathology sequence. Rather than teaching pathology itself, it teaches patients **how to understand the role of the Final Diagnosis section** within the overall pathology report. It provides the conceptual bridge from general report navigation (PP-0087) to future Population Packages covering **Tumor Grade**, **TNM Staging**, **Margin Status**, **Biomarker Results**, and other pathology-specific findings.
