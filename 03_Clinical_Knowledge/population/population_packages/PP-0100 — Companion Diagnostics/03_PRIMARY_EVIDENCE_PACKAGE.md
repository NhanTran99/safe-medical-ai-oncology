# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is a companion diagnostic?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **companion diagnostics** as laboratory tests that provide information essential for determining whether selected therapies are appropriate for an individual patient.

This Population Package explains the relationship between companion diagnostics, biomarker testing, molecular testing, and precision medicine while emphasizing that companion diagnostics are required only for specific therapies and clinical situations.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Companion diagnostics are linked to specific therapies.
- They provide information that supports treatment selection.
- Companion diagnostics represent a specialized category within biomarker testing.
- Only selected therapies require companion diagnostics.
- Companion diagnostics contribute to precision medicine alongside other clinical information.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The FDA provides the internationally recognized regulatory definition of companion diagnostics, while major oncology organizations consistently describe their role in biomarker-guided treatment selection. These organizations emphasize that companion diagnostics should be used according to validated clinical indications and interpreted together with pathology, imaging, staging, and the patient's overall clinical condition.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- regulatory terminology from the FDA;
- patient education principles from NCI and ACS;
- oncology guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP;
- international guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
