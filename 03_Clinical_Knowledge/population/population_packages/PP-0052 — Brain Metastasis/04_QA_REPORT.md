# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of the brain;
- definition of brain metastasis;
- distinction between brain metastasis and primary brain cancer;
- recognition that brain metastasis is possible but relatively uncommon;
- relationship between brain metastasis and metastatic gastric cancer;
- importance of comprehensive medical evaluation.

Excluded:

- primary brain tumors;
- neuroimaging;
- lumbar puncture;
- brain biopsy;
- neurosurgery;
- stereotactic radiosurgery;
- whole-brain radiation therapy;
- systemic treatment;
- prognosis.
