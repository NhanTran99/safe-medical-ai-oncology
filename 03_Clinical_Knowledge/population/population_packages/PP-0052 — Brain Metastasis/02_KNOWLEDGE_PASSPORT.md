**Reason:**

Brain metastasis is a clinically important but relatively uncommon form of distant metastatic disease in gastric cancer. Patients frequently have questions when brain imaging is discussed or neurological symptoms arise. This Population Package provides the conceptual foundation before introducing neuroimaging, neurosurgical treatment or radiation therapy.

# Clinical Scope

## Included

- Definition of the brain
- Definition of brain metastasis
- Relationship between gastric cancer and the brain
- Difference between primary brain cancer and brain metastasis
- Relationship between brain metastasis and metastatic gastric cancer
- Recognition that brain metastasis is possible but relatively uncommon
- Importance of appropriate medical evaluation

---

## Explicitly Excluded

- Primary brain tumors
- Glioblastoma
- Meningioma
- Brain MRI
- Brain CT
- Lumbar puncture
- Brain biopsy
- Neurosurgery
- Stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS)
- Whole-brain radiation therapy (WBRT)
- Systemic therapy
- M1 classification
- Prognostic statistics

These topics are intentionally delegated to independent Population Packages.
