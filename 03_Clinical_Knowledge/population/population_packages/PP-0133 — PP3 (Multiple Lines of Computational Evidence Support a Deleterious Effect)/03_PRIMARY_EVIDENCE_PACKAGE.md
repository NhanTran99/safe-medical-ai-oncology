# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PP3 (Multiple Lines of Computational Evidence Support a Deleterious Effect)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PP3 (Multiple Lines of Computational Evidence Support a Deleterious Effect)** as one of the standardized **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **PP3 provides supporting evidence when multiple computational prediction methods consistently suggest that a genetic variant is likely to have a harmful biological effect.** Computational predictions are valuable because they integrate biological knowledge and computational modeling, but they remain **supporting evidence only**. PP3 is never interpreted in isolation and must always be integrated with clinical findings, laboratory evidence, family history, and other ACMG evidence categories.

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PP3 is one individual ACMG evidence criterion.
- PP3 evaluates computational evidence.
- Multiple computational predictions may strengthen supporting evidence.
- Computational predictions alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PP3** as one of the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that multiple computational methods supporting a deleterious effect may contribute supporting evidence toward pathogenic classification while emphasizing that computational evidence alone is insufficient for definitive classification. ClinGen SVI provides additional recommendations for the use of computational evidence in variant interpretation, but these technical refinements intentionally remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
