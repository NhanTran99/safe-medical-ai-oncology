**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PP3** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how **multiple computational prediction methods** may contribute supporting evidence during evidence-based variant classification while emphasizing that computational predictions alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
