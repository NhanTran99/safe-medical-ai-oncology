# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PP3?

## Patient Explanation

**PP3** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

PP3 represents one type of **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence**.

It focuses on **computational evidence** that suggests a variant may have a harmful effect.

---

## Clinical Importance

PP3 provides supporting evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PP3
- Supporting pathogenic evidence
- ACMG Evidence Codes

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Is Computational Evidence?

## Patient Explanation

Computational evidence comes from specialized computer programs that estimate whether a genetic variant might affect how a gene or protein works.

These programs use scientific knowledge and biological information to make predictions.

---

## Clinical Importance

Computer-based prediction tools provide one source of scientific evidence during variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Computational evidence
- Computer prediction
- Gene function

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Is PP3 Important?

## Patient Explanation

A single computer prediction may not always be reliable.

When **multiple independent prediction methods** reach similar conclusions that a variant is likely to have a harmful effect, this may provide **supporting evidence** during variant interpretation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Agreement among multiple computational approaches increases confidence in supporting evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Multiple predictions
- Supporting evidence
- Variant interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PP3 Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PP3 **does not prove** that a variant causes disease.

Computer predictions provide only one type of supporting evidence and must always be considered together with clinical information, laboratory findings, family history, and other scientific evidence.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on integrating multiple evidence categories.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Professional interpretation
- Supporting evidence

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use PP3?

## Patient Explanation

Modern computer programs can analyze genetic variants much more quickly than people alone.

Using PP3 allows genetics professionals to include carefully evaluated computational information as one part of an evidence-based interpretation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized computational evidence improves consistency and reproducibility in clinical genomics.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

If a computer predicts that a variant is harmful, the variant definitely causes disease.

**Fact**

Computer predictions provide **supporting evidence only**.

The final interpretation always requires many additional types of scientific and clinical evidence.

---

# Key Messages

- PP3 is one Supporting Pathogenic Evidence criterion.
- PP3 uses computational evidence.
- Multiple prediction methods supporting the same conclusion strengthen supporting evidence.
- Computer predictions alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires integration of multiple evidence categories.
