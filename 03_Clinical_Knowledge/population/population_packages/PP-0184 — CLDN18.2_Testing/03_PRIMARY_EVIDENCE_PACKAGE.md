# 5. Primary Evidence Sources

## Primary Source 1 — NCCN Gastric Cancer v2.2026

**Source file:** `1. Gastric Cancer_v.2.2026_NCCN-3-109.pdf`

### Key source location

**Principles of Pathologic Review and Biomarker Testing (GAST-B), Table 4**

The source states that for patients with untreated inoperable locally advanced, recurrent, or metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma for whom zolbetuximab is being considered, CLDN18.2 assessment is performed on biopsy or surgical specimen by IHC.

The positivity criterion is:

- ≥75% viable tumor cells;
- moderate-to-strong membrane CLDN18.2 staining;
- 2+ or 3+ intensity.

The source defines negative as fewer than 75% viable tumor cells demonstrating moderate-to-strong membrane staining.

### Evidence role

**Highest-authority direct evidence for the testing definition and interpretation.**

---

## Primary Source 2 — NCCN Gastric Cancer v2.2026 systemic therapy discussion

**Source file:** `1. Gastric Cancer_v.2.2026_NCCN-3-109.pdf`

NCCN describes zolbetuximab as a monoclonal antibody targeting CLDN18.2 and identifies the CLDN18.2-positive, HER2-negative advanced gastric/EGJ population in which zolbetuximab-containing first-line therapy is recommended.

### Evidence role

Supports the clinical reason why CLDN18.2 testing matters.

---

## Primary Source 3 — NCI Gastric Cancer Treatment PDQ®

**Source file:** `16. Gastric Cancer Treatment (PDQ®) - NCI.pdf`

The NCI PDQ summarizes first-line treatment options for metastatic/inoperable/recurrent gastric cancer and includes zolbetuximab plus CAPOX or mFOLFOX6 for CLDN18.2-positive tumors.

It also summarizes evidence from randomized clinical trials.

### Evidence role

Independent high-authority support for treatment relevance.

# 9. Evidence Notes

## 9.1 Testing indication

The current NCCN v2.2026 GAST-B table is the controlling evidence for the PP.

The relevant population is not “all gastric-cancer patients.”

The current wording ties testing to:

- untreated;
- inoperable locally advanced, recurrent, or metastatic;
- gastric adenocarcinoma;
- zolbetuximab being considered.

This distinction must remain explicit.

---

## 9.2 Specimen

The NCCN table identifies:

> biopsy or surgical specimen.

The guideline narrative describes the IHC assay in FFPE tumor tissue.

Therefore the evidence package treats CLDN18.2 as a tissue biomarker.

---

## 9.3 Positivity criterion

The most important evidence statement is the current Table 4 definition:

> ≥75% viable tumor cells demonstrating moderate-to-strong membrane CLDN18.2 staining at 2+ or 3+ intensity.

This is a compound criterion.

The package must not reduce it to “high CLDN18.2 expression.”

---

## 9.4 Negative criterion

NCCN defines negative as:

> <75% viable tumor cells demonstrating moderate-to-strong membrane CLDN18.2 staining.

The educational interpretation is that the specimen does not meet the current clinical threshold.

The package does not claim that negative means biological absence of CLDN18.2.

---

## 9.5 Prevalence

NCCN reports approximately 24%–38% positivity among advanced gastric and EGJ adenocarcinomas.

This is not a universal prevalence because the guideline explicitly notes assay and definition variability.

The range is therefore contextual rather than a patient-specific probability.

---

## 9.6 Histology

The guideline describes expression in intestinal-type tumors and more frequent expression in diffuse subtype.

This is not sufficiently specific to replace testing.

---

## 9.7 Molecular-marker relationships

NCCN reports that CLDN18.2 positivity can be independent of established molecular subtypes including dMMR and HER2 status and of PD-L1.

Therefore CLDN18.2 testing is a distinct biomarker assessment.

---

## 9.8 EBV

Evidence for an association between CLDN18.2 and EBV is inconsistent.

The package classifies this as uncertain rather than actionable.

---

# 10. Treatment-Relevance Evidence

## SPOTLIGHT

NCCN v2.2026 summarizes the phase III SPOTLIGHT trial:

- 565 treatment-naïve patients;
- HER2-negative;
- CLDN18.2-positive;
- unresectable locally advanced or metastatic gastric/EGJ tumors;
- zolbetuximab + mFOLFOX6 versus placebo + mFOLFOX6.

Reported median PFS:

- 10.61 months with zolbetuximab;
- 8.67 months with placebo;
- HR 0.75.

Reported median OS:

- 18.23 months with zolbetuximab;
- 15.54 months with placebo;
- HR 0.75.

These results support the clinical importance of identifying the CLDN18.2-positive population.

---

## GLOW

NCCN v2.2026 summarizes the phase III GLOW trial:

- 507 untreated patients;
- HER2-negative;
- CLDN18.2-positive;
- unresectable locally advanced or metastatic gastric/EGJ adenocarcinoma;
- zolbetuximab + CAPOX versus placebo + CAPOX.

Reported median PFS:

- 8.21 months versus 6.8 months;
- HR 0.687.

Reported median OS:

- 14.39 months versus 12.16 months;
- HR 0.771.

Together, SPOTLIGHT and GLOW provide randomized evidence supporting the clinical relevance of CLDN18.2-directed treatment in the defined population.

# 11. Clinical Claims Summary

### Claim 1

CLDN18.2 is a tissue biomarker that can identify a clinically actionable subset of gastric adenocarcinoma.

### Claim 2

The current NCCN assessment is performed by IHC on tumor tissue.

### Claim 3

Biopsy or surgical specimens can be used.

### Claim 4

The current positivity threshold is ≥75% viable tumor cells with moderate-to-strong membranous staining at 2+ or 3+ intensity.

### Claim 5

Testing is particularly relevant in untreated patients with inoperable locally advanced, recurrent, or metastatic gastric adenocarcinoma when zolbetuximab is being considered.

### Claim 6

CLDN18.2 is distinct from HER2, PD-L1, and MSI/MMR.

### Claim 7

A positive result can make CLDN18.2-directed treatment relevant but does not independently determine an individualized treatment plan.

### Claim 8

Assay and definition variability affect reported prevalence.

### Claim 9

Histologic and molecular associations are contextual and should not replace direct testing.

# 13. Evidence Gaps

## Gap 1 — Assay variability

Reported prevalence varies by:

- assay;
- detection method;
- positivity definition.

### Handling

Do not present one universal prevalence estimate.

---

## Gap 2 — Tumor heterogeneity

Expression can vary across tumor areas.

### Handling

Explain sampling limitation without creating a detailed scoring algorithm.

---

## Gap 3 — EBV relationship

The relationship is inconsistent across studies.

### Handling

Classify as uncertain and non-actionable.

---

## Gap 4 — Prognostic interpretation

The current package does not establish CLDN18.2 as a standalone prognostic classifier.

### Handling

Do not use CLDN18.2 status to provide individualized prognosis.

---

## Gap 5 — Technical scoring detail

The current guideline provides the clinical positivity definition but the package does not reproduce a full pathology scoring manual.

### Handling

Delegate technical scoring to CLDN18.2 Scoring.
