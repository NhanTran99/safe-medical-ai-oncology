# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the foundational trilogy of oncology imaging modalities (**CT, MRI and PET/CT**). It introduces PET/CT as a hybrid imaging technique that combines anatomical and metabolic information while emphasizing that PET/CT is used to answer specific clinical questions rather than as a routine examination for every patient. The package establishes the conceptual foundation for future Population Packages on **FDG**, **radiotracers**, **PET preparation**, **PET safety**, and **understanding PET/CT reports**.
