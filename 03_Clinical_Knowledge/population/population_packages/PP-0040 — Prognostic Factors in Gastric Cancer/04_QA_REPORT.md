# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of prognostic factors;
- purpose of prognostic assessment;
- major categories of prognostic factors;
- explanation that prognosis depends on multiple factors;
- explanation that prognosis is an estimate rather than a certainty;
- explanation that prognosis may change as new information becomes available.

Excluded:

- survival statistics;
- five-year survival rates;
- Kaplan–Meier curves;
- hazard ratios;
- prognostic nomograms;
- AI prediction models;
- individualized survival prediction;
- biomarker-specific prognostic evidence.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
