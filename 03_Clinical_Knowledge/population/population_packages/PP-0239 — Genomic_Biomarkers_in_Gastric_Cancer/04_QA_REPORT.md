# Boundary Review

## PP-0238

PP-0238 explains the concept and strategy of molecular tumor profiling.

PP-0239 explains the clinically relevant genomic biomarker findings that profiling may identify.

**Boundary: PASS**

## PP-0237

PP-0237 covers ctDNA.

PP-0239 only explains ctDNA as a separate pathway for obtaining tumor-derived molecular information.

**Boundary: PASS**

## Hereditary Gastric Cancer

Germline cancer-predisposition genes and hereditary testing are kept separate from somatic tumor biomarkers.

**Boundary: PASS**

## Individual Biomarker Packages

Individual biomarkers are introduced at representative depth only.

**Boundary: PASS**

## NGS

NGS is treated as a testing method rather than as the definition of a genomic biomarker.

**Boundary: PASS**
