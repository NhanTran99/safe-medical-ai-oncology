# Clinical Scope

## Core Genomic Biomarkers

- HER2/ERBB2 amplification
- MSI/dMMR
- TMB-H
- NTRK gene fusions
- RET gene fusions
- BRAF V600E

## Emerging / Context-Dependent

- EBV status

## Comparator Biomarkers

Included only to clarify classification boundaries:

- PD-L1
- CLDN18.2
- HER2 protein expression
