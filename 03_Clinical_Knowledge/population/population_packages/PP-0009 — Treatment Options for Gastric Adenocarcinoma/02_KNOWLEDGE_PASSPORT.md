# Educational Intent

Primary Goal

Help patients understand the major treatment options available for gastric adenocarcinoma.

Secondary Goals

- Explain that treatment is individualized.
- Introduce the concept of multimodal therapy.
- Explain the role of the multidisciplinary team.
- Prepare patients for future Population Packages covering each treatment modality in detail.

# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. Treatment depends on cancer stage and individual patient factors.
2. Multiple treatment options are available.
3. Many patients receive a combination of treatments.
4. The multidisciplinary team develops individualized treatment plans.
5. Treatment goals differ depending on disease stage and clinical situation.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Treatment decisions should be made together with the healthcare team.
- Not every patient requires every treatment modality.
- Different patients with the same diagnosis may receive different treatments.
- Treatment plans may change as new clinical information becomes available.
