# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PP1 (Cosegregation with Disease in Multiple Affected Family Members)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PP1 (Cosegregation with Disease in Multiple Affected Family Members)** as one of the standardized **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **PP1 provides supporting evidence when a genetic variant cosegregates with disease in multiple affected family members.** Such family inheritance patterns may strengthen confidence that a variant is disease-related, but **PP1 alone is never sufficient to establish pathogenicity**. PP1 always contributes as **one component of the overall evidence assessment** and must be interpreted together with other clinical, genetic, and laboratory evidence.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PP1 is an individual ACMG evidence criterion.
- PP1 evaluates cosegregation within affected families.
- Family inheritance information contributes supporting evidence.
- PP1 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PP1** as one of the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that cosegregation of a variant with disease in multiple affected family members may contribute supporting evidence toward pathogenic classification while emphasizing that PP1 is interpreted together with other evidence categories. ClinGen SVI provides additional implementation guidance for applying PP1 at different evidence strengths, but these technical refinements remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
