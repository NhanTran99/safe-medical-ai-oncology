# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PP1?

## Patient Explanation

**PP1** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

PP1 stands for evidence based on **cosegregation with disease in multiple affected family members**.

It represents one type of **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence**.

---

## Clinical Importance

PP1 provides supporting evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PP1
- Supporting pathogenic evidence

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Does "Cosegregation" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

"Cosegregation" means that a genetic variant and a disease are observed together within several affected members of the same family.

If multiple relatives who have the disease also carry the same genetic variant, this pattern may support the possibility that the variant is related to the disease.

---

## Clinical Importance

Family inheritance patterns can provide supporting evidence when evaluating genetic variants.

---

## Key Concepts

- Cosegregation
- Family inheritance
- Affected family members

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Is PP1 Important?

## Patient Explanation

PP1 helps genetics professionals consider whether a variant follows the pattern of disease within a family.

This information becomes one part of the overall scientific assessment together with many other types of evidence.

---

## Clinical Importance

Professional variant interpretation integrates family information with multiple evidence categories.

## Key Concepts

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PP1 Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PP1 **does not prove** that a variant causes disease.

It provides supporting evidence that must always be interpreted together with many other scientific findings before a final classification is made.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on the complete body of available evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Supporting evidence
- Combined evidence
- Professional interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use PP1?

## Patient Explanation

Family inheritance patterns can provide valuable scientific information.

Using standardized evidence criteria such as PP1 helps genetics laboratories evaluate this information consistently and transparently.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence criteria improve consistency and reproducibility in clinical genomics.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

If several family members have the same variant, the variant definitely causes the disease.

**Fact**

Although this pattern may provide important supporting evidence, PP1 alone is never enough to determine that a variant is pathogenic.

Additional clinical and scientific evidence is always required.

---

# Key Messages

- PP1 is one Supporting Pathogenic Evidence criterion.
- PP1 is based on cosegregation with disease in affected family members.
- Family inheritance patterns can contribute valuable supporting evidence.
- PP1 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires integration of multiple evidence categories.
