**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports or discussing inherited cancer risk with genetics professionals may encounter **PP1** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how **cosegregation with disease in multiple affected family members** provides supporting evidence during evidence-based variant classification while emphasizing that PP1 alone is insufficient to establish pathogenicity.
