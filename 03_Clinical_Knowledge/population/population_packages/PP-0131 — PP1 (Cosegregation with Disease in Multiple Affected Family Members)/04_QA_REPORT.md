# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **PP1**, the first individual criterion within the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** category of the ACMG/AMP framework. It explains, in patient-friendly language, how **cosegregation of a genetic variant with disease in multiple affected family members may provide supporting evidence for pathogenicity**, while reinforcing that **PP1 alone never establishes pathogenicity**. Technical topics—including ClinGen PP1 strength upgrades, pedigree scoring, LOD score calculations, Bayesian interpretation, and statistical segregation analysis—are intentionally deferred to dedicated Population Packages, maintaining strict adherence to the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
