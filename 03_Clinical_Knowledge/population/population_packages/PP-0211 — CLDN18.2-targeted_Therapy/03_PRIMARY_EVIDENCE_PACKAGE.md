## Clinical Question

> **What does CLDN18.2-targeted therapy mean in gastric/EGJ adenocarcinoma, when is it clinically relevant, what evidence supports zolbetuximab-containing treatment, and what should patients understand about benefit, limitations, and safety?**

## Clinical-Educational Intent

Provide a source-grounded explanation of CLDN18.2-targeted treatment as a specialized precision-oncology modality. The package is positioned downstream of CLDN18.2 testing and general biomarker-directed treatment selection, and upstream of drug-specific and toxicity-specific packages. It is educational rather than an individualized prescribing tool.

## Detailed Evidence Appraisal

### CLDN18.2 target rationale

NCCN describes CLDN18.2 as a tight-junction transmembrane protein normally expressed in gastric epithelium. The guideline notes that changes in tumor cell adhesion and polarity can expose the normally less accessible protein, creating a therapeutic target.

This package uses that information only to explain why a biomarker can become a treatment target. Detailed CLDN18.2 biology remains delegated.

### Biomarker-treatment linkage

The treatment pathway is not simply 'positive test equals drug.' NCCN places CLDN18.2 testing in a defined disease context and links zolbetuximab consideration to untreated unresectable locally advanced, recurrent, or metastatic disease.

The guideline also specifies HER2-negative status for the preferred zolbetuximab-containing first-line pathway.

### SPOTLIGHT

SPOTLIGHT randomized 565 treatment-naive patients with HER2-negative, CLDN18.2-positive, unresectable locally advanced or metastatic gastric/EGJ tumors.

Zolbetuximab plus mFOLFOX6 produced median PFS of 10.61 months versus 8.67 months with placebo plus mFOLFOX6, HR 0.75.

Median OS was 18.23 months versus 15.54 months, HR 0.75.

The evidence supports a clinically meaningful benefit in the defined population, not a universal prediction for every CLDN18.2-positive patient.

### GLOW

GLOW randomized 507 untreated patients with unresectable locally advanced or metastatic HER2-negative, CLDN18.2-positive gastric/EGJ adenocarcinoma.

Zolbetuximab plus CAPOX produced median PFS of 8.21 months versus 6.80 months, HR 0.687.

Median OS was 14.39 months versus 12.16 months, HR 0.771.

The independent trial supports the reproducibility of benefit across a different fluoropyrimidine/platinum backbone.

### Safety

NCCN identifies nausea and vomiting as particularly prominent adverse events with zolbetuximab-containing treatment.

Grade ≥3 anemia and neutropenia occurred in GLOW; decreased appetite was also observed in SPOTLIGHT.

ACS adds gastrointestinal symptoms, fatigue, weight loss, sensory neuropathy, low blood counts, abnormal liver tests and electrolyte abnormalities.

These data justify explicit patient-facing safety education, but detailed toxicity management belongs elsewhere.

## Evidence Interpretation Framework

### Evidence question 1 — Is CLDN18.2 merely prognostic?

For PP-0211 the relevant question is treatment utility, not simply association with prognosis.

The supplied NCCN evidence links CLDN18.2 status to a specific targeted-treatment pathway and summarizes randomized trials of zolbetuximab.

Therefore the package classifies CLDN18.2 as treatment-relevant in the defined clinical setting.

It does not claim that CLDN18.2 positivity alone determines prognosis.

### Evidence question 2 — Is the treatment biomarker-specific?

Yes, at the treatment-selection level.

The intervention directly targets CLDN18.2.

The evidence is therefore different from a generic chemotherapy regimen that does not require CLDN18.2 positivity for its rationale.

### Evidence question 3 — Is the treatment agent established?

Within the supplied project evidence, zolbetuximab is the established CLDN18.2-targeted agent.

The NCI drug source lists zolbetuximab-clzb and Vyloy.

The NCCN guideline provides the clinical treatment recommendation.

### Evidence question 4 — Is benefit demonstrated in randomized evidence?

Yes.

Two global randomized phase III trials are summarized:

- SPOTLIGHT;
- GLOW.

Both compare zolbetuximab-containing therapy against chemotherapy plus placebo.

Both report PFS and OS benefit.

### Evidence question 5 — Can the benefit be generalized to every CLDN18.2-positive patient?

No.

The trials define their own populations.

The package therefore preserves:

- disease stage/setting;
- HER2 status;
- treatment line;
- treatment-naive status in the pivotal trials.

### Evidence question 6 — Can the evidence be generalized to every CLDN18.2 assay definition?

No.

Testing and positivity definitions are delegated to the dedicated CLDN18.2 testing/scoring packages.

The treatment package should not silently substitute one laboratory definition for another.

## What the Hazard Ratio Means at Educational Level

A hazard ratio below 1 in these trial comparisons indicates a lower relative hazard of the event being analyzed in the zolbetuximab group compared with the control group over the study period.

For PP-0211, the important patient-facing point is:

> The trials found better outcomes with zolbetuximab-containing treatment in the studied population.

The package does not require patients to interpret the HR mathematically.

## What the Median Means

A median is a population summary.

For example, a median OS of 18.23 months in SPOTLIGHT means that half of the patients in the relevant group had an observed survival time at or below that value and half had a value at or above it, subject to the trial's survival analysis.

It does not mean:

> “This treatment gives a patient exactly 18.23 months.”

That distinction is mandatory for safe patient education.

## Evidence Interpretation Safeguards

### Safeguard A — Do not confuse biomarker positivity with treatment necessity

The evidence supports a treatment option in a defined context.

It does not say:

> Every positive patient must receive it.

### Safeguard B — Do not confuse treatment relevance with treatment response

The biomarker identifies the population in which treatment benefit has been demonstrated.

It does not guarantee response.

### Safeguard C — Do not confuse first-line evidence with all-line evidence

SPOTLIGHT and GLOW were first-line/untreated populations.

The package does not generalize beyond that without evidence.

### Safeguard D — Do not confuse targeted therapy with monotherapy

The pivotal evidence is combination therapy.

### Safeguard E — Do not confuse guideline recommendation with individualized prescribing

Guidelines describe populations and options.

A treating clinician applies them to an individual.
