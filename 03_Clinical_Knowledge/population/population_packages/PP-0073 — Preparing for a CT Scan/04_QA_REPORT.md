# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational patient understanding of **preparation before CT examinations**. It emphasizes individualized preparation, adherence to healthcare instructions, communication of relevant medical history, and appropriate medication management while avoiding institution-specific protocols. It serves as the foundation for future Population Packages on **CT examination procedures**, **contrast safety**, **contrast allergy**, **kidney function**, and **post-CT care**.
