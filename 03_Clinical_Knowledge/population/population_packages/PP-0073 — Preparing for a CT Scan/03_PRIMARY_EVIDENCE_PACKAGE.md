# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **How should I prepare for a CT scan?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **general preparation before a CT scan**, including why preparation differs among examinations, why following the healthcare team's instructions is important, what information should be shared before the examination, and why medication changes should never be made without professional guidance.

This Population Package introduces general preparation principles without discussing CT protocols, contrast safety, examination procedures, or post-examination care.

---

# Scope

## Included

- General preparation principles
- Individualized preparation according to examination type
- Importance of following healthcare instructions
- Basic fasting guidance
- Health information to report before CT
- Medication guidance
- Preparation when contrast may be used

---

## Excluded

- CT scanning procedure
- CT contrast pharmacology
- Contrast allergy
- Kidney function assessment
- Radiation safety
- CT report interpretation
- Post-CT care
- PET/CT preparation
- MRI preparation

These topics require dedicated Population Packages.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- CT preparation is individualized.
- Following healthcare instructions helps ensure a successful examination.
- Fasting requirements depend on the specific CT examination.
- Important medical information should be shared before the examination.
- Medication changes should only occur under professional guidance.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence is highly consistent regarding:

- individualized preparation before CT examinations;
- patient communication before imaging;
- appropriate preparation for possible contrast administration;
- following institution-specific instructions.

Differences between references primarily concern local imaging protocols and preparation requirements for specific examinations, which remain intentionally outside the scope of this Population Package.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- educational principles from NCI PDQ and ACS;
- imaging recommendations consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO;
- radiology preparation guidance from ACR and RSNA.

---

# Evidence Gaps

This Population Package intentionally does **not** discuss:

- CT examination procedures;
- contrast allergy;
- kidney assessment;
- contrast administration;
- CT radiation;
- post-examination care;
- interpretation of CT results.

These subjects require independent Population Packages.
