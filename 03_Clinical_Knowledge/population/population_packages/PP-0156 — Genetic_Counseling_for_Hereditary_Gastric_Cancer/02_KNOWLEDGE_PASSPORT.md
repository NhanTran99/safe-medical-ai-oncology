# Runtime Roles

## Primary

- Explain genetic counseling.
- Prepare patients for hereditary genetic testing.
- Explain possible benefits and limitations.
- Support informed decision-making.
- Explain possible test results.
- Support posttest understanding.
- Explain psychosocial and family implications.

## Secondary

- Explain VUS uncertainty.
- Explain why negative results may not eliminate hereditary concern.
- Prepare patients for family communication.
- Explain privacy/confidentiality considerations.
- Route patients to appropriate downstream packages.
