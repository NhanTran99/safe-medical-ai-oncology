# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is Variant Classification?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **Variant Classification** as the process of assigning a genetic variant to a **standardized clinical category** after evidence-based interpretation.

This Population Package introduces variant classification as the standardized reporting step that follows variant interpretation. It explains why standardized classification is necessary for consistent communication among laboratories and healthcare professionals while emphasizing that classifications are based on current scientific evidence and may change over time.

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major professional organizations consistently describe variant classification as the standardized outcome of evidence-based variant interpretation. Standardized classification systems improve consistency across laboratories, facilitate communication among clinicians, and support reproducible genomic reporting. Guidance also consistently emphasizes that classifications should be periodically reviewed as new scientific evidence emerges.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- classification standards from ACMG and AMP;
- clinical genomics guidance from ClinGen;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP;
- oncology implementation guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- patient education principles from NCI and ACS;
- international consistency with ESMO.
