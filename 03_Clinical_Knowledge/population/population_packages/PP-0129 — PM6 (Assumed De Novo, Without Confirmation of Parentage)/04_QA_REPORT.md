# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **PM6**, the final individual evidence code within the **PM (Moderate Pathogenic Evidence)** family, by explaining how an **assumed de novo** observation contributes to professional variant interpretation when **formal confirmation of parentage is unavailable**. The package clearly distinguishes between **assumed** and **confirmed** de novo evidence, helping patients understand why evidence strength depends on the completeness of supporting information. Advanced concepts—including de novo mutation biology, parentage testing, trio sequencing, mosaicism, inheritance mechanisms, and ClinGen implementation guidance—are intentionally deferred to dedicated Population Packages, preserving strict compliance with the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
