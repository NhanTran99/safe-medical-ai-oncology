**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PM6** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how an **assumed de novo** variant without formal confirmation of parentage contributes one category of moderate evidence during professional interpretation while emphasizing that this observation alone is insufficient to establish pathogenicity.
