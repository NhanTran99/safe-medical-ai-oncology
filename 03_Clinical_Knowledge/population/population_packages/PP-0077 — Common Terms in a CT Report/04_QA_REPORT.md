# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package serves as the **gateway to radiology literacy** within the imaging education pathway. Rather than teaching patients to interpret radiology findings independently, it builds foundational understanding of commonly encountered terminology while reinforcing that imaging language must always be interpreted within the broader clinical context. It establishes the educational foundation for future Population Packages explaining individual radiology terms such as **Lesion**, **Mass**, **Nodule**, and **Incidental Finding**.
