# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the **foundational targeted genomic testing node** within the repository. It resolves one of the most common patient misconceptions by clearly distinguishing **gene panels (the predefined group of genes being analyzed)** from **Next-Generation Sequencing (the technology used to analyze them)**. Together with PP-0097 through PP-0101, it completes the educational progression from biomarker concepts to practical genomic testing strategies before introducing broader sequencing approaches such as **Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)** and **Whole Exome Sequencing (WES)**.
