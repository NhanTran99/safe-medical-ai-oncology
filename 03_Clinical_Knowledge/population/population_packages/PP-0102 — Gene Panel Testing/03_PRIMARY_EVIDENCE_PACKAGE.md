# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is gene panel testing?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **gene panel testing** as molecular testing that evaluates a predefined group of clinically relevant genes.

This Population Package explains the relationship between gene panels, molecular testing, Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS), and precision medicine while emphasizing that different gene panels are designed for different clinical purposes and are selected according to the patient's clinical situation.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Gene panel testing evaluates selected clinically relevant genes.
- Gene panels and NGS represent different concepts.
- Different cancers may require different gene panels.
- Gene panel testing supports biomarker testing and precision medicine.
- Gene panel testing should be performed according to clinical indications.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently describe gene panel testing as molecular analysis focused on predefined groups of clinically relevant genes. They distinguish the **testing target** (the selected genes included in a panel) from the **testing technology** used to analyze those genes. Clinical guidance consistently recommends selecting gene panels according to the cancer type, clinical question, and current evidence-based practice guidelines.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- terminology from NCI;
- oncology guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP and AMP;
- patient education principles from ACS;
- international guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
