# Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

Clinical genomics is the umbrella clinical-use layer connecting molecular testing, genomic findings, pathology, variant interpretation/classification, germline and somatic testing, and precision oncology.

It is therefore a central hub in the genomic knowledge graph.
