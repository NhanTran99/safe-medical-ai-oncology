# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of the TNM staging system;
- purpose of TNM staging;
- general meaning of T, N and M;
- role of TNM in determining overall cancer stage;
- contribution of TNM to treatment planning and prognostic assessment;
- explanation that TNM is only one part of comprehensive clinical evaluation.

Excluded:

- detailed T categories;
- detailed N categories;
- detailed M categories;
- AJCC Stage Groups;
- clinical versus pathological staging;
- restaging;
- stage-specific survival statistics;
- stage-specific treatment recommendations.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
