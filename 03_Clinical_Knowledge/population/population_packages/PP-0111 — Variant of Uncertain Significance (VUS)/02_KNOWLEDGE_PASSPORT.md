**Reason:**

Patients frequently encounter the term **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)** in genomic reports. This Population Package provides a foundational explanation of scientific uncertainty and helps prevent misinterpretation of VUS results as either disease-causing or harmless.

## Typical Trigger Questions

- What is a VUS?
- Does a VUS mean I have cancer?
- Is a VUS dangerous?
- Can a VUS change in the future?
- Why couldn't the laboratory determine whether the variant is harmful?

---

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

VUS is one of the most commonly misunderstood genomic classifications and is essential for patient education regarding uncertainty, evidence-based medicine, and future variant reclassification.
