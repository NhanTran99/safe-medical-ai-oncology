# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is a Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of a **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)** as a genetic variant for which **current scientific evidence is insufficient** to determine whether it is disease-causing or benign.

This Population Package explains that a VUS represents **scientific uncertainty rather than laboratory error**, emphasizes that a VUS should not be interpreted as either pathogenic or harmless, and reinforces that clinical decisions require consideration of the patient's complete clinical context.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- A VUS reflects insufficient current evidence.
- A VUS is neither proven pathogenic nor proven benign.
- Scientific uncertainty does not indicate testing error.
- Variant classifications may evolve as scientific knowledge advances.
- Clinical interpretation requires integration with the patient's overall medical context.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently define a Variant of Uncertain Significance as a classification used when available evidence is insufficient to determine whether a genetic variant contributes to disease. Guidance consistently emphasizes that VUS findings should not be interpreted as either positive or negative results and that clinical management should not rely solely on a VUS. Recommendations also recognize that continued research and accumulation of evidence may lead to future reclassification.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- classification standards from ACMG and AMP;
- clinical genomics guidance from ClinGen;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP;
- oncology implementation guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- patient education principles from NCI and ACS;
- international consistency with ESMO.
