The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

- definition of Complete Response;
- Complete Response as a standardized treatment response category;
- distinction between Complete Response and cure;
- importance of continued follow-up;
- integration into comprehensive clinical assessment.

Excluded:

- RECIST technical criteria;
- response algorithms;
- Partial Response (PR);
- Stable Disease (SD);
- Progressive Disease (PD);
- recurrence risk;
- post-treatment management;
- prognosis.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational understanding of **Complete Response**, with particular emphasis on preventing the common misconception that an excellent treatment response is equivalent to permanent cure.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- Partial Response (PR)
- Stable Disease (SD)
- Progressive Disease (PD)
- RECIST Response Algorithm
- Follow-up After Treatment
- Cancer Recurrence

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
