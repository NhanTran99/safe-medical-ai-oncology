# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is cancer?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of what cancer is before introducing cancer-specific diseases such as gastric cancer.

This package establishes the scientific foundation for all subsequent Population Packages.

---

# Scope

## Included

- Definition of cancer
- Normal cell versus cancer cell
- Basic carcinogenesis
- Benign versus malignant tumors
- Basic concept of metastasis
- Inherited versus acquired cancer
- General prognosis concepts

---

## Excluded

- Gastric cancer
- Organ-specific cancers
- Diagnosis
- Screening
- Prevention
- Staging
- Biomarkers
- Treatment
- Surgery
- Chemotherapy
- Radiotherapy
- Targeted therapy
- Immunotherapy

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Cancer originates from abnormal body cells.
- Cancer develops gradually.
- Cancer differs fundamentally from normal tissue.
- Benign and malignant tumors are biologically different.
- Metastasis is a defining characteristic of malignant disease.
- Most cancers are sporadic rather than inherited.
- Modern cancer treatment has substantially improved outcomes.
