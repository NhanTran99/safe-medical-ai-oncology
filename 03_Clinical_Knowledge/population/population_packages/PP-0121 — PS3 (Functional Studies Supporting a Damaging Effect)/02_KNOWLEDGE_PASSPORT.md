**Reason:**

Patients reviewing detailed genomic reports or discussing genetic findings with specialists may encounter **PS3**. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how carefully performed functional studies may contribute one category of strong evidence during professional variant interpretation while emphasizing that PS3 is not a final diagnosis.
