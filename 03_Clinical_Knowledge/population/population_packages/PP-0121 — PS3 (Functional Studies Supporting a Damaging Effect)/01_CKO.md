# Educational Objectives

After reading this Population Package, the reader should be able to:

- Understand what PS3 is.
- Understand the basic concept of functional studies.
- Recognize that PS3 is one individual ACMG Evidence Code within the PS evidence-code family.
- Understand why functional studies may provide strong evidence supporting pathogenicity.
- Recognize that PS3 is one component of evidence rather than a final diagnosis.
- Feel more confident understanding how scientific research contributes to genetic variant interpretation.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PS3?

## Patient Explanation

**PS3** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

It belongs to the **PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence)** family and represents one specific type of **strong scientific evidence**.

---

## Clinical Importance

PS3 contributes one category of strong evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PS3
- Strong evidence
- ACMG Evidence Code

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Are Functional Studies?

## Patient Explanation

**Functional studies** are scientific experiments designed to understand **how a gene or protein works**.

Researchers may use these studies to determine whether a genetic variant changes the normal function of a gene or protein.

---

## Clinical Importance

Functional studies provide experimental evidence about the biological effects of genetic variants.

---

## Key Concepts

- Functional study
- Gene function
- Protein function

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Can Functional Studies Be Important?

## Patient Explanation

If well-designed scientific studies show that a genetic variant disrupts the normal function of a gene or protein, those findings may provide **strong evidence** supporting the possibility that the variant contributes to disease.

---

## Clinical Importance

Reliable functional evidence may strengthen professional assessment of pathogenicity.

---

## Key Concepts

- Scientific evidence
- Biological function
- Pathogenicity

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PS3 Mean the Variant Is Definitely Pathogenic?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PS3 is **not the final classification**.

Healthcare professionals always combine PS3 with many other types of scientific evidence before determining whether a variant should be classified as pathogenic.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on the complete body of available evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Final classification
- Professional interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Is PS3 Important?

## Patient Explanation

PS3 helps experts use carefully performed scientific research when interpreting genetic variants.

Using standardized evidence codes helps ensure that laboratories evaluate scientific findings consistently.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized use of functional evidence improves consistency, transparency, and reproducibility in clinical genomics.

---

## Key Concepts

- Standardization
- Functional evidence
- Clinical genomics

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Any laboratory experiment automatically proves that a genetic variant causes disease.

**Fact**

Only **well-established, carefully evaluated functional studies** may provide strong evidence under the ACMG framework, and PS3 is always interpreted together with many other evidence categories.

---

# Key Messages

- PS3 is one ACMG Evidence Code.
- PS3 belongs to the PS (Strong Pathogenic Evidence) family.
- Functional studies evaluate how genes or proteins work.
- High-quality functional studies may provide strong supporting evidence.
- PS3 contributes one category of evidence but is not the final variant classification.
