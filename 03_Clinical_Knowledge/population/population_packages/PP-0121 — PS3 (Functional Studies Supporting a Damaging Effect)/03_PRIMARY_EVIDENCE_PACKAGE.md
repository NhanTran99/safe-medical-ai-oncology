# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PS3 (Functional Studies Supporting a Damaging Effect)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PS3 (Functional Studies Supporting a Damaging Effect)** as one of the standardized **ACMG/AMP Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes**.

This Population Package explains that **PS3 represents strong evidence supporting pathogenicity when well-established functional studies demonstrate that a genetic variant has a damaging effect on normal gene or protein function.** It emphasizes that PS3 is **one component of the ACMG evidence framework**, not a diagnosis or final variant classification.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PS3 is one standardized ACMG/AMP evidence code.
- Functional studies may provide strong evidence regarding the biological effects of a variant.
- Only high-quality, well-established functional evidence is appropriate for PS3.
- PS3 contributes one category of evidence within structured variant interpretation.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PS3** as one of the standardized **Strong Pathogenic Evidence Codes** within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that PS3 may be applied when **well-established functional studies** demonstrate that a genetic variant disrupts normal gene or protein function. ClinGen SVI recommendations further define the quality standards required for functional evidence while emphasizing that PS3 represents **one component of structured evidence-based variant interpretation** rather than an independent diagnostic conclusion.
