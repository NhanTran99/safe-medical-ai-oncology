## Intended Runtime Usage

Use this PP when the user asks:

- What is endoscopic surveillance for HDGC?
- Why do I need repeated endoscopy if I have no symptoms?
- Why are random biopsies needed?
- What is the difference between targeted and random biopsies?
- How often is HDGC surveillance performed?
- What does a negative endoscopy mean?
- What happens if signet-ring cells are found?
- Is surveillance equivalent to prophylactic gastrectomy?
- Can HDGC-like families undergo surveillance?

Do not use this PP as a substitute for:

- individualized surveillance scheduling;
- genetic counseling;
- exact testing criteria;
- pathology interpretation;
- individualized RRTG decision.
