# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the **foundational radiology terminology trilogy** of **Lesion → Mass → Nodule**. It emphasizes that a **nodule** is a descriptive imaging finding rather than a diagnosis and corrects two common misconceptions simultaneously: **"nodule = cancer"** and **"small means harmless."** This provides the conceptual foundation for future Population Packages comparing these three terms and introducing organ-specific nodule evaluation pathways.
