# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of biopsy;
- why biopsy is performed;
- relationship between upper endoscopy and biopsy;
- why pathology examination is necessary;
- why biopsy results require time;
- patient expectations while awaiting results.

Excluded:

- biopsy techniques;
- pathology interpretation;
- biomarker testing;
- molecular pathology;
- cancer staging;
- treatment planning.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
