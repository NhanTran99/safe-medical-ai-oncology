# Knowledge Block 1 — What Are BP Evidence Codes?

## Patient Explanation

**BP Evidence Codes** are one group of standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant is likely to be **benign (not disease-causing).**

BP stands for **Supporting Benign Evidence**.

Each BP code represents one type of supporting evidence that may indicate a variant is unlikely to cause disease.

---

## Clinical Importance

BP evidence contributes supporting information during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- BP Evidence Codes
- Supporting benign evidence
- ACMG Evidence Codes

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Does "Supporting Benign Evidence" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

Supporting benign evidence means there is **some scientific evidence** suggesting that a genetic variant is probably **not harmful**.

However, this level of evidence is **not strong enough by itself** to determine that the variant is benign.

---

## Clinical Importance

Supporting benign evidence represents one level within the benign evidence hierarchy.

---

## Key Concepts

- Supporting evidence
- Benign variant
- Evidence strength

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Are BP Evidence Codes Important?

## Patient Explanation

Different types of scientific information can suggest that a variant is unlikely to cause disease.

BP evidence codes allow genetics professionals to evaluate these different observations in a consistent and standardized way.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence improves consistency in clinical variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Standardized evaluation
- Benign evidence
- Variant interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Do BP Evidence Codes Mean the Variant Is Definitely Benign?

## Patient Explanation

No.

A single BP evidence code **does not prove** that a variant is benign.

Genetics professionals always evaluate BP evidence together with many other types of scientific evidence before making a final classification.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on integrating multiple evidence categories.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Professional interpretation
- Benign classification

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use BP Evidence Codes?

## Patient Explanation

Different scientific observations provide different amounts of information.

Using standardized BP evidence codes helps genetics professionals evaluate benign evidence consistently and transparently across laboratories.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence frameworks improve reproducibility and quality in genomic medicine.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

If one BP evidence code is present, the variant is definitely benign.

**Fact**

BP evidence provides **supporting evidence only**.

The final classification always depends on integrating multiple evidence categories using professional guidelines.

---

# Key Messages

- BP Evidence Codes represent Supporting Benign Evidence.
- Each BP code provides one type of supporting benign evidence.
- Supporting benign evidence alone does not establish benign classification.
- BP evidence is interpreted together with other benign evidence categories.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive evidence integration.
