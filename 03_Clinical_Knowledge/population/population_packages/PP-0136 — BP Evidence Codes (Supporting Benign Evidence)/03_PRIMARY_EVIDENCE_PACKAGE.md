# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What are BP Evidence Codes (Supporting Benign Evidence)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **BP Evidence Codes (Supporting Benign Evidence)** as one of the standardized **Benign Evidence** categories within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **BP Evidence Codes provide supporting evidence that a genetic variant is likely to be benign (not disease-causing).** Each BP criterion represents one type of supporting benign evidence that contributes to evidence-based variant interpretation. Although BP evidence is valuable, **supporting benign evidence alone is never sufficient to classify a variant as benign**. BP evidence must always be interpreted together with stronger benign evidence categories and all other relevant scientific information.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- BP Evidence Codes are standardized benign evidence criteria.
- BP codes provide supporting evidence for benign classification.
- Supporting benign evidence contributes to evidence-based interpretation.
- BP evidence alone cannot establish benign classification.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **BP Evidence Codes** as the **Supporting Benign Evidence** category within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that BP evidence contributes supporting information suggesting that a variant is likely benign while emphasizing that supporting evidence alone is insufficient for definitive benign classification. ClinGen SVI provides additional implementation recommendations for individual BP criteria, but these technical refinements intentionally remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
