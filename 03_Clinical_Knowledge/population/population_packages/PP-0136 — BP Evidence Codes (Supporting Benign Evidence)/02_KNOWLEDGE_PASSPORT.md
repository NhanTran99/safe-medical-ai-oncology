**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **BP Evidence Codes** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how **Supporting Benign Evidence** contributes to evidence-based classification while emphasizing that BP evidence alone is insufficient to classify a variant as benign.
