# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the repository's **patient-facing educational coverage of the five standardized ACMG variant classification categories**. It establishes **Benign Variant** as the highest-confidence non-disease-causing classification while clarifying that the designation applies to an **individual genetic variant**, not to an entire gene or to every aspect of biology. Together with **Pathogenic**, **Likely Pathogenic**, **Variant of Uncertain Significance (VUS)**, and **Likely Benign**, this package completes the foundational ACMG classification sequence and prepares the repository for the next stage: **Variant Reclassification**, which explains how classifications can evolve as scientific evidence accumulates.
