# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What are PP Evidence Codes (Supporting Pathogenic Evidence)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PP Evidence Codes (Supporting Pathogenic Evidence)** as one of the standardized evidence-strength categories within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **PP Evidence Codes represent supporting-level evidence that may contribute to the conclusion that a genetic variant is pathogenic.** Although supporting evidence is weaker than **Very Strong (PVS)**, **Strong (PS)**, and **Moderate (PM)** evidence, it remains an important part of structured variant interpretation. PP Evidence Codes always contribute as **one component of the overall evidence assessment** and are never interpreted in isolation.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PP Evidence Codes are one standardized ACMG/AMP evidence category.
- PP codes represent supporting pathogenic evidence.
- Supporting evidence contributes meaningfully to variant interpretation.
- Supporting evidence alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PP Evidence Codes** as the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** category within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that PP codes contribute **supporting-level evidence** toward pathogenic classification while emphasizing that they are interpreted together with stronger evidence categories when appropriate. ClinGen SVI recommendations further refine implementation of individual PP criteria while maintaining that **supporting evidence represents one component of structured evidence-based interpretation rather than an independent diagnostic conclusion.**
