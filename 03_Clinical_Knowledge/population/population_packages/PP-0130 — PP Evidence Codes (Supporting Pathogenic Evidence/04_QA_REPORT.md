# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces the **PP (Supporting Pathogenic Evidence)** category within the ACMG/AMP framework and completes the educational progression of pathogenic evidence strengths (**PVS → PS → PM → PP**). It explains, in patient-friendly language, that **supporting evidence contributes meaningfully to variant interpretation despite being the lowest pathogenic evidence-strength category**, and reinforces that PP evidence is **never interpreted in isolation**. Individual PP criteria (PP1–PP5), ACMG evidence-combination rules, Bayesian interpretation, ClinGen implementation guidance, and other technical subjects are intentionally deferred to dedicated Population Packages, maintaining strict adherence to the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
