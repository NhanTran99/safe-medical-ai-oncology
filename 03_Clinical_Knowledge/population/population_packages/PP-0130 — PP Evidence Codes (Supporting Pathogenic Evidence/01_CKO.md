- Recognize that supporting evidence alone cannot determine that a variant is pathogenic.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Are PP Evidence Codes?

## Patient Explanation

**PP Evidence Codes** are one group of standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

The letters **PP** stand for **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence**.

Each PP code represents one type of **supporting scientific evidence** that may help determine whether a variant is disease-causing.

---

## Clinical Importance

PP Evidence Codes contribute supporting evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PP Evidence Codes
- Supporting evidence
- ACMG Evidence Codes

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Does "Supporting Evidence" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

Not all scientific evidence has the same strength.

Within the ACMG system, **supporting evidence** is considered a lower level of evidence than **Very Strong**, **Strong**, or **Moderate** evidence.

However, supporting evidence is still an important part of the overall evaluation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Different categories of evidence contribute different levels of confidence during variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Supporting evidence
- Evidence strength
- Scientific confidence

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Are PP Evidence Codes Important?

## Patient Explanation

A single piece of supporting evidence is usually **not enough** to determine whether a variant causes disease.

Instead, genetics professionals combine supporting evidence with many other types of evidence before reaching a final conclusion.

---

## Clinical Importance

Professional interpretation depends on integrating multiple categories of scientific evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Evidence integration
- Multiple evidence types
- Variant interpretation

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Do PP Evidence Codes Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PP Evidence Codes **do not provide a final diagnosis**.

They contribute one category of supporting evidence that must always be evaluated together with other scientific findings.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on the complete body of available evidence.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Professional interpretation
- Final classification

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use PP Evidence Codes?

## Patient Explanation

Using standardized evidence codes helps genetics laboratories evaluate variants in a consistent and transparent way.

Even evidence that is considered "supporting" helps build a complete scientific assessment.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence categories improve consistency and reproducibility in clinical genomics.

---

## Key Concepts

- Standardization
- Evidence framework
- Clinical genomics

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Supporting evidence is too weak to be useful.

**Fact**

Supporting evidence is an important part of professional variant interpretation.

Although it is weaker than higher evidence categories, it contributes to the overall body of evidence used to classify genetic variants.

---

# Key Messages

- PP Evidence Codes represent Supporting Pathogenic Evidence.
- Supporting evidence contributes to professional variant interpretation.
- Supporting evidence is weaker than Very Strong, Strong, and Moderate evidence.
- Supporting evidence is still clinically important.
- PP Evidence Codes never determine the final variant classification on their own.
