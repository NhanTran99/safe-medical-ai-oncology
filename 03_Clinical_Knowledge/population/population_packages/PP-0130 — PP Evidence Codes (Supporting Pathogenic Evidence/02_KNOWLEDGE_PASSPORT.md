**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PP Evidence Codes** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** contributes to evidence-based variant classification while emphasizing that supporting evidence alone is insufficient to establish pathogenicity.
