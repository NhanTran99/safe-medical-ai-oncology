**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PM5** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how prior knowledge about one pathogenic missense variant can contribute one category of moderate evidence when evaluating a different missense variant affecting the same amino acid position, while emphasizing that this evidence alone is not sufficient to establish pathogenicity.
