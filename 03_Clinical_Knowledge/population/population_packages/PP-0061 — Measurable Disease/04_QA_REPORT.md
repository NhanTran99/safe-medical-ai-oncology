- explanation that non-measurable lesions remain clinically important;
- professional determination of measurability.

Excluded:

- lymph node measurement criteria;
- target lesions;
- non-target lesions;
- RECIST calculations;
- CR/PR/SD/PD;
- treatment decisions;

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational understanding of **measurable disease**, helping patients distinguish between standardized measurement terminology and the overall clinical importance of cancer lesions.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- Non-target Lesions
- RECIST Size Criteria
- Lymph Node Measurement Criteria
- Complete Response (CR)
- Partial Response (PR)
- Stable Disease (SD)
- Progressive Disease (PD)
- RECIST Measurements

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
