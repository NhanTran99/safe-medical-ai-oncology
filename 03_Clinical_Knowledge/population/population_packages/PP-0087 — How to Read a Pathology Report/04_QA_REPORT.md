# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package serves as the **navigation guide** for pathology reports rather than an interpretation guide. It prepares patients to understand the structure of pathology reports and communicate effectively with their healthcare team before progressing to detailed Population Packages covering **Final Diagnosis**, **Tumor Grade**, **Margin Status**, **Biomarker Results**, and **Molecular Testing**.
