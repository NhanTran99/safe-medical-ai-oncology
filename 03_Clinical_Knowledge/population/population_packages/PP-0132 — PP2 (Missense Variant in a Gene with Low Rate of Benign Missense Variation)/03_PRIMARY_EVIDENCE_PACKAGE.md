# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PP2 (Missense Variant in a Gene with Low Rate of Benign Missense Variation)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PP2 (Missense Variant in a Gene with Low Rate of Benign Missense Variation)** as one of the standardized **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **PP2 provides supporting evidence when a missense variant occurs in a gene where missense variants are a common disease mechanism and benign missense variants are relatively uncommon.** Under these circumstances, the presence of a missense variant may contribute supporting evidence toward pathogenicity. However, **PP2 never establishes pathogenicity on its own** and must always be interpreted together with other clinical, genetic, and laboratory evidence.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PP2 is one individual ACMG evidence criterion.
- PP2 evaluates missense variants within the context of the affected gene.
- Some genes make missense variants more informative than others.
- PP2 provides supporting—not definitive—evidence.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PP2** as one of the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that PP2 applies when a missense variant occurs in a gene where missense variants are a common disease mechanism and benign missense variants are uncommon. ClinGen SVI provides additional implementation recommendations for applying PP2 in specific gene contexts, but these technical refinements remain intentionally outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
