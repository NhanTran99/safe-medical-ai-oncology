- Understand why PP2 may provide supporting evidence for pathogenicity.
- Recognize that PP2 alone cannot determine that a variant is pathogenic.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PP2?

## Patient Explanation

**PP2** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

PP2 represents one type of **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence**.

It focuses on **missense variants** that occur in certain genes.

---

## Clinical Importance

PP2 provides supporting evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PP2
- Supporting pathogenic evidence
- ACMG Evidence Codes

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Is a Missense Variant?

## Patient Explanation

A **missense variant** is a genetic change that causes one building block (amino acid) of a protein to be replaced by another.

Many missense variants have **no harmful effect**, while others may affect how a protein works.

---

## Clinical Importance

Not every missense variant causes disease.

---

## Key Concepts

- Missense variant
- Amino acid
- Protein

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Is PP2 Important?

## Patient Explanation

Some genes are known to develop disease mainly through missense variants.

In these genes, harmless missense variants are relatively uncommon.

Because of this, finding a new missense variant may provide **supporting evidence** that the variant could be disease-causing.

---

## Clinical Importance

The characteristics of a gene influence how genetic variants are interpreted.

---

## Key Concepts

- Gene characteristics
- Supporting evidence

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PP2 Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PP2 **does not prove** that a variant causes disease.

It provides one piece of supporting evidence that must always be considered together with many other scientific findings.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on integrating multiple evidence categories.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Supporting evidence
- Professional interpretation

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use PP2?

## Patient Explanation

Some genes behave differently from others.

Using PP2 helps genetics professionals consider whether a missense variant occurs in a type of gene where this kind of change is more likely to be important.

This improves consistency when interpreting genetic test results.

---

## Clinical Importance

Standardized evidence criteria improve consistency and reproducibility in clinical genomics.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Every missense variant causes disease.

**Fact**

Most missense variants do **not** automatically cause disease.

PP2 simply recognizes that, in certain genes, missense variants may provide supporting evidence for pathogenicity and must always be interpreted together with other evidence.

---

# Key Messages

- PP2 is one Supporting Pathogenic Evidence criterion.
- PP2 applies to missense variants in certain genes.
- Not all missense variants are harmful.
- PP2 provides supporting—not definitive—evidence.
- Final variant classification always requires integration of multiple evidence categories.
