**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PP2** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, why **missense variants occurring in certain genes** may contribute supporting evidence during evidence-based variant classification while emphasizing that PP2 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
