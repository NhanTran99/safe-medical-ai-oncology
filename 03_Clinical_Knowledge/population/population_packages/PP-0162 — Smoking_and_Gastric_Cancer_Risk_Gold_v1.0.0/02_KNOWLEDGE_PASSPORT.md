## Knowledge Classification
- **Clinical Domain:** Risk & Prevention
- **Knowledge Type:** Risk-factor / prevention education
- **Atomic Question:** How does cigarette smoking affect gastric-cancer risk, and what does stopping smoking mean for reducing that risk?

## Patient Journey Classification
- Risk recognition
- Prevention
- Modifiable risk reduction

## Intended Runtime Usage
Use when a patient asks:
- whether smoking causes stomach cancer;
- how much smoking changes gastric-cancer risk;
- whether quitting helps;
- whether former smokers remain at increased risk;
- whether smoking interacts with other gastric-cancer risk factors.

Do not use as a generic smoking-cessation treatment manual or individualized gastric-cancer risk calculator.

## Retrieval / Runtime Relevance
**Very High** for gastric-cancer risk/prevention questions involving smoking.

## Evidence Classification
**Authoritative educational synthesis.**

### Strongly Supported
- Smoking increases gastric-cancer risk.
- Current smokers have higher risk than former smokers.
- Risk decreases after cessation.

### Context-dependent
- Sex-specific magnitude.
- Exposure-response.
- Smoking in combination with other risk factors.

### Not supported as precise outputs by current sources
- Individual absolute-risk calculation.
- Universal pack-year threshold.
- Exact time-to-baseline-risk threshold.
