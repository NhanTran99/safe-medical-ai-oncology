# Educational Intent

Primary Goal

Help patients understand the role of surgery in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma.

Secondary Goals

- Explain when surgery may be recommended.
- Introduce the major types of gastrectomy.
- Explain the goals of curative and palliative surgery.
- Prepare patients for future Population Packages covering surgical techniques, postoperative recovery and nutrition.

# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. Surgery is an important treatment option for many patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.
2. Surgical treatment is individualized.
3. Curative and palliative surgery have different goals.
4. Partial and total gastrectomy are the most common surgical approaches.
5. Surgical decisions are made through multidisciplinary evaluation.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Surgery is not appropriate for every patient.
- The decision to operate depends on disease stage, overall health and multidisciplinary assessment.
- Curative surgery aims to remove all detectable cancer whenever feasible.
- Patients should discuss the risks and expected benefits of surgery with their surgical oncology team.
