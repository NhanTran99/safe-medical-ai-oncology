# Evidence Extraction Summary

## Core Concepts

- Surgery is a cornerstone treatment for many patients with localized gastric adenocarcinoma.
- The goal of surgery depends on disease stage and clinical circumstances.
- Surgical treatment is individualized.
- Surgery is often combined with other treatment modalities.

---

## Major Surgical Concepts

Curative surgery

- Aims to remove all detectable cancer.

Palliative surgery

- Aims to relieve symptoms and improve quality of life when cure is not feasible.

Common procedures

- Partial gastrectomy.
- Total gastrectomy.

Supporting procedures

- Lymph node dissection (introduction).

---

## Important Clinical Messages

Not every patient is a surgical candidate.

The extent of surgery depends on tumor characteristics and patient factors.

Treatment planning is performed by a multidisciplinary team.
