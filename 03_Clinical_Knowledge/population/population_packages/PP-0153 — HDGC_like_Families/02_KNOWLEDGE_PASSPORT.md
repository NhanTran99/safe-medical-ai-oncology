# Primary Runtime Roles

- Recognize the formal HDGC-like family definition
- Explain why negative genetic testing does not necessarily eliminate hereditary concern
- Explain uncertainty of gastric-cancer risk
- Explain when endoscopic surveillance may be considered
- Explain source-supported surveillance timing
- Explain why surveillance is not automatically lifelong annual screening
- Explain why negative endoscopy does not eliminate familial concern
- Explain why risk-reducing total gastrectomy is not automatically advised
- Prevent inappropriate extrapolation from CDH1-positive HDGC
