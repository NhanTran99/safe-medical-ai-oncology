# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What are antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) and why are they important in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an evidence-based understanding of antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) as an emerging therapeutic platform in modern precision oncology.

This Population Package explains what ADCs are, how they generally work, why biomarker testing is often required before treatment, and how ADC technology expands treatment options for selected patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.

It serves as the prerequisite before introducing ADC mechanisms, payload biology, linker technology, individual ADC medicines and toxicity management.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- ADCs are an important advancement in precision oncology.
- ADCs combine targeted therapy with delivery of an anti-cancer medicine.
- Biomarker testing is usually necessary before treatment selection.
- ADCs expand treatment options for selected patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.
- ADCs remain cancer medicines with potential risks and require clinical monitoring.
- Patients should understand why ADC therapy is individualized.
