## Intended Runtime Usage

Use this PP when the user asks:
- what H. pylori eradication means;
- why eradication can prevent gastric cancer;
- who may benefit from eradication;
- eradication after early gastric cancer;
- eradication with precursor gastric changes;
- how treatment selection should be approached;
- what happens after treatment failure;
- why confirmation of eradication matters;
- recurrence/reinfection;
- antibiotic resistance.

Do not use this PP to provide:
- an individualized prescription;
- exact current antibiotic regimens without an authoritative current H. pylori guideline source;
- detailed diagnostic testing instructions;
- detailed test-of-cure timing;
- detailed salvage therapy.
