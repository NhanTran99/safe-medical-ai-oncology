# Intended Runtime Usage

## Primary Runtime Role

- Foundational educational response regarding the payload component of ADCs.

## Secondary Runtime Role

- Prerequisite knowledge retrieval before Payload Pharmacology, Bystander Effect and ADC Toxicity Population Packages.

## Typical Trigger Questions

- What is the payload in an ADC?
- What does the payload do?
- Why is the payload attached to an antibody?
- How does the payload reach cancer cells?
- Why is the payload important?
- Does the payload still cause side effects?
- Why is the payload part of precision medicine?

## Retrieval Priority

High

**Reason**

The payload is one of the three core components of every antibody-drug conjugate and is fundamental to understanding ADC therapy.
