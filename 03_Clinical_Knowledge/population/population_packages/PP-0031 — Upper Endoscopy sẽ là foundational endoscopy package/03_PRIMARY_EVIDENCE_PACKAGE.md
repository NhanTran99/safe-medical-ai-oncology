# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is upper endoscopy and why is it performed?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of upper endoscopy, including its purpose, what doctors examine during the procedure, why it is commonly recommended, and how it contributes to the evaluation of stomach diseases, including stomach cancer.

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational concept of upper endoscopy before introducing biopsy, pathology, diagnosis and staging.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Upper endoscopy is a direct visual examination of the upper digestive tract.
- It is an important tool for evaluating symptoms and stomach abnormalities.
- It plays a central role in detecting abnormalities that may require further investigation.
- Biopsy may be performed when appropriate.
- Histopathological examination is often required to establish a definitive diagnosis.
- Most people undergoing upper endoscopy do not ultimately have stomach cancer.

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence is highly consistent regarding:

- the purpose of upper endoscopy;

Differences between references are limited to wording and educational depth.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- scientific definitions from NCI PDQ;
- patient-friendly explanations from ACS;
- terminology consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.
