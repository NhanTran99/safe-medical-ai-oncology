## Clinical Scope

### Core
definition and significance of high-incidence populations, geographic incidence variation, Japan/Korea/China screening evidence, photofluorography experience, endoscopic screening evidence in Asia, population-level mortality associations, participation, transferability/generalizability, and benefit–harm implications of incidence

### Supporting
migrant effects, environmental risk context, high-risk clinical subgroups, screening-program implementation context

### Explicitly Excluded
individual screening eligibility, detailed endoscopy technique, serum pepsinogen methodology, screening harms/false-result detail, hereditary genetic testing, diagnostic work-up, treatment, universal screening intervals, and individualized absolute-risk calculation

### Delegated-to PP
PP-0170, PP-0171, PP-0172, PP-0174, PP-0160, PP-0165, PP-0166, PP-0167, hereditary-risk packages, and downstream diagnosis
