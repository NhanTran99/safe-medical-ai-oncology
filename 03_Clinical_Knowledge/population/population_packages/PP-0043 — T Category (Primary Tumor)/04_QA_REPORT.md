# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of the T category;
- meaning of the primary tumor;
- relationship between T category and depth of tumor invasion;
- general relationship between increasing T category and local disease extent;
- contribution of the T category to TNM staging, treatment planning and prognostic assessment;
- explanation that T category must always be interpreted together with N, M and other clinical information.

Excluded:

- Tis classification;
- T1–T4a/T4b criteria;
- stomach wall anatomy;
- AJCC Stage Groups;
- clinical versus pathological staging;
- stage-specific prognosis;
- treatment recommendations according to T category.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
