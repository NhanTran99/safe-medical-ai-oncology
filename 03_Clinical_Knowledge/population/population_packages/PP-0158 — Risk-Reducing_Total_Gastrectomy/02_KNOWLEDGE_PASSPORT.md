## Intended Runtime Usage

Use this PP when the user asks:

- What is risk-reducing total gastrectomy?
- Why remove the stomach if I have no symptoms?
- Who with HDGC may consider gastrectomy?
- Why can endoscopy miss HDGC?
- What are the benefits and risks of RRTG?
- What happens after the stomach is removed?
- What if I do not want surgery yet?
- Can surveillance be used instead?
- What should I expect after RRTG?

Do not use this PP as a substitute for:

- individualized surgical consultation;
- genetic counseling;
- exact testing eligibility;
- endoscopic surveillance protocol;
- variant interpretation.
