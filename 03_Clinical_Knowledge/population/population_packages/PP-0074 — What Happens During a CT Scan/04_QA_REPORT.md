# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- patient experience during CT;
- positioning on the examination table;
- remaining still;
- brief breath-holding;
- communication with imaging staff;
- temporary sensations from contrast material;
- completion of the examination.

Excluded:

- CT preparation;
- scanner technology;
- radiation;
- contrast safety;
- CT interpretation;
- post-examination care.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package explains the **patient experience during a CT examination**, helping reduce uncertainty and anxiety before imaging. It complements **PP-0073 (Preparing for a CT Scan)** by focusing on the examination itself and provides the foundation for future Population Packages covering **post-CT care**, **CT results**, **contrast safety**, and **radiation safety**.
