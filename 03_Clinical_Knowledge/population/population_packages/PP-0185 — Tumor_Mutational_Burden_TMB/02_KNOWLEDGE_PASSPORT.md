# Primary Runtime Role

- Explain TMB in plain language.
- Explain TMB-H.
- Explain mutations per megabase.
- Explain why TMB can matter clinically in advanced gastric cancer.
- Clarify the distinction between TMB and other biomarkers.
- Prevent overinterpretation of TMB results.

- Molecular biomarker education.
- Precision oncology education.
- Interpretation of a single TMB result at a conceptual level.
- Preparation for understanding NGS reports.

# Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

TMB is explicitly identified by NCCN as a biomarker implicated in the clinical management of advanced gastric cancer, and TMB-H is linked to a defined treatment context. Patients may encounter TMB as part of a genomic report and need to understand its meaning without confusing it with other biomarkers or assuming that it independently determines treatment.

# Clinical Scope

## Included

### Core Concept

- Definition of TMB.
- Tumor mutational burden as a quantitative genomic biomarker.
- Meaning of mutations per megabase.
- Definition of TMB-H.
- Current NCCN gastric-cancer treatment-context threshold of ≥10 mutations/Mb.
- Conceptual relationship between TMB and genomic testing.
- Conceptual relationship between TMB and NGS.
- Why high mutational burden may be relevant to immune recognition.
- TMB-H as a treatment-relevant biomarker.
- Selected advanced gastric-cancer clinical context.
- Conceptual relevance of TMB-H to pembrolizumab.
- Patient-facing interpretation.
- Major limitations and evidence gaps.
- Distinction from other biomarkers.
- Common misconceptions.

### Clinical Interpretation

The package explains what a TMB result can and cannot tell a patient. It emphasizes that TMB-H is a treatment-relevant biomarker in a defined context, not an automatic treatment directive.

### Evidence Limitation

The package preserves the NCCN-described limitation that no gastroesophageal cancer patients were included in the KEYNOTE-158 TMB analysis used as pivotal evidence.
