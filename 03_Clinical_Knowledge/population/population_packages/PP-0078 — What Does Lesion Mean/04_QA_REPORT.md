# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the **foundational understanding of the term "lesion"**, one of the most frequently misunderstood words in radiology reports. By emphasizing that a lesion is a descriptive observation rather than a diagnosis, it reduces unnecessary anxiety and prepares patients for subsequent Population Packages explaining **Mass**, **Nodule**, **Target Lesion**, **RECIST terminology**, and lesion-specific diagnostic pathways.
