- Recognize that different cancers use different biomarkers.
- Understand that biomarker testing is one part of personalized cancer care.
- Feel more confident discussing biomarker testing with the healthcare team.

# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is Biomarker Testing?

## Patient Explanation

**Biomarker testing** is laboratory testing that looks for certain **biological characteristics** of a cancer or, in some situations, the body.

The results may provide information that helps doctors better understand the cancer and guide medical care.

---

## Clinical Importance

Biomarker testing provides clinically useful biological information.

---

## Key Concepts

- Biomarkers.
- Biological characteristics.
- Laboratory testing.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — Why Is Biomarker Testing Performed?

## Patient Explanation

Depending on the type of cancer, biomarker testing may help doctors:

- learn more about the cancer;
- identify treatments that may be appropriate;
- determine whether certain therapies are likely to be beneficial;
- support personalized cancer care.

The purpose of testing varies between different cancers.

---

## Clinical Importance

Biomarker testing supports individualized clinical decision-making.

---

## Key Concepts

- Personalized care.
- Treatment selection.
- Cancer characteristics.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — What Samples Can Be Used?

## Patient Explanation

Biomarker testing may be performed using:

- tissue collected during a biopsy or surgery;
- blood or other biological samples when appropriate.

The type of sample depends on the clinical situation and the test being performed.

---

## Clinical Importance

Different biomarker tests require different specimen types.

---

## Key Concepts

- Tissue sample.
- Blood sample.
- Specimen selection.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does Everyone Need the Same Biomarker Tests?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Different cancers use different biomarkers.

Even among people with the same type of cancer, doctors may recommend different tests depending on the individual's situation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Biomarker testing is individualized according to clinical guidelines.

---

## Key Concepts

- Individualized testing.
- Disease-specific testing.
- Clinical guidelines.

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Does Biomarker Testing Replace Other Tests?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Biomarker testing is only **one part** of cancer evaluation.

Doctors also consider:

- medical history;
- physical examination;
- imaging;
- pathology findings;
- stage;
- many other clinical factors.

---

## Clinical Importance

Biomarker testing complements, rather than replaces, other diagnostic information.

---

## Key Concepts

- Comprehensive assessment.
- Multiple information sources.
- Integrated clinical care.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Everyone with cancer should have exactly the same biomarker tests.

**Fact**

The appropriate biomarker tests depend on the type of cancer, the clinical situation, and current medical guidelines.

---

# Key Messages

- Biomarker testing looks for important biological characteristics.
- Different cancers use different biomarkers.
- Not every patient needs the same biomarker tests.
- Biomarker testing supports personalized cancer care.
- Biomarker testing is only one part of a complete clinical evaluation.
