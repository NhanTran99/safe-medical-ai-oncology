# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is biomarker testing?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **biomarker testing** as laboratory testing performed to identify biological characteristics that may help guide cancer diagnosis, prognosis, treatment selection, and other aspects of clinical care depending on the clinical context.

This Population Package introduces the role of biomarker testing in modern oncology while emphasizing that testing is individualized and complements—rather than replaces—other clinical information.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Biomarker testing evaluates biological characteristics relevant to cancer care.
- Biomarker testing may contribute to diagnosis, prognosis, treatment selection, or disease management depending on the clinical context.
- Different cancers require different biomarkers.
- Biomarker testing is individualized.
- Biomarker testing is only one component of comprehensive clinical evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently describe biomarker testing as a fundamental component of modern precision oncology. They emphasize that testing should be selected according to the cancer type, clinical question, available evidence, and current practice guidelines. Biomarker testing should always be interpreted together with pathology findings, imaging, staging, and the patient's overall clinical situation.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- terminology from NCI;
- clinical guidance from NCCN;
- precision oncology principles from ASCO;
- pathology standards from CAP;
- patient education principles from ACS;
- international guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
