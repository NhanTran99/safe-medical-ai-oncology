# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is TNM Staging?

## Patient Explanation

**TNM staging** is an internationally used system for describing **how far a cancer has spread in the body**.

Doctors use TNM staging to describe the extent of cancer in a consistent way.

---

## Clinical Importance

TNM staging standardizes description of the anatomical extent of cancer.

---

## Key Concepts

- Cancer staging.
- Extent of disease.
- International standard.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Does "T" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

The **T** stands for **Tumor**.

It describes the primary tumor, including its size or how far it has grown into nearby tissues, depending on the type of cancer.

---

## Clinical Importance

The T category describes characteristics of the primary tumor.

---

## Key Concepts

- Primary tumor.
- Tumor extent.
- Tumor characteristics.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — What Do "N" and "M" Mean?

## Patient Explanation

The **N** stands for **Nodes**.

It describes whether nearby lymph nodes are involved.

The **M** stands for **Metastasis**.

It describes whether the cancer has spread to distant parts of the body.

---

## Clinical Importance

N describes regional lymph node involvement, while M describes distant metastasis.

---

## Key Concepts

- Regional lymph nodes.
- Distant metastasis.
- Cancer spread.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — How Is the Overall Stage Determined?

## Patient Explanation

Doctors combine the **T**, **N**, and **M** categories to determine the overall stage of a cancer.

The stage helps describe how advanced the cancer is, but it is only one part of the complete evaluation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Overall stage integrates T, N and M classifications.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined assessment.
- Overall stage.
- Clinical evaluation.

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Is TNM Stage the Same as Tumor Grade?

## Patient Explanation

No.

**Tumor stage** describes **how far the cancer has spread**.

**Tumor grade** describes **how abnormal the cancer cells look under the microscope**.

Doctors consider both stage and grade, together with many other factors, when caring for patients.

---

## Clinical Importance

Stage and grade provide complementary but different clinical information.

---

## Key Concepts

- Stage.
- Grade.
- Different purposes.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

A higher tumor grade automatically means a higher TNM stage.

**Fact**

Tumor grade and TNM stage measure different aspects of cancer. A higher grade does not necessarily mean the cancer has spread further.

---

# Key Messages

- TNM staging describes how far cancer has spread.
- T describes the primary tumor.
- N describes nearby lymph nodes.
- M describes distant metastasis.
- TNM stage is different from tumor grade.
- TNM staging is one part of the overall assessment used to guide cancer care.
