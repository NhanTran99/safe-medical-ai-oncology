# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is TNM staging?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **TNM staging** as the internationally accepted system for describing the anatomical extent of cancer.

This Population Package explains the meaning of **Tumor (T)**, **Nodes (N)** and **Metastasis (M)**, clarifies how these components contribute to overall cancer staging, and emphasizes that TNM staging represents only one part of comprehensive cancer assessment.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- TNM is the standard system for describing cancer extent.
- T, N and M each describe a different aspect of disease.
- These components are combined to determine the overall stage.
- TNM staging facilitates communication among healthcare professionals.
- Stage and grade are different concepts.
- TNM staging is only one component of comprehensive cancer evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

AJCC and UICC consistently define TNM staging as the international standard for describing the anatomical extent of malignant disease. Major oncology organizations further emphasize that staging should always be interpreted alongside pathology, tumor biology, biomarkers, patient characteristics, and multidisciplinary clinical assessment.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- international staging standards from AJCC and UICC;
- educational principles from NCI and ACS;
- multidisciplinary oncology guidance consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.
