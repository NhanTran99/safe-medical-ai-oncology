# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the **foundational cancer staging framework** for the repository. It introduces the universal language of **T (Tumor), N (Nodes), and M (Metastasis)** without entering disease-specific staging systems or stage groupings. It forms the conceptual bridge between **Tumor Grade (PP-0089)** and the subsequent educational packages on **Stage I–IV**, **Clinical vs Pathological Stage**, and detailed TNM classification.
