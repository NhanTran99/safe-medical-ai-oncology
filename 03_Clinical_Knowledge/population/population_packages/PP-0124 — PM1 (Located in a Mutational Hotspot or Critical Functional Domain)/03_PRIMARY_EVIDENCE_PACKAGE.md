# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PM1 (Located in a Mutational Hotspot or Critical Functional Domain)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PM1 (Located in a Mutational Hotspot or Critical Functional Domain)** as one of the standardized **ACMG/AMP Moderate Pathogenic Evidence Codes**.

This Population Package explains that **PM1 represents moderate evidence supporting pathogenicity when a genetic variant is located within a well-established mutational hotspot or an important functional region of a gene or protein where disease-causing variants are known to occur and benign variation is uncommon.** It emphasizes that PM1 provides **location-based evidence** and is only one component of the overall ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PM1 is one standardized ACMG/AMP evidence code.
- The location of a variant may contribute moderate evidence supporting pathogenicity.
- Certain functional regions and mutational hotspots have particular clinical importance.
- PM1 contributes one category of evidence within structured variant interpretation.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PM1** as one of the standardized **Moderate Pathogenic Evidence Codes** within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that PM1 may be applied when a variant occurs in a **well-established mutational hotspot** or a **critical functional domain** where pathogenic variants are known to occur and benign variation is uncommon. ClinGen SVI recommendations further refine the application of PM1 while emphasizing that **variant location represents one component of structured evidence-based interpretation rather than an independent diagnostic conclusion.**
