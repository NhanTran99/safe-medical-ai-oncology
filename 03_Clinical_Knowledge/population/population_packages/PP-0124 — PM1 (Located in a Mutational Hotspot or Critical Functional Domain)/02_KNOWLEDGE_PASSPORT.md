**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PM1** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, why the **location** of a genetic variant within a particularly important region of a gene or protein may contribute one category of moderate evidence during professional interpretation.

**Very High**

**Reason:**

PM1 introduces the concept of **location-based evidence**, one of the fundamental moderate evidence categories within ACMG/AMP variant interpretation, while establishing the conceptual foundation for later Population Packages covering protein domains, hotspot biology, and structural genomics.
