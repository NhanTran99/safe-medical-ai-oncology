# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **PM1**, the first individual evidence code within the **PM (Moderate Pathogenic Evidence)** family, by explaining that the **location** of a genetic variant can itself contribute clinically meaningful evidence during variant interpretation. It presents the concepts of **mutational hotspots** and **critical functional domains** in patient-friendly language while emphasizing that location alone is never sufficient to establish pathogenicity. Advanced topics—including protein structural biology, hotspot discovery methods, evolutionary conservation, and ClinGen implementation guidance—are appropriately deferred to future Population Packages, maintaining strict adherence to the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
