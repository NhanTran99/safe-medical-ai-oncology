**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **PM2** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how information from large population databases contributes one category of moderate evidence during professional variant interpretation, while emphasizing that a rare variant is not automatically disease-causing.
