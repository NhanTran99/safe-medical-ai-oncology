# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of target lesions;
- rationale for selecting only certain lesions;
- standardized follow-up over time;
- importance of professional selection;
- continued importance of non-selected lesions.

Excluded:

- measurable disease;
- non-target lesions;
- lesion measurement criteria;
- RECIST calculations;
- CR/PR/SD/PD;
- treatment decisions;
- prognosis.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational understanding of **target lesions**, emphasizing that standardized follow-up focuses on selected lesions while comprehensive clinical assessment continues to consider all relevant disease sites.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- Measurable Disease
- Non-target Lesions
- Maximum Number of Target Lesions
- Lymph Node Measurement Criteria
- Sum of Target Lesion Diameters
- Complete Response (CR)
- Partial Response (PR)
- Stable Disease (SD)
- Progressive Disease (PD)
- RECIST Measurements

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
