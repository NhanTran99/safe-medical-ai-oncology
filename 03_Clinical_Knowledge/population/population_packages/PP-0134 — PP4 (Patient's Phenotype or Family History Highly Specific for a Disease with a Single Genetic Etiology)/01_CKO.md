# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is PP4?

## Patient Explanation

**PP4** is one of the standardized **ACMG Evidence Codes** used by genetics professionals when evaluating whether a genetic variant may cause disease.

PP4 represents one type of **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence**.

It focuses on information obtained from a person's **clinical features (phenotype)** and **family history**.

---

## Clinical Importance

PP4 provides supporting evidence during professional variant interpretation.

---

## Key Concepts

- PP4
- Supporting pathogenic evidence
- ACMG Evidence Codes

# Knowledge Block 2 — What Is a Phenotype?

## Patient Explanation

A person's **phenotype** refers to the characteristics that can be observed or identified, such as symptoms, physical findings, laboratory abnormalities, or other clinical features.

Doctors compare these features with what is already known about inherited diseases.

---

## Clinical Importance

Clinical features are an important source of evidence in genetic medicine.

---

## Key Concepts

- Phenotype
- Clinical features
- Symptoms

# Knowledge Block 3 — Why Is Family History Important?

## Patient Explanation

Family history describes illnesses that have occurred in close relatives.

When several family members have a pattern of disease that is highly characteristic of a known inherited condition caused by a single gene, this information may provide **supporting evidence** during variant interpretation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Family history can strengthen clinical assessment during genetic evaluation.

---

## Key Concepts

- Family history
- Inherited disease
- Supporting evidence

# Knowledge Block 4 — Does PP4 Mean the Variant Definitely Causes Disease?

## Patient Explanation

No.

PP4 **does not prove** that a variant causes disease.

Clinical features and family history provide one part of the evidence used by genetics professionals.

They must always be interpreted together with laboratory findings, genetic evidence, and other scientific information.

---

## Clinical Importance

Final variant classification depends on integrating multiple evidence categories.

---

## Key Concepts

- Combined evidence
- Professional interpretation
- Supporting evidence

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Why Does the ACMG Use PP4?

## Patient Explanation

Genetic test results are most meaningful when interpreted together with information about the patient.

PP4 helps genetics professionals include important clinical observations and family history in a structured, evidence-based evaluation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Integrating clinical information with laboratory evidence improves consistency and accuracy in genomic medicine.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

Having symptoms that match a genetic disease automatically proves that a genetic variant is disease-causing.

**Fact**

Clinical features and family history may provide **supporting evidence**, but they never establish pathogenicity by themselves.

Additional genetic and scientific evidence is always required.

---

# Key Messages

- PP4 is one Supporting Pathogenic Evidence criterion.
- PP4 uses clinical features and family history.
- Highly characteristic clinical findings may provide supporting evidence.
- PP4 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires integration of multiple evidence categories.
