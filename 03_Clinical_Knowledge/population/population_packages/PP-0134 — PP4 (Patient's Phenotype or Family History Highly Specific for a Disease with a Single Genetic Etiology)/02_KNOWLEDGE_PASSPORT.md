**Reason:**

Patients undergoing genetic evaluation frequently ask why doctors pay close attention to their symptoms and family history in addition to genetic test results. This Population Package explains how **highly characteristic clinical features or family history** may contribute **supporting pathogenic evidence** during evidence-based variant interpretation while emphasizing that PP4 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
