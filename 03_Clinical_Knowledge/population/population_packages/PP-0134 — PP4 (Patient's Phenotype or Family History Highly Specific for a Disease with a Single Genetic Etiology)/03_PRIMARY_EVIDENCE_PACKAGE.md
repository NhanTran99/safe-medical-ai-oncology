# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is PP4 (Patient's Phenotype or Family History Highly Specific for a Disease with a Single Genetic Etiology)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **PP4 (Patient's Phenotype or Family History Highly Specific for a Disease with a Single Genetic Etiology)** as one of the standardized **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the **ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework**.

This Population Package explains that **PP4 provides supporting evidence when a patient's clinical features (phenotype) or family history are highly characteristic of a disease known to result from changes in a single gene.** Such information can strengthen confidence that a genetic variant may be disease-causing. However, **PP4 represents supporting evidence only** and must always be interpreted together with genetic findings, laboratory evidence, and other ACMG evidence categories.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- PP4 is one individual ACMG evidence criterion.
- PP4 incorporates phenotype and family history into variant interpretation.
- Highly characteristic clinical findings may provide supporting evidence.
- PP4 alone cannot establish pathogenicity.
- Final variant classification always requires comprehensive professional evaluation.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Professional organizations consistently recognize **PP4** as one of the **Supporting Pathogenic Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP variant interpretation framework. Guidance consistently explains that highly characteristic clinical findings or family history for a disease with a single genetic etiology may contribute supporting evidence toward pathogenic classification. ClinGen SVI provides additional implementation recommendations for phenotype-based evidence assessment, but these technical refinements intentionally remain outside the scope of this introductory educational package.
