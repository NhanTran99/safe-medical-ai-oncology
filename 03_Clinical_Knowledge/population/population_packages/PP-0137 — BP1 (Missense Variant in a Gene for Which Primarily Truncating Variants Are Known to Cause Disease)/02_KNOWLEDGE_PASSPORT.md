**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **BP1** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, why a **missense variant** occurring in a gene where disease is usually caused by **truncating variants** may provide **supporting benign evidence**, while emphasizing that BP1 alone cannot establish benign classification.
