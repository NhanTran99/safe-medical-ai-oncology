# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **BP1**, the first individual criterion within the **Supporting Benign Evidence** category of the ACMG/AMP framework. It explains, in patient-friendly language, that **a missense variant may provide supporting benign evidence when it occurs in a gene where disease is primarily caused by truncating variants rather than missense variants**, while emphasizing that **BP1 alone never establishes benign classification**. Gene-specific implementation guidance, molecular loss-of-function mechanisms, PVS1 interpretation, ClinGen recommendations, Bayesian implementation, and ACMG evidence-combination rules are intentionally deferred to dedicated Population Packages, maintaining strict adherence to the **Atomic Knowledge Principle**.
