# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is Trastuzumab?

## Patient Explanation

Trastuzumab is a targeted cancer medicine used to treat certain HER2-positive cancers.

It is designed for tumors that have been confirmed to express HER2.

Unlike chemotherapy, trastuzumab specifically targets cancer cells with the HER2 characteristic.

---

## Clinical Importance

Trastuzumab was one of the first HER2-targeted therapies to become an important part of precision oncology.

---

## Key Concepts

- Targeted therapy
- HER2-specific treatment
- Precision medicine

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — Why Is HER2 Testing Required?

## Patient Explanation

Before trastuzumab can be considered, the tumor must first be tested for HER2.

Only patients whose tumors are HER2-positive are generally considered for trastuzumab treatment.

If the tumor is HER2-negative, other treatments are usually recommended instead.

---

## Clinical Importance

HER2 testing identifies patients who may benefit from trastuzumab.

---

## Key Concepts

- HER2-positive required
- Biomarker-guided therapy
- Personalized treatment

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — How Does Trastuzumab Work?

## Patient Explanation

Trastuzumab works by recognizing HER2-positive cancer cells and helping interfere with signals that support tumor growth.

Its action is directed toward tumors with the HER2 biomarker rather than all rapidly growing cells.

The detailed biological mechanism is discussed in another Population Package.

---

## Clinical Importance

Trastuzumab is an example of biomarker-guided targeted therapy.

---

## Key Concepts

- Targets HER2
- Different from chemotherapy
- Precision oncology

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — When May Trastuzumab Be Used?

## Patient Explanation

Trastuzumab may be recommended for patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma when appropriate according to current clinical guidelines.

Whether it is recommended depends on:

- HER2 status;
- cancer stage;
- previous treatments;
- overall health;
- treatment goals.

Your healthcare team will determine whether trastuzumab is suitable for your individual situation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Treatment decisions require integration of biomarker results and the overall clinical picture.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not appropriate for everyone
- Individualized treatment
- Guideline-based care

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — What Are the Potential Benefits?

## Patient Explanation

For appropriate patients, trastuzumab may:

- improve treatment effectiveness;
- provide an additional treatment option;
- support personalized cancer care.

The amount of benefit differs from one patient to another.

---

## Clinical Importance

Trastuzumab has become an important treatment option for HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.

---

## Key Concepts

- Additional treatment option
- Personalized therapy
- Individual benefit varies

---

# Knowledge Block 6 — What Are the Limitations?

## Patient Explanation

Trastuzumab is not suitable for every patient.

It generally requires confirmation that the tumor is HER2-positive.

Even then, your healthcare team considers many additional factors before recommending treatment.

---

## Clinical Importance

HER2 positivity alone does not automatically determine treatment.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not suitable for everyone
- Multiple factors influence treatment
- Individual clinical judgement

---

# Knowledge Block 7 — Common Side Effects and Questions

## Patient Explanation

Like all cancer medicines, trastuzumab can cause side effects.

Many patients tolerate treatment well, but side effects can occur.

Your healthcare team will monitor your health throughout treatment.

The detailed management of side effects is discussed in separate Population Packages.

---

## Common Misconception

**Myth**

Everyone with HER2-positive gastric cancer will receive trastuzumab.

**Fact**

Trastuzumab is considered only when it is appropriate for the patient's overall clinical situation and treatment goals.

---

# Knowledge Block 8 — What Should Patients Expect?

Before starting trastuzumab, patients should discuss:

- HER2 test results;
- expected benefits;
- possible risks;
- treatment goals;
- other available treatment options.

Shared decision-making helps ensure treatment reflects both medical evidence and patient preferences.

---

## Clinical Importance

Patient education supports informed decision-making throughout treatment.

---

# Key Messages

- Trastuzumab is a HER2-targeted therapy.
- HER2 testing is required before treatment.
- Trastuzumab is used only for appropriate HER2-positive patients.
- Treatment decisions depend on more than HER2 status alone.
- Benefits and risks should be discussed with the healthcare team.
- Shared decision-making is an important part of treatment planning.
