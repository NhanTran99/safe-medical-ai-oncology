# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is trastuzumab and why is it used in the treatment of HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an evidence-based understanding of trastuzumab as the foundational HER2-targeted medicine used for appropriate patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package explains what trastuzumab is, why HER2 testing is required before treatment, how trastuzumab generally works, its role in modern gastric cancer care, and the overall benefits and limitations of treatment.

It serves as the prerequisite before introducing trastuzumab deruxtecan, infusion procedures, cardiac monitoring, toxicity management and HER2 resistance.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Trastuzumab is a cornerstone HER2-targeted therapy for appropriate patients with HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.
- HER2 testing is essential before trastuzumab treatment.
- Trastuzumab represents precision medicine rather than traditional chemotherapy.
- Treatment recommendations require consideration of biomarker status and the overall clinical context.
- Patients should understand both expected benefits and potential limitations before treatment.
- Shared decision-making is an essential part of trastuzumab treatment planning.
