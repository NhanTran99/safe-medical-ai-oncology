# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is the difference between clinical stage and pathological stage?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of the differences between **clinical stage** and **pathological stage**.

This Population Package explains when each type of staging is determined, what information is used, why the two stages may differ, and how both contribute to high-quality cancer care.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Clinical stage is assessed before treatment begins.
- Pathological stage is assessed after surgical removal of the tumor.
- Surgery provides additional pathological information that may change staging.
- Differences between the two stages are expected in some patients.
- Both staging systems contribute to optimal cancer management.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

AJCC and UICC consistently distinguish clinical staging from pathological staging according to the timing of assessment and the available sources of information. Major oncology organizations further emphasize that both approaches are valid and complementary throughout the cancer care pathway.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- international staging standards from AJCC and UICC;
- educational principles from NCI and ACS;
- multidisciplinary oncology guidance consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO.
