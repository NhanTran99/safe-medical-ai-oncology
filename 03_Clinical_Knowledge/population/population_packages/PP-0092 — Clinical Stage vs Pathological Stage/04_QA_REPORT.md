# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package completes the **core cancer staging foundation** by explaining why patients may encounter more than one stage during their cancer journey. It clarifies that **clinical stage** and **pathological stage** are based on different sources of information collected at different time points, and that both are valid components of modern oncology practice. It naturally prepares the knowledge graph for **Restaging**, **ypStage**, and other advanced staging concepts.
