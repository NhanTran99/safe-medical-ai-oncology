# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is RECIST 1.1?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **RECIST 1.1**, including what it is, why it was developed, why it is the internationally accepted update to the original RECIST framework, and how it helps standardize assessment of changes in solid tumors during cancer care.

This Population Package introduces RECIST 1.1 as the current standard framework without explaining its technical measurement rules, lesion selection criteria, response categories or clinical implementation details.

---

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- RECIST 1.1 is the current internationally accepted update to the RECIST framework.
- It standardizes how changes in solid tumors are evaluated during follow-up.
- Standardization improves communication across hospitals, clinicians and clinical trials.
- RECIST 1.1 is one assessment tool rather than a treatment or staging system.
- Clinical decisions require integration of RECIST 1.1 with symptoms, examination findings, laboratory data, pathology and other clinical information.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

The evidence is highly consistent regarding:

- RECIST 1.1 as the internationally adopted update to RECIST;
- its purpose of standardizing treatment response assessment;
- its widespread use in oncology practice and research;
- the importance of interpreting RECIST 1.1 within the broader clinical context.

Differences between references primarily concern technical implementation and measurement rules, which are intentionally outside the scope of this Population Package.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- educational principles from NCI PDQ and ACS;
- terminology consistent with NCCN, JNCCN and ESMO;
- internationally accepted RECIST 1.1 concepts without introducing technical measurement criteria.
