# Educational Intent

Primary Goal

Help patients understand the purpose and contents of a pathology report.

Secondary Goals

- Explain why biopsy is required.
- Explain why pathology confirms cancer.
- Prepare patients for future Population Packages about tumor grade, Lauren classification, biomarkers and staging.

# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. A pathology report confirms the diagnosis.
2. Pathologists examine tissue under a microscope.
3. The report contains standardized diagnostic information.
4. Treatment planning depends on pathology findings.
5. Not every pathology term needs to be understood immediately.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Pathology confirms the diagnosis.
- Endoscopy alone cannot definitively diagnose cancer.
- Treatment planning relies heavily on pathology findings.
- Patients should discuss unclear pathology terms with their treating team.
