# Evidence Extraction Summary

## Core Concepts

- Biopsy provides tissue for diagnosis.
- Pathologists examine tissue microscopically.
- Histopathology confirms gastric adenocarcinoma.
- Pathology findings guide treatment planning.

---

## Common Components of a Pathology Report

- Diagnosis.
- Histologic type.
- Tumor differentiation (grade).
- Specimen.
- Margins (when applicable).
- Lymphovascular invasion.
- Perineural invasion.

---

## Important Clinical Messages

Endoscopy identifies suspicious lesions.

Biopsy provides tissue.

Pathology confirms the diagnosis.

Treatment decisions depend on pathology findings.

---

## Key Patient Messages

Endoscopy

↓

Biopsy

↓

Pathology Report

↓

Diagnosis Confirmed

↓

Treatment Planning

Patients should understand that pathology is the definitive diagnostic confirmation.
