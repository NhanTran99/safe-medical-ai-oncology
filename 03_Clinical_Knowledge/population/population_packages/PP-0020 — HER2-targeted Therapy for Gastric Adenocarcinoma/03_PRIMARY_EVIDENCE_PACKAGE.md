# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is HER2-targeted therapy and why is it used in the treatment of gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an evidence-based understanding of HER2-targeted therapy as a major precision medicine approach for HER2-positive gastric adenocarcinoma.

This Population Package explains the purpose of HER2-targeted therapy, how it generally works, why HER2 testing is required before treatment, and how this treatment fits into modern personalized cancer care.

It serves as the prerequisite before introducing individual HER2-targeted medicines, treatment protocols and toxicity management.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- HER2-targeted therapy is a major example of precision oncology.
- HER2 testing is essential before treatment selection.
- HER2-targeted therapy is intended only for appropriate HER2-positive tumors.
- Treatment planning considers biomarker status together with other clinical factors.
- HER2-targeted therapy complements, rather than replaces, other treatment modalities.
- Patients should understand why biomarker-guided treatment differs from traditional treatment approaches.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary authoritative references.

All major references consistently recommend HER2-targeted therapy for appropriate patients whose tumors demonstrate HER2 positivity.

Differences among references primarily involve:

- individual drugs;
- treatment algorithms;
- sequencing strategies;
- adverse-effect management.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- clinical recommendations from NCCN;
- evidence interpretation from JNCCN;
- patient-oriented explanations from NCI and ACS;
- internationally harmonized terminology from ESMO.
