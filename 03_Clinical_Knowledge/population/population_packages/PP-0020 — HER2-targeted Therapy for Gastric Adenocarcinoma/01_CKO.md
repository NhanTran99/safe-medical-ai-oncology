# Knowledge Block 1 — What Is HER2-targeted Therapy?

## Patient Explanation

HER2-targeted therapy is a type of cancer treatment designed for tumors that are HER2-positive.

Unlike chemotherapy, which affects rapidly growing cells more broadly, HER2-targeted therapy focuses on cancer cells with the HER2 characteristic.

Because of this, the treatment is only appropriate for selected patients whose tumors have been confirmed to be HER2-positive.

---

## Clinical Importance

HER2-targeted therapy is an important component of precision oncology for appropriate patients with gastric adenocarcinoma.

---

## Key Concepts

- HER2-targeted therapy is personalized treatment.
- It requires HER2-positive cancer.
- It is different from chemotherapy.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — Why Is HER2 Testing Necessary?

## Patient Explanation

Before HER2-targeted therapy can be considered, the tumor must first be tested for HER2.

The test helps doctors determine whether the cancer has the biological feature required for this type of treatment.

Without HER2 testing, doctors cannot determine whether HER2-targeted therapy is appropriate.

---

## Clinical Importance

HER2 testing is the foundation for selecting HER2-targeted treatment.

---

## Key Concepts

- Test before treatment.
- HER2-positive is required.
- Biomarker-guided treatment.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — How Does HER2-targeted Therapy Work?

## Patient Explanation

HER2-targeted therapy works by recognizing and acting against cancer cells that express HER2.

Its goal is to interfere with cancer growth while helping direct treatment toward tumors with this specific biological characteristic.

The exact mechanism differs among individual medicines and is discussed in separate Population Packages.

---

## Clinical Importance

Targeted therapies are designed to act on specific biological characteristics of the tumor.

---

## Key Concepts

- Targets HER2-positive cancer cells.
- Different from traditional chemotherapy.
- Precision medicine approach.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — When May HER2-targeted Therapy Be Used?

## Patient Explanation

HER2-targeted therapy may be considered for patients whose tumors are confirmed to be HER2-positive.

Whether it is recommended depends on many factors, including:

- cancer stage;
- previous treatments;
- overall health;
- current clinical guidelines.

Your healthcare team will explain whether this treatment is appropriate for your individual situation.

---

## Clinical Importance

Treatment decisions are individualized according to both biomarker status and the overall clinical picture.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not appropriate for everyone.
- Requires individualized assessment.
- Depends on clinical context.

---

## Patient Explanation

For appropriate patients, HER2-targeted therapy may:

- improve treatment effectiveness;

The expected benefit differs from one patient to another.

---

## Clinical Importance

HER2-targeted therapy expands treatment options for selected patients.

---

## Key Concepts

- Additional treatment option.

# Knowledge Block 6 — What Are the Limitations?

## Patient Explanation

HER2-targeted therapy is not suitable for every patient.

It is generally considered only when:

- the tumor is HER2-positive;
- the overall clinical situation supports its use.

Even among HER2-positive patients, treatment recommendations may differ.

---

## Clinical Importance

Biomarker positivity alone does not determine the treatment plan.

---

## Key Concepts

- Not every patient is eligible.
- HER2-positive alone is not sufficient.
- Multiple clinical factors are considered.

---

# Knowledge Block 7 — Common Questions About HER2-targeted Therapy

## Patient Explanation

Some patients believe HER2-targeted therapy replaces all other cancer treatments.

In reality, HER2-targeted therapy is often one component of a broader treatment plan.

It may be combined with other treatments depending on the patient's condition.

---

## Common Misconception

**Myth**

HER2-targeted therapy works for every gastric cancer.

**Fact**

Only patients whose tumors are confirmed to be HER2-positive may be candidates, and treatment decisions remain individualized.

---

# Knowledge Block 8 — What Should Patients Expect?

Patients considering HER2-targeted therapy should discuss with their healthcare team:

- HER2 test results;
- treatment goals;
- expected benefits;
- possible limitations;
- available treatment options.

Shared decision-making helps ensure that treatment reflects both medical evidence and patient preferences.

---

## Clinical Importance

Patient education and shared decision-making are essential parts of precision oncology.

---

# Key Messages

- HER2-targeted therapy is personalized treatment for HER2-positive gastric cancer.
- HER2 testing is required before treatment can be considered.
- HER2-targeted therapy works differently from chemotherapy.
- Not every patient is eligible for this treatment.
- Treatment decisions depend on HER2 status and the overall clinical picture.
- Patients should discuss all treatment options with their healthcare team.
