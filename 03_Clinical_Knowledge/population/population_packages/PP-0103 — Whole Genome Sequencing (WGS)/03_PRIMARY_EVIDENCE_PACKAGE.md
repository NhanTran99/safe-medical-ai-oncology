# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS)** as comprehensive genomic sequencing that analyzes nearly the entire genome.

This Population Package introduces WGS as an advanced genomic testing application within molecular diagnostics while emphasizing that WGS is not routinely indicated for every patient and should be performed according to the clinical situation and professional guidelines.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- WGS evaluates nearly the entire genome.
- WGS is an application of NGS technology.
- WGS provides broader genomic information than targeted gene panel testing.
- WGS is used selectively according to the clinical indication.
- WGS results should always be interpreted together with other clinical findings.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently describe Whole Genome Sequencing as comprehensive genomic analysis capable of evaluating nearly the complete DNA sequence of a biological sample. They distinguish WGS from targeted genomic approaches by its breadth of genomic coverage while emphasizing that WGS is typically performed using Next-Generation Sequencing technology. Clinical guidance consistently recommends WGS only when appropriate for the cancer type, clinical question, available resources, and current evidence-based practice.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- terminology from NCI and NHGRI;
- oncology guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- molecular pathology principles from CAP and AMP;
- patient education principles from ACS;
- international guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
