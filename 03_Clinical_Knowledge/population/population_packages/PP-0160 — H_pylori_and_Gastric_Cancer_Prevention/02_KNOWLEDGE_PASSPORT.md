## Intended Runtime Usage

Use this PP when the user asks:

- Does H. pylori cause gastric cancer?
- Can treating H. pylori prevent gastric cancer?
- Does eradication help if I have no symptoms?
- Does family history change the prevention benefit?
- Does eradication still matter after early gastric cancer?
- Can eradication eliminate gastric-cancer risk?
- Why does timing of eradication matter?
- Why should not everyone automatically be treated?
- How is H. pylori prevention different from H. pylori eradication treatment?

Do not use this PP as a substitute for:

- diagnostic testing instructions;
- eradication regimens;
- resistance-guided therapy;
- individualized medical advice;
- detailed atrophic gastritis/intestinal metaplasia management.
