# Educational Intent

Secondary Goals

- Clarify the relationship between TNM and Stage I–IV.

# Core Concepts

This Population Package should explain:

1. Cancer staging describes how far the cancer has spread.
2. TNM is the internationally accepted staging framework.
3. T, N and M each describe different aspects of disease extent.
4. Stage I–IV is derived from TNM.
5. Treatment planning depends heavily on accurate staging.

# Clinical Safety Notes

This Population Package must clearly state:

- Cancer stage is determined through medical evaluation, not symptoms alone.
- A higher stage does not automatically mean that treatment is impossible.
- Staging guides treatment planning but is only one component of clinical decision-making.
- Patients should discuss their individual stage with their oncology team.
