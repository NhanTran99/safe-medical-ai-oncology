# Evidence Extraction Summary

## Core Concepts

- Cancer staging describes the extent of disease.
- TNM is the internationally accepted staging system.
- Stage grouping summarizes disease extent.
- Accurate staging is essential before treatment planning.

---

## TNM Components

Tumor (T)

- Extent of the primary tumor.

Node (N)

- Regional lymph node involvement.

Metastasis (M)

- Presence or absence of distant metastasis.

---

## Clinical Importance

Staging helps:

- Select appropriate treatment.
- Estimate prognosis.
- Standardize communication among healthcare professionals.
- Compare outcomes across studies.

---

## Key Patient Messages

Diagnosis

↓

Staging

↓

Treatment Planning

↓

Prognosis Discussion

Patients should understand that cancer stage is determined through multiple clinical assessments rather than symptoms alone.
