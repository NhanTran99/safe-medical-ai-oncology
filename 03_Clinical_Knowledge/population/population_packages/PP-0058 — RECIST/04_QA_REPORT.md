# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of RECIST;
- meaning of the acronym;
- purpose of standardized tumor response assessment;
- general role of imaging;
- distinction between RECIST, staging and treatment.

Excluded:

- RECIST 1.1;
- target lesions;
- measurable disease;
- CR/PR/SD/PD;
- iRECIST;
- treatment decisions;
- prognosis.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational concept of **RECIST**, allowing patients to understand why clinicians use a standardized language to describe changes in solid tumors over time while intentionally avoiding technical implementation details.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- RECIST 1.1
- Target Lesions
- Non-target Lesions
- Measurable Disease
- Complete Response (CR)
- Partial Response (PR)
- Stable Disease (SD)
- Progressive Disease (PD)
- Sum of Target Lesion Diameters
- iRECIST
- Imaging Assessment of Treatment Response

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
