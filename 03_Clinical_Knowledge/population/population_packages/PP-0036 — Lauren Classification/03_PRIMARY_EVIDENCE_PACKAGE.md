# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is the Lauren Classification and what should patients understand about it?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of the Lauren Classification, including why it is used, how it is determined from pathological examination, what the three major Lauren categories represent, and why the Lauren Classification should be viewed as one component of the overall assessment of gastric cancer.

This Population Package introduces the Lauren Classification without discussing detailed histologic criteria, prognostic implications or treatment decisions.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Lauren Classification describes the microscopic pattern of gastric adenocarcinoma.
- It is assigned by a pathologist after examining tissue.
- The three principal categories are intestinal, diffuse and mixed.
- Lauren Classification is included in the pathology report.
- Lauren Classification is different from TNM staging.
- Lauren Classification contributes to diagnosis but does not independently determine prognosis or treatment.
