# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is gastric cancer?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide newly diagnosed patients and caregivers with a clear, evidence-based explanation of gastric cancer before introducing risk factors, diagnosis, staging, treatment, or prognosis.

This package establishes the disease-specific foundation for all subsequent gastric cancer Population Packages.

---

# Scope

## Included

- Definition of gastric cancer
- Basic stomach anatomy
- Site of origin
- Major pathological types (high level)
- Difference between gastric cancer and common benign stomach diseases
- Importance of early detection

---

## Excluded

- Risk factors
- Helicobacter pylori
- Prevention
- Screening
- Symptoms
- Diagnosis
- Staging
- Prognosis
- Surgery
- Chemotherapy
- Radiotherapy
- Targeted therapy
- Immunotherapy

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Gastric cancer develops from abnormal cells in the stomach.
- Most gastric cancers begin in the stomach lining.
- Gastric adenocarcinoma is the predominant histological type.
- Many common stomach diseases are not cancer.
- Earlier detection is associated with better treatment opportunities.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Differences were limited to terminology depth and educational wording.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:
