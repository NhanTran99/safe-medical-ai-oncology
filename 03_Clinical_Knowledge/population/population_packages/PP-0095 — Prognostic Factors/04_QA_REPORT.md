# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the **foundational concept of prognosis** within the repository. It explains that prognostic factors estimate the likely course of disease using multiple complementary pieces of information rather than a single measurement. It serves as the conceptual bridge from **Prognostic Stage (PP-0094)** to future Population Packages covering **Predictive Factors**, **Prognostic Biomarkers**, **Risk Stratification**, and **Personalized Oncology**.
