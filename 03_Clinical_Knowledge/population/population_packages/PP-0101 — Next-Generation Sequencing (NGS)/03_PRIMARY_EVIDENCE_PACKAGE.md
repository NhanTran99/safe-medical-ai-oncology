# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What is Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)** as a modern molecular testing technology capable of analyzing multiple genes or genomic regions simultaneously.

This Population Package introduces NGS as an important technology within molecular diagnostics and precision oncology while emphasizing that it is one testing method among many and should be used according to the clinical situation.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- NGS is a high-throughput molecular testing technology.
- NGS analyzes many genes simultaneously.
- NGS supports biomarker testing and precision medicine.
- NGS should be used according to the clinical indication.
- NGS results are interpreted together with other clinical information.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently describe Next-Generation Sequencing as a powerful molecular diagnostic technology capable of evaluating multiple genomic regions in a single analysis. They emphasize that NGS is selected according to the clinical question, cancer type, and current evidence-based guidelines. NGS results should always be interpreted alongside pathology findings, imaging studies, cancer staging, and the patient's overall clinical context.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- terminology from NCI;
- oncology guidance from NCCN and ASCO;
- molecular pathology standards from CAP and AMP;
- patient education principles from ACS;
- international guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
