# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package introduces **BP5**, one of the individual **Supporting Benign Evidence** criteria within the ACMG/AMP framework.

The package intentionally emphasizes the critical distinction between **finding another pathogenic variant** and **identifying another molecular basis that convincingly explains the patient's disease**. The latter is the clinically meaningful concept underlying BP5.

The package also preserves the ACMG/AMP caution that BP5 may be less informative in recessive disorders and in disorders where multiple variants can contribute to disease. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

Disease-specific ClinGen expert-panel specifications may further restrict, modify, or prohibit BP5 in particular contexts; therefore, such implementation rules remain outside this foundational patient-education package. :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

The package therefore maintains strict adherence to the **Atomic Knowledge Principle** while preserving the clinically important safety boundary around BP5.
