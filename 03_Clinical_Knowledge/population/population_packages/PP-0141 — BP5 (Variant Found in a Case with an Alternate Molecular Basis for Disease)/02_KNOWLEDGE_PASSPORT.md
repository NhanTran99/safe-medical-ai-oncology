**Reason:**

Patients undergoing genomic testing may have more than one potentially relevant genetic finding. This Population Package explains how an **alternate molecular basis for disease** may contribute **Supporting Benign Evidence** for another variant, while emphasizing that the alternative finding must actually provide a convincing explanation for the patient's disease.

## Retrieval Priority

**Very High**

**Reason:**

BP5 introduces an important context-dependent principle in benign variant interpretation: an alternative molecular diagnosis may reduce the likelihood that the evaluated variant explains the phenotype, but only when the alternative cause is genuinely capable of explaining the disease.
