# Evidence Traceability Review

Each major clinical claim is linked to an approved source family in the Primary Evidence Package.

Particular attention was given to the following high-risk claims:

1. Blood/plasma can provide tumor-derived molecular information.
2. Liquid biopsy may be considered when tissue is limited or traditional biopsy cannot be performed.
3. Liquid biopsy may identify targetable alterations.
4. Liquid biopsy may provide information about tumor-clone evolution.
5. A negative liquid-biopsy result does not exclude tumor presence.

These claims remain within the evidence-supported scope.
