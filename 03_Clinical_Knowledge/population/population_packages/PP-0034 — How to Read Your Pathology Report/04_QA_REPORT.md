The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

- purpose of pathology reports;
- general report structure;
- common report sections;
- importance of the Final Diagnosis section;
- report updates;
- communication with the healthcare team.

Excluded:

- interpretation of pathology terminology;
- histologic subtype;
- tumor grading;
- biomarker interpretation;
- molecular pathology;
- treatment planning.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
