# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> What is gastric adenocarcinoma?

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients with a clear understanding of gastric adenocarcinoma, the most common histological type of stomach cancer, before introducing pathology classification, biomarkers, staging or treatment.

---

# Scope

## Included

- Definition of gastric adenocarcinoma
- Cell of origin
- Relationship with gastric cancer
- Other major gastric malignancies (overview only)
- Clinical importance of histopathological diagnosis

---

## Excluded

- Lauren classification
- WHO histological classification
- Tumor grading
- Molecular classification
- Biomarker testing
- Pathology report interpretation
- Treatment implications

# Clinical Claims Summary

Current evidence consistently supports that:

- Gastric adenocarcinoma is the predominant histological subtype.
- The tumor develops from glandular epithelial cells.
- Histological confirmation is required before treatment planning.
- Other stomach malignancies exist but are much less common.
