# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of Stable Disease;
- Stable Disease as a standardized treatment response category;
- explanation that disease stability may represent treatment benefit;
- distinction from Partial Response and Progressive Disease;
- importance of continued monitoring and comprehensive assessment.

Excluded:

- RECIST technical criteria;
- percentage thresholds;
- Progressive Disease;
- response algorithms;
- recurrence risk;
- prognosis;
- treatment strategy after Stable Disease.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational understanding of **Stable Disease**, with particular emphasis on correcting the common misconception that Stable Disease automatically means treatment is ineffective. It reinforces that maintaining disease stability may itself represent an important therapeutic objective, especially in advanced cancer.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- Progressive Disease (PD)
- RECIST Response Algorithm
- Treatment Response Monitoring
- Cancer Recurrence

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
