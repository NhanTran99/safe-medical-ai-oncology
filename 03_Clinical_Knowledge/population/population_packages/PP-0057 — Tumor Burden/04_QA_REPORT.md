# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of tumor burden;
- explanation that it reflects the overall amount of cancer present in the body;
- recognition that it is a clinical concept;
- explanation that no universal measurement exists;
- importance of comprehensive clinical evaluation.

Excluded:

- RECIST;
- target lesion measurements;
- volumetric assessment;
- PET quantitative parameters;
- ctDNA;
- tumor markers;
- treatment;
- prognosis.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.

# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational educational concept of **tumor burden**, enabling patients to understand a commonly used oncology term without introducing technical measurement systems or treatment-response frameworks.

Future Population Packages should expand on:

- How Is Tumor Burden Measured?
- RECIST
- Target Lesions
- Volumetric Tumor Assessment
- PET Tumor Volume
- Tumor Markers and Disease Burden
- ctDNA Monitoring
- Monitoring Response to Treatment
- Prognostic Significance of Tumor Burden

without expanding or modifying the scope of this foundational package.
