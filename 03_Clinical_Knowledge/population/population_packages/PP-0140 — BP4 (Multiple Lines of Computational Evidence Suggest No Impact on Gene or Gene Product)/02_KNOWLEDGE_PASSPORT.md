**Reason:**

Patients reviewing genomic laboratory reports may encounter **BP4** during variant interpretation. This Population Package explains, in patient-friendly language, how **multiple computational prediction methods** suggesting that a variant has **no meaningful impact on a gene or its protein** may contribute **Supporting Benign Evidence**, while emphasizing that BP4 alone cannot establish benign classification.
