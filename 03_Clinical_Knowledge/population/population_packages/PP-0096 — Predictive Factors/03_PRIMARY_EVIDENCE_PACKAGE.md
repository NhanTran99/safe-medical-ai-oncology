# Clinical Question

**Primary Educational Question**

> **What are predictive factors?**

---

# Educational Intent

Provide patients and caregivers with an accurate, evidence-based explanation of **predictive factors** as characteristics associated with the likelihood of benefit from a **specific treatment**.

This Population Package explains how predictive factors differ from prognostic factors, introduces common categories of predictive factors, and emphasizes that predictive factors guide—but do not guarantee—treatment outcomes.

# Clinical Claims Summary

The evidence consistently supports the following educational messages:

- Predictive factors help estimate response to specific therapies.
- Predictive factors differ from prognostic factors.
- Predictive factors are often based on biological characteristics of the tumor.
- Predictive factors support personalized treatment selection.
- Predictive factors do not guarantee individual treatment outcomes.

---

# Evidence Consistency Review

No clinically meaningful disagreement was identified among the primary references.

Major oncology organizations consistently distinguish predictive factors from prognostic factors and emphasize their role in selecting appropriate therapies rather than estimating the natural course of disease. They further recognize predictive factors as a cornerstone of precision oncology while acknowledging that treatment response remains probabilistic.

The Clinical Knowledge Object follows:

- terminology from NCI;
- treatment selection principles from NCCN and ASCO;
- patient education principles from ACS;
- international oncology guidance consistent with ESMO and JNCCN.
