# Knowledge Block 1 — What Are Predictive Factors?

## Patient Explanation

**Predictive factors** are characteristics that help doctors estimate **how likely a specific treatment is to work**.

They help guide treatment selection but cannot guarantee that a treatment will or will not be successful.

---

## Clinical Importance

Predictive factors estimate the likelihood of benefit from specific therapies.

---

## Key Concepts

- Treatment response.
- Treatment selection.
- Personalized care.

---

# Knowledge Block 2 — How Are Predictive Factors Different from Prognostic Factors?

## Patient Explanation

Although the names sound similar, they answer different questions.

- **Prognostic factors** help estimate how a disease may behave over time.
- **Predictive factors** help estimate whether a particular treatment is likely to be beneficial.

Both are important, but they serve different purposes.

---

## Clinical Importance

Predictive and prognostic factors provide complementary clinical information.

---

## Key Concepts

- Prognosis.
- Treatment benefit.
- Different clinical roles.

---

# Knowledge Block 3 — What Types of Predictive Factors Are Used?

## Patient Explanation

Depending on the type of cancer, predictive factors may include:

- biomarkers;
- genetic alterations;
- protein expression;
- other biological characteristics of the tumor.

Not every cancer uses the same predictive factors.

---

## Clinical Importance

Predictive factors are disease-specific and treatment-specific.

---

## Key Concepts

- Biomarkers.
- Molecular characteristics.
- Tumor biology.

---

# Knowledge Block 4 — Why Are Predictive Factors Important?

## Patient Explanation

Predictive factors help doctors:

- choose treatments that are more likely to help;
- avoid treatments that are unlikely to provide benefit;
- support personalized cancer care.

They are one part of the information used when making treatment decisions.

---

## Clinical Importance

Predictive factors support evidence-based treatment selection.

---

## Key Concepts

- Personalized medicine.
- Treatment planning.
- Evidence-based care.

---

# Knowledge Block 5 — Do Predictive Factors Guarantee Treatment Success?

## Patient Explanation

No.

Even when a predictive factor suggests that a treatment is likely to work, no test can guarantee an individual outcome.

Doctors interpret predictive factors together with many other aspects of your health and your cancer.

---

## Clinical Importance

Predictive factors estimate probability rather than certainty.

---

## Key Concepts

- Probability.
- Individual variation.
- Clinical judgment.

---

# Common Misconception

**Myth**

A positive predictive factor means a treatment will definitely work.

**Fact**

Predictive factors estimate the likelihood of benefit, but they cannot guarantee how an individual patient will respond.

---

# Key Messages

- Predictive factors help estimate whether a treatment is likely to work.
- Predictive factors are different from prognostic factors.
- Different cancers use different predictive factors.
- Predictive factors support personalized treatment planning.
- Predictive factors provide guidance, not certainty.
