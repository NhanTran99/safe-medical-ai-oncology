## Intended Runtime Usage

Use this package when the user asks:
- what gastric intestinal metaplasia means;
- whether intestinal metaplasia is cancer;
- whether intestinal metaplasia increases gastric-cancer risk;
- how IM relates to H. pylori or atrophic gastritis;
- why biopsy may detect IM;
- whether IM always progresses to cancer;
- why follow-up may be considered.

Do not use this package as a detailed surveillance, biopsy, histopathology, H. pylori-treatment, or gastric-cancer-treatment resource.

## Evidence Classification

### Established / directly supported
- Intestinal metaplasia is a gastric mucosal change.
- H. pylori is the most common risk factor for IM in the cited NCI material.
- IM is associated with increased gastric-cancer risk.
- IM occurs within Correa's cascade.
- IM is not always visible on endoscopy and may be identified by biopsy.

### Context-dependent / nuanced
- H. pylori eradication after IM.
- Appropriate surveillance.
- Relative importance of additional risk factors.
- Extent/location-specific risk.

### Not established by the supplied source set
- Universal individual absolute risk.
- Universal surveillance interval.
- Reliable reversal of IM after H. pylori eradication.
- Complete/incomplete IM risk algorithm.
- OLGA/OLGIM-based patient decision algorithm.
