# Educational Boundary Review

The Population Package successfully remains within the predefined educational boundary.

Included:

- definition of metastasis;
- definition of the primary tumor;
- distinction between primary and metastatic tumors;
- relationship between metastasis and the M category;
- relationship between metastasis and cancer staging;
- importance of appropriate medical evaluation.

Excluded:

- M0–M1 classifications;
- organ-specific metastases;
- biological mechanisms;
- metastatic pathways;
- imaging investigations;
- AJCC Stage Groups;
- stage-specific prognosis;
- treatment recommendations.

The **Atomic Knowledge Principle** has been fully preserved.
