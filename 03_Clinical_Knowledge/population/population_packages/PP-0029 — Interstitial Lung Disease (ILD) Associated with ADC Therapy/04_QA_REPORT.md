# Scope Verification

This Population Package intentionally focuses only on **interstitial lung disease (ILD) associated with some antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs)** from a patient education and treatment safety perspective.

The following topics are intentionally excluded and delegated to future Population Packages:

- CTCAE grading
- HRCT interpretation
- Bronchoscopy
- Lung pathology
- ILD pathophysiology
- Incidence statistics
- Risk prediction models
- Corticosteroid treatment
- Drug rechallenge algorithms
- Drug-specific comparisons
- Clinical trial safety analyses

This satisfies the Gold Population Package principle of **Atomic Knowledge (Single Educational Question).**

# Reviewer Notes

This package establishes the foundational patient education for one of the most clinically important serious toxicities associated with selected ADC therapies before introducing dedicated Population Packages covering:

- Diagnosis of ADC-associated ILD
- Imaging Evaluation of ILD
- Corticosteroid Therapy
- Dose Modification and Treatment Interruption
- Rechallenge After ILD
- Drug-specific ILD Profiles

The package appropriately emphasizes:

- early symptom recognition;
- prompt communication with the healthcare team;
- avoidance of self-management;
- the importance of routine monitoring throughout treatment.

Educational scope, evidence traceability and governance metadata are complete.
