# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the foundational understanding of **serial imaging during cancer care**. It bridges the concepts of RECIST response assessment with future educational packages explaining individual imaging modalities (CT, MRI and PET/CT). The package emphasizes that repeated imaging provides longitudinal information supporting clinical decision-making without implying that imaging alone determines treatment.
