# Reviewer Notes

This Population Package establishes the **foundational health literacy** needed for understanding radiology reports. It explains that CT reports are professional communication documents rather than stand-alone diagnoses, reinforcing the importance of multidisciplinary interpretation and clinician-patient discussion. It provides the foundation for future Population Packages covering **common CT terminology**, **incidental findings**, **staging language**, and **discussion of imaging results with healthcare professionals**.
