## Knowledge Units

### KU-01 — Definition

Peritoneal-only metastatic disease is gastric cancer metastatic to the peritoneal compartment without documented extraperitoneal metastases.

### KU-02 — Positive Cytology

Positive peritoneal cytology is included in the NCCN peritoneal-only pathway.

### KU-03 — Exclusion of Extraperitoneal Disease

The absence of documented extraperitoneal disease is a defining pathway condition.

### KU-04 — Baseline Workup

The pathway incorporates clinical assessment, CT, selected laparoscopy, pathology review and selected additional investigations.

### KU-05 — Initial Systemic Therapy

Systemic therapy is the initial treatment backbone.

### KU-06 — Minimum Three-Month Framework

NCCN v2.2026 specifies systemic therapy for a minimum of 3 months before restaging.

### KU-07 — Restaging

Restaging includes CT and diagnostic laparoscopy with washings and documented PCI ± biopsy, with selected PET/CT and EGD.

### KU-08 — Response

Stable/improved disease supports the favorable pathway; progression moves the patient away from the selected local-treatment branch.

### KU-09 — PCI

PCI measures peritoneal disease burden and distribution.

### KU-10 — PCI ≤10

Lower-burden category used in the current NCCN pathway.

### KU-11 — PCI >10

Higher-burden category associated with the non-favorable branch.

### KU-12 — Complete Cytoreduction

Predicted complete cytoreduction is a major condition for the selected surgical/HIPEC branch.

### KU-13 — Incomplete Cytoreduction

If complete cytoreduction is not predicted, NCCN identifies clinical trial or continued systemic therapy rather than automatic surgery.

### KU-14 — HIPEC

HIPEC is a selected downstream treatment, not an automatic consequence of peritoneal-only disease.

### KU-15 — Clinical Trials

Clinical trials are integrated into multiple branches of the pathway.

### KU-16 — Best Supportive Care

Best supportive care is a possible pathway destination in high-burden/progressive/extraperitoneal disease.

### KU-17 — Multidisciplinary Assessment

Selected local treatment requires multidisciplinary assessment.

## Evidence Maturity

### Established / Guideline-Supported

- Peritoneal-only disease is a dedicated NCCN pathway.
- Positive cytology is included.
- Systemic therapy precedes restaging.
- Minimum 3-month systemic-treatment framework.
- CT/restaging.
- Laparoscopy with washings and PCI.
- Absence of extraperitoneal disease.
- PCI ≤10 versus >10 pathway distinction.
- Complete cytoreduction as a selection factor.
- Multidisciplinary consultation.

### Selected / Evolving

- Cytoreductive surgery.
- Gastrectomy in selected metastatic patients.
- IC/HIPEC.
