1. PURPOSE

This document defines the strategic prioritization framework governing the order in which Clinical Knowledge Population activities are executed.

It ensures that repository growth is driven by clinical value, patient safety, evidence maturity, and governance readiness rather than implementation convenience or repository size.

2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Knowledge population prioritization follows the principles of:

Clinical Value First
Patient Safety First
Evidence-driven Expansion
Governance-guided Prioritization
Progressive Repository Growth
Strategic Resource Allocation
Amendment-first Evolution

Knowledge population shall maximize clinical impact before repository completeness.

3. ROLE

This framework governs:

prioritization strategy;
priority hierarchy;
population waves;
batch selection;
re-prioritization.

It does not define:

repository organization;
execution workflow;
evidence governance;
runtime retrieval.
4. PRIMARY PRIORITIZATION PRINCIPLE

The primary criterion for all knowledge population activities shall be Clinical Value.

Clinical Value represents the expected benefit of a Population Package to patient education, clinical safety, and evidence-based communication.

Implementation effort, document quantity, or development convenience shall not determine prioritization.

5. PRIORITY HIERARCHY

Population activities shall follow the standardized priority hierarchy.

Priority Level 1

Safety-critical clinical topics

↓

Priority Level 2

High-frequency patient questions

↓

Priority Level 3

Guideline-driven standard care

↓

Priority Level 4

High-impact treatment pathways

↓

Priority Level 5

Supportive care

↓

Priority Level 6

Rare diseases and specialized topics

Lower-priority knowledge shall not delay development of higher-value clinical content.

6. POPULATION ROADMAP

Repository expansion follows progressive Population Waves.

Wave 1

Core Oncology Patient Education

Focus:

foundational concepts;
diagnosis;
treatment overview;
common toxicities;
patient safety.
Wave 2

Disease-specific Knowledge

Focus:

cancer-specific education;
staging;
biomarkers;
treatment pathways.
Wave 3

Advanced Clinical Knowledge

Focus:

survivorship;
supportive care;
complex decision support;
multidisciplinary care.
Wave 4

Specialized and Emerging Topics

Focus:

rare conditions;
novel therapies;
rapidly evolving evidence;
future expansion.

Each wave builds upon the governance foundation established in previous waves.

7. BATCH PRIORITIZATION RULE

Population Batches shall be selected using the following governance criteria:

clinical priority;
patient impact;
evidence maturity;
repository completeness;
governance readiness.

Batch selection shall remain evidence-driven and organizationally transparent.

8. RE-PRIORITIZATION POLICY

Population priorities may be revised following:

publication of updated clinical guidelines;
emergence of significant new evidence;
safety alerts;
governance decisions;
strategic organizational priorities.

Every prioritization change shall be documented through governed amendments and remain fully traceable.

9. GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES

Knowledge population prioritization shall maintain:

explicit prioritization rationale;
transparent governance decisions;
evidence-based sequencing;
patient-centered focus;
continuous strategic alignment;
organizational consistency.

Strategic prioritization shall remain dynamic while preserving governance integrity.

10. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every prioritization decision shall satisfy:

documented clinical justification;
governance approval;
consistency with repository strategy;
alignment with evidence maturity;
traceable decision records;
compatibility with Population Waves.

Only approved priorities shall guide repository expansion.
