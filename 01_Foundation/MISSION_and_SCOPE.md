# 1. PURPOSE

This document formally defines the mission, vision, objectives, scope, target users, and boundaries of the project.

It serves as the highest-level specification describing what the project intends to achieve and, equally importantly, what it intentionally does not attempt to do.

All downstream architecture, implementation, and evaluation documents shall remain consistent with this document.

---

# 2. PROJECT MISSION

Develop an evidence-based, safe, explainable oncology patient assistant that bridges clinical guidelines and patient education through trustworthy AI.

---

# 3. PROJECT GOAL

Design and implement a Safe Medical AI System for Oncology Patient Education.

The system should demonstrate how modern AI can improve patient education while maintaining patient safety, transparency, and adherence to evidence-based medicine.

---

# 4. PROJECT VISION

To establish a research-grade medical AI platform that demonstrates responsible use of Large Language Models in oncology patient education.

The project aims to become:

- a strong PhD portfolio project;
- a reproducible research platform;
- a clinically responsible AI system;
- a foundation for future expansion into additional cancer types and medical specialties.

---

# 5. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

## Primary Objectives

- Deliver trustworthy oncology patient education.
- Improve patient understanding of cancer and its management.
- Explain clinical guidelines in language understandable to patients.
- Promote safe and responsible use of AI in medicine.

---

## Research Objectives

- Design an explainable medical AI architecture.
- Develop a robust medical governance framework.
- Demonstrate evidence-grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
- Establish a reproducible software architecture suitable for research dissemination.

---

## Engineering Objectives

- Build a maintainable and modular software system.
- Support long-term extensibility.
- Maintain documentation-driven development.
- Enable collaboration between multiple AI agents through governed documentation.

---

# 6. PROJECT SCOPE

## In Scope

The system is intended to provide:

- Oncology patient education.
- Evidence-based oncology knowledge.
- Clinical guideline explanation.
- Educational explanations of cancer symptoms.
- Educational explanations of treatment modalities.
- Medical terminology explanation.
- Evidence citation and explainable responses.

---

## Out of Scope

The system shall not:

- diagnose diseases;
- recommend personalized treatment;
- prescribe medications;
- perform shared decision support;
- replace emergency medical services;
- replace physician consultation;
- function as an autonomous clinical decision-making system.

---

# 7. TARGET USERS

## Primary Users

- Cancer patients.
- Family members and caregivers.

These users represent the primary design target for user interface, response style, and educational content.

---

## Secondary Users

- Medical students.
- Junior healthcare professionals.
- Researchers evaluating the system.

Secondary users may benefit from the system for educational and research purposes, but the platform is not primarily designed for professional clinical decision support.

---

# 8. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The project is guided by the following philosophy.

Evidence before opinion.

Safety before completeness.

Education before recommendation.

Transparency before automation.

Support before replacement.

These principles shall guide future architectural and implementation decisions.

---

# 9. SUCCESS CRITERIA

The project will be considered successful if it demonstrates:

- safe oncology patient education;
- evidence-based response generation;
- explainable AI behavior;
- maintainable software architecture;
- research-grade documentation;
- potential for future clinical validation.

---

# 10. PROJECT BOUNDARIES

The project is intentionally limited to oncology patient education.

Expansion into diagnosis support, treatment planning, or physician-facing decision support requires separate governance, validation, and documentation and is outside the scope of the current project.
