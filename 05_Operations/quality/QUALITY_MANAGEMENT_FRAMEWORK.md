# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Quality Management Framework of the Safe Medical AI System.

The framework establishes the organizational Quality Management System (QMS) governing clinical quality, patient safety, governance integrity, operational excellence, documentation quality, and continuous organizational improvement.

---

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Quality Management follows the principles of:

- Quality by Design
- Governance-first quality
- Patient safety first
- Organizational accountability
- Continuous quality improvement
- Technology independence

---

# 3. ROLE

The Quality Management Framework is responsible for:

- governing organizational quality
- defining quality objectives
- coordinating quality reviews
- governing nonconformities
- monitoring quality performance
- supporting organizational governance

The framework is not responsible for:

- runtime execution
- deployment
- implementation
- software development

---

# 4. QUALITY PHILOSOPHY

Quality Management governs the complete Quality Management System.

Quality shall be designed into every organizational process rather than verified only after implementation.

Quality governance spans the complete lifecycle of the Safe Medical AI System.

---

# 5. QUALITY SCOPE

Quality governance covers:

- Clinical Knowledge
- System Architecture
- Governance Frameworks
- Runtime Pipeline
- Operational Processes
- Documentation

Additional organizational domains may be incorporated through governed amendments.

---

# 6. QUALITY OBJECTIVES

Standard organizational quality objectives include:

- Clinical Accuracy
- Patient Safety
- Governance Compliance
- Explainability
- Reliability
- Maintainability

Quality objectives shall remain measurable and periodically reviewed.

---

# 7. QUALITY PLANNING

Every project phase shall define:

- Quality Objectives
- Acceptance Criteria
- Review Plan

Quality planning shall precede implementation activities.

---

# 8. QUALITY REVIEWS

Standard Quality Reviews include:

- Architecture Review
- Clinical Review
- Governance Review
- Documentation Review
- Release Review

Additional review types may be introduced through governance approval.

---

# 9. QUALITY RECORD

Every Quality Management activity shall generate a standardized Quality Record.

Minimum metadata include:

- Quality Identifier
- Activity Type
- Reviewer
- Status
- Timestamp

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 10. NONCONFORMITY MANAGEMENT

Every nonconformity shall follow the standardized lifecycle.

Identify

↓

Record

↓

Assess

↓

Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA)

↓

Verification

↓

Closure

Nonconformities shall never be closed without verification.

---

# 11. QUALITY METRICS

Minimum organizational quality metrics include:

- Incident Rate
- Validation Pass Rate
- Release Success Rate
- Documentation Completeness
- CAPA Completion Rate

Additional quality metrics may be governed through formal amendments.

---

# 12. MANAGEMENT REVIEW

Management Review shall be conducted periodically.

Minimum review inputs include:

- Quality Metrics
- Incident Trends
- Improvement Progress
- Release Performance

Management Review supports strategic organizational governance.

---

# 13. QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM INTEGRATION

The Quality Management Framework governs integration across:

- Monitoring Framework
- Observability Framework
- Incident Management Policy
- System Evaluation Framework
- Release Policy
- Continuous Improvement Framework

The Quality Management Framework serves as the umbrella governance framework for the organizational Quality Management System.

---

# 14. ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

The Quality Management Framework follows:

- Quality by Design
- Governance-first quality
- Organizational accountability
- Continuous improvement
- Traceability
- Separation of concerns
- Technology independence
