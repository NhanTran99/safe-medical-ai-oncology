# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Risk Management Framework of the Safe Medical AI System.

The framework establishes standardized governance for identifying, evaluating, controlling, monitoring, and reviewing organizational risks throughout the complete system lifecycle while preserving patient safety, governance integrity, and technology independence.

---

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Risk Management follows the principles of:

- Risk-informed governance
- Patient safety first
- Preventive governance
- Continuous risk management
- Evidence-based decision making
- Technology independence

---

# 3. ROLE

The Risk Management Framework is responsible for:

- identifying organizational risks
- evaluating risk significance
- governing risk controls
- maintaining the Risk Register
- monitoring residual risks
- supporting strategic governance

The framework is not responsible for:

- incident execution
- runtime monitoring
- release approval
- implementation activities

---

# 4. RISK MANAGEMENT PHILOSOPHY

Risk Management is a continuous governance process.

Its objective is to proactively identify, evaluate, control, monitor, and review risks before they become operational incidents.

Risk governance spans the complete organizational lifecycle.

---

# 5. RISK SCOPE

Governed risk domains include:

- Clinical Risk
- Patient Safety Risk
- Knowledge Risk
- AI Behavior Risk
- Operational Risk
- Governance Risk
- Organizational Risk

Additional risk domains may be incorporated through governed amendments.

---

# 6. RISK LIFECYCLE

Every governed risk follows the standardized lifecycle.

Identify

↓

Analyze

↓

Evaluate

↓

Control

↓

Monitor

↓

Review

↓

Retire

Lifecycle transitions shall remain fully traceable.

---

# 7. RISK CLASSIFICATION

Every governed risk shall be evaluated using:

- Likelihood
- Impact

Standard risk levels include:

- Critical
- High
- Medium
- Low

Risk classification shall remain independent of technical severity alone.

---

# 8. GOVERNED RISK REGISTER

Every governed risk shall be maintained within the Risk Register.

Minimum metadata include:

- Risk Identifier
- Risk Category
- Description
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Risk Level
- Risk Owner
- Current Status

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 9. RISK CONTROLS

Every governed risk shall maintain at least one approved control strategy.

Illustrative control categories include:

- Preventive Controls
- Detective Controls
- Corrective Controls

Risk controls shall remain traceable to their corresponding risks.

---

# 10. RISK REVIEW

Governed risks shall undergo periodic review.

Minimum review activities include:

- Risk Level Review
- Control Effectiveness Review
- Emerging Risk Assessment
- Residual Risk Assessment

Risk reviews support continuous governance.

---

# 11. RISK RECORD

Every Risk Management activity shall generate a standardized Risk Record.

Minimum metadata include:

- Risk Identifier
- Activity Type
- Reviewer
- Timestamp
- Outcome

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 12. RISK MANAGEMENT INDEPENDENCE

Risk Management remains independent from:

- Incident Management
- Monitoring Framework
- Release Governance

These frameworks provide governed inputs but do not replace Risk Management responsibilities.

---

# 13. ENTERPRISE RISK GOVERNANCE

Risk Management provides governed information supporting:

- Organizational Governance
- Quality Management
- Release Governance
- Strategic Planning

Risk acceptance remains the responsibility of the designated Governance Authority.

---

# 14. ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

The Risk Management Framework follows:

- Risk-informed governance
- Preventive governance
- Patient safety first
- Continuous assessment
- Traceability
- Separation of concerns
- Technology independence
