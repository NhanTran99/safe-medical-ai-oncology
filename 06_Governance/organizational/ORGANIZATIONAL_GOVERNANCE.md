# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Organizational Governance Framework of the Safe Medical AI System.

The framework establishes the highest level of governance responsible for preserving strategic direction, governance integrity, organizational continuity, quality oversight, and long-term sustainability while remaining independent of implementation technology.

---

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Organizational Governance follows the principles of:

- Governance-first leadership
- Strategic direction
- Organizational accountability
- Patient safety
- Long-term sustainability
- Technology independence

---

# 3. ROLE

The Organizational Governance Framework is responsible for:

- defining organizational strategy
- governing organizational policies
- approving governance direction
- overseeing quality management
- governing organizational risks
- preserving institutional knowledge
- ensuring long-term organizational continuity

The framework is not responsible for:

- runtime execution
- software implementation
- deployment operations
- incident execution
- monitoring activities

---

# 4. ORGANIZATIONAL GOVERNANCE PHILOSOPHY

Organizational Governance represents the highest governance authority of the Safe Medical AI System.

Its primary responsibility is to preserve strategic direction, governance integrity, organizational accountability, and sustainable organizational evolution throughout the complete system lifecycle.

---

# 5. GOVERNANCE SCOPE

Organizational Governance governs:

- Organizational Strategy
- Quality Management
- Clinical Governance
- Architecture Governance
- Operational Governance
- Organizational Risk and Compliance

The governance scope extends across the complete organizational lifecycle.

---

# 6. GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY

Organizational Governance defines standardized governance authorities.

Illustrative authorities include:

- Project Coordinator
- Clinical Governance Lead
- Quality Authority
- Executive Governance Board

Specific organizational structures may vary while governance responsibilities remain standardized.

---

# 7. GOVERNANCE RESPONSIBILITIES

Organizational Governance is responsible for:

- Strategic Direction
- Governance Approval
- Quality Oversight
- Resource Prioritization
- Risk Acceptance
- Policy Approval

Governance responsibilities remain independent from operational execution.

---

# 8. GOVERNANCE DECISION RECORD

Every organizational governance decision shall generate a standardized Governance Decision Record.

Minimum metadata include:

- Governance Decision Identifier
- Governance Authority
- Decision
- Rationale
- Effective Date

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 9. GOVERNANCE REVIEW MEETINGS

Standardized governance review meetings include:

- Strategic Review
- Quality Review
- Release Review
- Risk Review

Meeting outputs shall be formally governed and traceable.

---

# 10. GOVERNANCE ESCALATION

The standardized governance escalation hierarchy is:

Operational Governance

↓

Quality Governance

↓

Executive Governance

↓

Organizational Governance Decision

Governance escalation shall preserve accountability and traceability.

---

# 11. GOVERNANCE PERFORMANCE

Organizational Governance shall monitor:

- Governance KPIs
- Quality KPIs
- Strategic KPIs
- Improvement KPIs

Performance indicators support organizational oversight rather than runtime management.

---

# 12. GOVERNANCE INDEPENDENCE

Organizational Governance remains independent from:

- Development
- Deployment
- Runtime Execution
- Infrastructure Operations

Organizational Governance governs organizational direction without participating in implementation activities.

---

# 13. GOVERNANCE CONTINUITY

Organizational Governance preserves continuity of:

- Vision
- Mission
- System Architecture
- Governance Policies
- Organizational Knowledge
- Organizational Learning

Continuity shall be maintained across all releases, organizational changes, and future system evolution.

---

# 14. ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

The Organizational Governance Framework follows:

- Governance-first leadership
- Strategic oversight
- Organizational accountability
- Long-term sustainability
- Traceability
- Separation of governance and execution
- Technology independence
