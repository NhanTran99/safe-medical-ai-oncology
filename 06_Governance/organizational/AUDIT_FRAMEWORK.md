# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Audit Framework of the Safe Medical AI System.

The Audit Framework establishes independent governance verification across the complete organizational governance system, ensuring compliance, traceability, quality assurance, and continuous organizational accountability while remaining independent of implementation technology.

---

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Audit Framework follows the principles of:

- Independent verification
- Governance-first assurance
- Evidence-based auditing
- Organizational accountability
- Continuous compliance
- Technology independence

---

# 3. ROLE

The Audit Framework is responsible for:

- conducting independent audits
- evaluating governance compliance
- verifying organizational processes
- identifying nonconformities
- supporting CAPA verification
- providing executive assurance

The framework is not responsible for:

- operational execution
- runtime monitoring
- implementation
- governance decision making

---

# 4. AUDIT PHILOSOPHY

Audit provides independent governance verification.

The objective is to determine whether organizational activities conform to approved governance frameworks rather than evaluating operational performance alone.

Audit functions independently from operational execution.

---

# 5. AUDIT SCOPE

Audit encompasses the complete organizational governance system.

Illustrative audit scope includes:

- Foundation Documents
- System Architecture
- Knowledge Governance
- Runtime Pipeline
- Operational Governance
- Quality Management

Additional organizational domains may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 6. AUDIT TYPES

Standardized audit types include:

- Internal Audit
- Release Audit
- Clinical Audit
- Governance Audit
- Documentation Audit

Additional audit classifications, including Regulatory Audit, may be introduced through governance approval.

---

# 7. AUDIT CRITERIA

Audit shall evaluate compliance only against approved governance criteria.

Illustrative criteria include:

- Policy Compliance
- Documentation Completeness
- Traceability
- Version Integrity
- Process Compliance

Subjective audit criteria are prohibited.

---

# 8. AUDIT EVIDENCE

Every Audit Finding shall be supported by governed evidence.

Approved evidence sources include:

- Governance Records
- Quality Records
- Monitoring Records
- Evaluation Records
- Incident Records

Audit conclusions shall remain evidence-based.

---

# 9. AUDIT FINDINGS

Audit Findings shall be classified using standardized categories:

- Conformity
- Observation
- Minor Nonconformity
- Major Nonconformity

Finding classifications shall support standardized CAPA governance.

---

# 10. AUDIT RECORD

Every Audit shall generate a standardized Audit Record.

Minimum metadata include:

- Audit Identifier
- Audit Type
- Audit Scope
- Auditor
- Audit Date
- Findings
- Status

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 11. AUDITOR INDEPENDENCE

Auditors shall remain independent from audited activities.

Self-audit within the same governance scope is prohibited.

Auditor independence supports objective organizational assurance.

---

# 12. AUDIT FOLLOW-UP

Every Major Nonconformity shall complete:

Finding

↓

CAPA

↓

Verification

↓

Closure

Audit shall not be closed until mandatory follow-up activities are completed.

---

# 13. ORGANIZATIONAL ASSURANCE

The Audit Framework provides independent assurance to:

- Governance Authority
- Quality Management
- Release Governance
- Executive Management

Audit supports governance decisions while remaining independent from governance approval authority.

---

# 14. ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

The Audit Framework follows:

- Independent assurance
- Governance-first verification
- Evidence-based auditing
- Organizational accountability
- Traceability
- Separation of duties
- Technology independence
