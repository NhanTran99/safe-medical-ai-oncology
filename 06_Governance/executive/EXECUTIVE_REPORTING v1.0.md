# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Executive Reporting Framework of the Safe Medical AI System.

The framework establishes standardized executive governance reporting for communicating organizational performance, governance status, quality, risks, and strategic priorities using governed organizational evidence while preserving traceability, accountability, and technology independence.

---

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Executive Reporting follows the principles of:

- Governance-first communication
- Executive decision support
- Evidence-based reporting
- Organizational transparency
- Standardized reporting
- Technology independence

---

# 3. ROLE

The Executive Reporting Framework is responsible for:

- preparing executive governance reports
- communicating governance status
- summarizing organizational performance
- supporting strategic planning
- preserving executive reporting records

The framework is not responsible for:

- runtime monitoring
- dashboard visualization
- governance approval
- operational execution

---

# 4. EXECUTIVE REPORTING PHILOSOPHY

Executive Reporting represents the official organizational communication mechanism for governance.

Reports summarize governed organizational information to support informed executive decision making.

---

# 5. REPORTING SCOPE

Executive Reports consolidate governed information from:

- Governance Dashboard
- Governance Metrics Framework
- Quality Management Framework
- Risk Management Framework
- Audit Framework
- Regulatory Readiness Framework
- Continuous Improvement Framework

Direct runtime information shall not be reported without passing through governed frameworks.

---

# 6. STANDARD REPORT TYPES

Standard Executive Reports include:

- Governance Status Report
- Quality Report
- Risk Report
- Release Report
- Strategic Review Report
- Annual Governance Report

Additional report types may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 7. EXECUTIVE REPORT STRUCTURE

Every Executive Report shall contain at minimum:

- Executive Summary
- Current Governance Status
- Key Governance Metrics
- Key Organizational Risks
- Major Governance Decisions
- Recommended Actions

Additional sections may be included where appropriate.

---

# 8. REPORT EVIDENCE

Every conclusion presented within an Executive Report shall be traceable to governed organizational records.

Executive Reporting shall remain evidence-based.

---

# 9. REPORT GOVERNANCE WORKFLOW

Executive Reports follow the standardized lifecycle:

Prepare

↓

Review

↓

Approve

↓

Distribute

↓

Archive

All workflow stages shall remain traceable.

---

# 10. EXECUTIVE REPORT RECORD

Every Executive Report shall generate a standardized Executive Report Record.

Minimum metadata include:

- Report Identifier
- Report Type
- Reporting Period
- Approval Date
- Report Owner
- Version

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 11. REPORTING INDEPENDENCE

Executive Reporting remains independent from:

- Dashboard implementation
- Monitoring implementation
- Runtime execution

Executive Reporting consumes governed organizational information without modifying source governance records.

---

# 12. EXECUTIVE AUDIENCE

Executive Reports are intended for:

- Governance Authority
- Executive Governance Board
- Quality Authority
- Strategic Leadership

Reports are optimized for governance and executive oversight rather than technical implementation.

---

# 13. STRATEGIC COMMUNICATION

Executive Reporting supports:

- Strategic Planning
- Organizational Alignment
- Executive Decision Support
- Long-term Governance

Executive Reports communicate governance status but do not replace formal governance decisions.

---

# 14. ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

Executive Reporting follows:

- Governance-first communication
- Evidence-based reporting
- Executive decision support
- Organizational transparency
- Traceability
- Separation of concerns
- Technology independence
