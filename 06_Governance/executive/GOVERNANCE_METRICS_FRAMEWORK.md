# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the Governance Metrics Framework of the Safe Medical AI System.

The framework establishes standardized governance metrics, Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), measurement principles, governance rules, and organizational performance monitoring while preserving traceability, consistency, and technology independence.

---

# 2. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Governance Metrics follows the principles of:

- Governance-first measurement
- Objective evaluation
- Standardized KPIs
- Evidence-based performance measurement
- Organizational transparency
- Technology independence

---

# 3. ROLE

The Governance Metrics Framework is responsible for:

- governing organizational KPIs
- standardizing metric definitions
- governing KPI lifecycle
- supporting organizational measurement
- preserving metric traceability
- supporting executive decision making

The framework is not responsible for:

- runtime monitoring
- dashboard visualization
- evaluation execution
- governance approval decisions

---

# 4. GOVERNANCE METRICS PHILOSOPHY

Governance Metrics evaluate the effectiveness of the Governance System.

The objective is to measure organizational governance performance rather than individual AI model performance or infrastructure utilization.

---

# 5. GOVERNANCE METRICS SCOPE

Governance Metrics evaluate:

- Foundation
- Architecture
- Knowledge Governance
- Runtime Governance
- Operational Governance
- Organizational Governance

Additional governance domains may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 6. STANDARD METRICS DOMAINS

Standard governance measurement domains include:

- Quality Metrics
- Safety Metrics
- Governance Metrics
- Operational Metrics
- Risk Metrics
- Improvement Metrics

Each domain shall remain independently governable.

---

# 7. KPI STANDARDIZATION

Every governed KPI shall define:

- KPI Name
- Standard Definition
- Calculation Formula
- Data Source
- Update Frequency
- KPI Owner

No governed KPI shall exist without standardized documentation.

---

# 8. METRIC GOVERNANCE

Governance Metrics shall follow a governed lifecycle.

Metric creation, modification, and retirement require Governance Approval.

All metric changes shall remain fully traceable.

---

# 9. METRICS RECORD

Every KPI Snapshot shall generate a standardized Metrics Record.

Minimum metadata include:

- Metric Identifier
- KPI Version
- Measured Value
- Timestamp
- Data Source

Additional metadata may be introduced through governed amendments.

---

# 10. TARGET AND THRESHOLD GOVERNANCE

Every governed KPI shall define:

- Target
- Warning Threshold
- Critical Threshold

Threshold governance remains independent from dashboard presentation.

---

# 11. METRICS REVIEW

Governance Metrics shall undergo periodic review.

Minimum review activities include:

- KPI Relevance
- Trend Analysis
- Data Quality Assessment
- Threshold Suitability Review

Periodic review supports long-term governance stability.

---

# 12. METRICS INDEPENDENCE

The Governance Metrics Framework remains independent from:

- Governance Dashboard
- Monitoring Framework
- System Evaluation Framework

These frameworks consume governed metrics without owning metric definitions.

---

# 13. STRATEGIC DECISION SUPPORT

Governance Metrics support:

- Executive Decision Making
- Resource Allocation
- Risk Prioritization
- Quality Improvement
- Strategic Planning

Final governance decisions remain the responsibility of Governance Authority.

---

# 14. ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES

The Governance Metrics Framework follows:

- Governance-first measurement
- Standardized KPIs
- Organizational transparency
- Evidence-based governance
- Traceability
- Separation of concerns
- Technology independence
