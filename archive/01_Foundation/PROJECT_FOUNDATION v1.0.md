# 2. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Project Name

Safe Medical AI System for Oncology Patient Education

Mission

Develop an evidence-based, safe, explainable oncology patient assistant that bridges clinical guidelines and patient education through trustworthy AI.

Primary Goal

Design and implement a Safe Medical AI System for Oncology Patient Education suitable for research, portfolio development, and future clinical validation.

Project Nature

Research-oriented software project integrating:

- Medical AI
- Health Informatics
- Clinical Decision Governance
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Explainable AI
- Evidence-based Medicine

---

# 3. PROJECT VISION

The project aims to become a research-grade oncology patient education platform that:

- provides evidence-based educational support;
- improves patient understanding of cancer care;
- assists communication between patients and clinicians;
- prioritizes safety over completeness;
- demonstrates responsible medical AI design.

The system is designed to support healthcare professionals rather than replace them.

---

# 4. PROJECT PRINCIPLES

The project follows six core principles.

Evidence-based

Every response should be supported by trustworthy medical evidence whenever possible.

---

Patient Safety First

Patient safety has higher priority than answer completeness.

---

Structured Clinical Navigation

The system should guide users through structured clinical pathways before generating responses.

---

Explainable AI

Responses should be transparent, understandable, and supported by appropriate evidence.

---

Human-centered

The system supports clinicians and empowers patients rather than replacing professional medical care.

---

Research-grade

Every design decision should maximize:

- scientific value;
- reproducibility;
- portfolio quality;
- future publication potential.

---

# 5. CORE ARCHITECTURAL CONCEPTS

The project currently incorporates the following foundational concepts.

Clinical Navigation Engine

Provides structured navigation through clinical scenarios before knowledge retrieval.

---

Conversation Constraint Engine

Ensures the AI remains within approved scope and avoids unsafe medical behaviors.

---

Dual Governance

The project distinguishes between:

Project Governance

Responsible for development, documentation, and implementation.

Medical Governance

Responsible for safe clinical behavior and response generation.

---

Evidence Strength Dashboard

The concept has been approved.

The implementation strategy remains open for future discussion and evaluation.
