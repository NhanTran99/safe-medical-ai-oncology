# 1. PURPOSE

This document defines the long-term strategic development roadmap of the Safe Medical AI System.

The roadmap provides a governed execution plan for evolving the project from architectural foundation to a clinically deployable Medical AI platform while preserving governance integrity and technology independence.

---

# 2. ROADMAP PRINCIPLES

Development follows the principles of:

- Architecture before implementation
- Knowledge before intelligence
- Governance before automation
- Evidence before optimization
- Continuous organizational learning
- Amendment before rewrite
